# Intranets — the people

Who appears on the plant intranets. Years are in-world (Y120 to Y160).

Two lists:

- **From the forum cast** — people who already exist in the forums. Their forum
  details are repeated here so the two files can be reconciled later.
- **New people** — operators, foremen, clerks and supervisors written for the
  intranets. Several of them never use the forums at all.

A forum name is also the name used on plant paperwork and on the message
service. Where a new person has a real name as well, both are given.

## Where the plants are

The group owns nine plants. Six companies have no plant of their own: they
supply from head office and put contractors on site.

- **Plant 1** — Brannock & Daughters (Copper Works). Fences, presses, molders,
  belts, crates, poles, basic assembly. Site: selfservice.plant1.lan
- **Plant 2** — Ashgrove Ironstone (Iron & Steel). Drills, crushing, the yard.
- **Plant 3** — Hollowmere Mining Co. (Copper Works). The mine and its surface
  plant. Sites: portal.plant3.lan, shiftlog.plant3.lan
- **Plant 4** — Cinderhall Foundry (Iron & Steel). Blast furnaces, coke ovens,
  steel, and a great deal of waste.
- **Plant 5** — Wellbourne Waterworks (Steam & EC). Pumps, pipes, the mixer.
- **Plant 6** — Kettering & Vane (Steam & EC). Boilers, engines, the wire
  drawer, the winder.
- **Plant 7** — Underhill Excavation (Construction). Kilns, brick, glass,
  concrete, the yard. Site: msg.plant7.lan
- **Plant 8** — Pillar & Sons (Construction). Girders, the rolling mill, the
  welder, floors and beams.
- **Plant 9** — Swiftline Conveyors (Machinery). Fast belts, the basic
  assembler.

Head office only, with staff on site: Gearwright Machine Works, Ampleworth
Motor Works, Lattice & Finch, Sablon Glassworks, Halverson Smelting, Meridian
Instruments.

The message service on Plant 7 was bought by the group and carries for every
plant. That is why an operator at Plant 3 can write to one at Plant 8.
