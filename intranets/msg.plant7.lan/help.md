# How the service works

Open to everybody.

## What it is

One box each. You write to a person, not to a group, and the message sits in
their box until they open it. People usually read the box at the start of a
shift and at the end of one, and there is no hurry in it.

The service was bought by the group in Y143. Before that it was Plant 7's
own, and it ran on the Plant 7 office machine, and the accounts that
existed then were given a word instead of a number. There are twenty of
those and Verity Shaw has the list.

## The rules

- Write to a person. A message that names five people is read by none of
  them.
- Keep it short. The service will not take a message of more than about
  four hundred words and it will not tell you that it has not sent it.
- Do not put anything in a message that you would not want kept for thirty
  years. They are kept for thirty years.
- Nothing on the service is private from the office. The office does not
  read them. The office could.

## What is kept

- Messages are kept thirty years.
- Nothing has ever been deleted by anybody, including the office.
- An account that is closed is kept and can be opened again by the office.

## The groups

There are three group boxes that everybody on the service can read: the
plant list, the nights list, and the notice box. Anything else is between
two people.

## If a message goes missing

It has probably not gone missing. It has been read and not answered, which
is the same thing as far as the service is concerned, and it is the most
common fault there is.
