# The archive

Open to everybody. What is in the box, and how to read it.

The message service keeps everything. It has kept everything since the Plant 7
system was turned on in Y126 and it kept the twelve years before that on paper,
which are in the archive in two boxes and are not on here.

- Thirty-four years, Y126 to Y160.
- The internal mail of the other eight plants, loaded in at the Y143 group
  move. A thread from before Y143 between two people at another plant came
  in that way.
- Every account that has ever been issued, including the ones that have come
  off the books. An account that is off the books keeps its messages.
- Nothing has been deleted. The service has no delete. A man who wanted a
  message gone had to ask the office, and the office has no delete either.

## Reading it

The threads are kept by the year they started, in
[[msg.plant7.lan/dm/160/the-last-page|the way they were written]], and a thread
that runs across a year is kept in the year it started, which is why a
conversation from Y129 can have a message in it from Y131.

Most of it is nothing. Swaps, suppers, a man asking another man to hold a
door. The office has read all of it, twice, and has written nothing down about
what it read.

## The old system

Before the group move there were twenty words for twenty men and women on the
Plant 7 system, and the words were written on a card in the office. The card
is still there. Verity Shaw has it and she will read it out to anybody who
asks her, and she has been asked four times in eleven years.
