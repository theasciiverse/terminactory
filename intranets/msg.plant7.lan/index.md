## If you cannot get in

Your password is your staff number, the four digits, except for a few
accounts that were given a word on the old Plant 7 system before the group
move. Verity Shaw in the Plant 7 office has that list and she will read it
to you if you ask her.

The office is open 08:00 to 16:00 on days. At night the desk covers it and
the desk has the list too.
