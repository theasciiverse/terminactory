## What is on the list

- The pension, after thirty years on the books or at sixty-five, whichever
  comes first.
- Half an hour off the hour for a man who has been on the books thirty
  years, if he asks and his foreman agrees.
- Boots and gloves from stores, which is not a benefit but which people
  think is.
- The canteen, which is open for the whole shift, which is not a benefit
  either.

## Notes on the half hour

The half hour is not money. It is half an hour off the hour, taken when it
suits, and it has to be asked for, and the foreman has to agree, and no one
has ever been refused here and no one has ever asked why.

Old Bert asked on the Friday and his foreman agreed on the Friday and it was
in the book on the Monday. There is no form. There has never been a form.
Ask.
