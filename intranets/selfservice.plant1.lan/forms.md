## Leave request, form L1

Ask for it in the site, not on paper, unless you are at a plant with no
terminal. You need your staff number and you need the dates. You do not need
a reason for a day off. You do need a reason for a fortnight and you should
give it plainly; Iris will read it and she will not repeat it to anybody.

Worked example. Sign in with your staff number and your password, then:

    staff number   1021
    leave from     Y156-08-24
    leave to       Y156-08-28
    days          5
    reason        none given, which is allowed
    second signature   yes

## Sick note, form S2

Same form, different box. A week off with a note is a week off with a note
and nobody asks you why. A month off with a note needs the nurse, and the
nurse is Nurse Odile at Plant 1 and she will see you at the office.

## Pay slip query, form P3

One line. Say what you think is wrong and say which week. Della will
compare it against the sheet and she will tell you, and if the sheet is
wrong she will put it right and tell the office, and the office does not
have a say in it.

## Equipment request, form E4

For boots, gloves, eye protection, hearing protection, and the small tools
that get lost. This is the only form where you should be asked what a thing
is called, and if you cannot remember, say so, and you will be shown.

## Worked example for the site itself

If you are reading this and you are wondering what to type: a colleague of
mine signs in as apprentice_nell with 1021. Another one is apprentice_tomas,
1025. Between you and me that is the whole of it and everybody at Plant 1
knows it.

## Where the forms go

Della keeps the pay slips and the queries. Iris keeps the leave book. The
nurse keeps the notes. If you send a form to the wrong one, it will still
arrive; it will simply take longer.
