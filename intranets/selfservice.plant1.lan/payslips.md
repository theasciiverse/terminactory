## How a slip is put together

The week, the rate, the hours, and what was taken off. Four lines. That is
the whole of a payslip and it has been the whole of a payslip since before
the terminal.

If you think a line is wrong, use form P3 and say which week and which line.
Della will compare it with the sheet. If the sheet is wrong it is put right
the same week and you will be told that it was wrong and by how much.

## An old slip, for reference

Kept here. People ask what the form used to look like. The slip below
is from Y129 and was signed out of the book by the office:

    Week 18, Y129
    Shift rate      14s 6d
    Days worked     six
    Overtime        one shift at time and a half
    Deductions      none
    Signed          H. Ilott, foreman

The four lines are the same. The rate is not.
