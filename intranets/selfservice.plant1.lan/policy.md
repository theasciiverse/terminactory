# Leave and pay policy

Open to everybody. Read it once and you will not need to read it again.

## Leave

- Five days a year, taken when you like, told to Iris.
- Ten days a year if you have been on the books five years.
- Fifteen days a year if you have been on the books ten years, or if you
  are over sixty.
- Leave is in hours, not days, for the shifts that do not run six hours.
  If you work nights, one night counts as one and a half days.
- A day off is a day off. You do not have to say why. If you would rather
  not say why, leave the box empty.
- If you are ill, say so on the form and let the office know the same day.
  You are not asked for anything else.

## Pay

- Pay is weekly, on the Friday, in an envelope, from the office.
- The slip says the week, the rate, the hours, and what was taken off. If
  one of those four things is wrong, use form P3.
- Nobody is paid less for saying they are ill. There is no clause for it
  and there has never been one.

## Benefits

- The pension is with you after thirty years, or at sixty-five, whichever
  comes first.
- The benefit is half an hour an hour off for a man who has been on the
  books thirty years, if he asks for it and his foreman agrees.
- The canteen is not a benefit and is not counted as one. The canteen is
  open for the whole shift.

## If you are asked to sign for somebody else

Do not. That is the whole of the rule.
