# Passwords, and where they are

Every account in the three locked sites, with the password, and the page that
gives it away. Nothing here has to be guessed and nothing has to be broken.

## How it works

There are three rules, and they are all stated on open pages.

1. **The staff number is the password.** Four digits, the same number on the
   badge, the payroll sheet, the day sheet and the account. This is true of
   every account issued at the Y143 group move.
2. **Plant 3 went to badge numbers at the Y126 change.** The portal is
   Plant 3's own and everybody there uses their number. There is one
   exception and it is written down.
3. **Twenty accounts were given a word instead of a number.** These were
   the accounts that existed on the old Plant 7 system before the group
   bought it. The words have not been changed. The list is in a drawer in
   the Plant 7 office and Verity Shaw will read it out.

## Where a reader starts

Three open pages carry most of it:

- [[ops.dashboard.lan/roster|The day sheet]] — the staff numbers for every
  plant in the group, with the job and the plant. Open.
- [[portal.plant3.lan/staff-directory|Plant 3 staff directory]] — badge
  numbers for Plant 3, including the retired men. Open.
- [[selfservice.plant1.lan/staff-numbers|Plant 1 staff numbers]] — staff
  numbers for Plant 1. Open.

Two of the three also have a worked example on an open page:

- [[selfservice.plant1.lan/forms|Forms]] — a worked example that names
  apprentice_nell, 1021, and apprentice_tomas, 1025.
- [[shiftlog.plant3.lan/y159|Y159 of the shift log]] — the gate code in
  plain sight on the Y159-11-06 entry: the keypad at the yard entrance uses
  the same code as the gate. That code is west_gate_pat's password.

## The chain, in order

1. Read the day sheet. Every staff number in the group is on it.
2. Sign in to the message service as anybody whose number is on the sheet.
   Read the thread where somebody has forgotten their word.
3. Three of those words are the same three words on the self service site.
4. Sign in to the Plant 3 portal with a badge number and read the notice
   board, where a man has written down a gate code and told the whole plant
   not to be a nuisance about it.
5. The self service site is the easiest of the three. The forms page tells
   you two accounts outright.

## Difficulty

- **easy** — read straight off an open page.
- **medium** — one message account is needed first, and the word is in a
  thread between two people.
- **hard** — the thread that gives the word away is between two people who
  both have words, so the first one has to be got before the second.
