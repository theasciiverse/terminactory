# Plant 3 shift log — Y120

Y120-01-04 days — office — The book starts today. One line a shift. Keep it
short. Keep it true.
Y120-01-05 days — office — Face wet. Two hundred baskets by the shaft.
Y120-01-05 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-01-11 days — office — Coal up from the yard. Nine tons.
Y120-01-12 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-01-18 days — eldermill — The mill runs slow in the cold. We oiled it and
it ran slow anyway. It has always run slow in the cold.
Y120-01-19 days — the_first_drill — Twenty-one baskets. The drill is sound.
Y120-01-20 nights — Voss_shift — Two lamps out at the pit bottom. Replaced.
Y120-01-26 days — the_first_drill — Twenty baskets. I am told this is a good
day and I have written it down so that it is written down.
Y120-02-02 days — office — The cart track to the lower level is washed out.
Three days of the wheelbarrow.
Y120-02-03 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-02-09 days — OldHollowmere — Two hundred and forty baskets. My father
counted them before me and his father before him. The number is the same.
Y120-02-10 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-02-16 days — office — New crib timbers. Two by two, forty lengths.
Y120-02-17 nights — greasy_hands — The winch is dry. It is always dry. I
have oiled it twice this month and I will oil it again and it will be dry
again tomorrow.
Y120-02-23 days — the_first_drill — Nineteen baskets and a broken shovel.
Y120-02-24 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-03-02 days — office — The first coal on the new heap. It is not the
heap we had before. It is on higher ground and the carts do not like it.
Y120-03-03 days — Voss_shift — Heap is settling. Do not load the top until
it is cool.
Y120-03-09 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-03-10 days — greasy_hands — Fit the new wedge to the winch. It bites
now. It bit before.
Y120-03-16 days — office — Pay on Friday. The office is open from eight.
Y120-03-17 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-03-23 days — the_first_drill — Eighteen baskets, and the hole is good.
Y120-03-30 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-04-06 days — office — Two of the cribs have gone soft at the bottom end.
Replaced with oak.
Y120-04-07 days — OldHollowmere — Two hundred baskets. The hill remembers
what we forget.
Y120-04-13 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-04-14 days — office — The pump is drawing slow. Cleaned the strainer.
Y120-04-20 days — Voss_shift — Coal up. The carts are quicker on the new
ground, whatever the last three weeks have said.
Y120-04-21 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-04-27 days — greasy_hands — Winch greased. Cold weather coming.
Y120-05-04 days — office — Nothing to report.
Y120-05-05 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-05-11 days — the_first_drill — Twenty-two baskets. The best of the
month and I have put it down for the month.
Y120-05-18 days — office — The pit road is being rolled again. Keep to the
left.
Y120-05-19 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-05-25 days — office — Repairs to the office roof. It has been open to
the sky since winter.
Y120-06-01 days — office — New hut at the pit bottom. Six men to a hut.
Y120-06-02 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-06-08 days — greasy_hands — The winch is biting on the second setting.
It bit on the first and it bit on the second.
Y120-06-15 days — office — Drills away for a week. No fault. Nothing to say.
Y120-06-16 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-06-22 days — Voss_shift — Coal up, and a wagon of spare timber with it.
Y120-06-29 days — the_first_drill — Twenty baskets. Somebody has written on
the board that this plant does not work in summer. I have written this down
so that it is written down.
Y120-07-06 days — office — Nothing to report.
Y120-07-07 nights — office — Nothing.

Y120-07-21 nights — office — Nothing.

Y120-12-28 days — office — The year closes. Two thousand one hundred and
forty baskets in the year, give or take the wet weeks. There is a line of
men going back a hundred years with their figures written at the top of it.
Y120-12-29 nights — office — Nothing.
Y120-12-27 days — the_first_drill —  First year of the book and the book is
a ledger with the wrong lines in it and we are writing between the lines.
Sixteen baskets today and the men say that is a good day and I have no other
day to compare it with. The drill ran and the belt behind it ran and the
tunnel was cold. I have written the three of them in the wrong order and put
a line through the order and written it again. The man who kept the book
before me has left and has taken his way of doing it with him, which is the
whole trouble with a book.
