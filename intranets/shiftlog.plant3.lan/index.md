# Plant 3 shift log

The book. One entry per shift, in the hand of whoever was on, written up
before the shift ended.

This is the typed copy of the paper book that is kept in the records
cupboard. The paper is the record. Where the two differ, the paper is right.

- From Y141 the book was written up on the portal and copied here each
  morning.
- The years before Y141 were put in afterwards, up from the paper, between
  Y146 and Y148, by w_corrin and by whoever else sat down and read it out.
  Those pages are in pencil in the original and they are set in the same
  plain hand here.
- Weeks where the book was not written up are left as the paper has them,
  which is one line and the word carried.

## How to read a line

The date, then the shift, then the hand it was written in, then what was
written. Days is six until two. Nights is two until six. Where a line says
nothing else, nothing else happened.

## A note on the entries

The early entries are long: there was more to say. The later ones are
short: the plant knows what it is doing. That is the whole of it.
