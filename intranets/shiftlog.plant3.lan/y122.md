Y122-12-24 days — old_bert_hands —  Christmas Eve and half the plant gone
home and the kiln still up. Four of us on and we have done the day between
us and written it up between us in four hands. Somebody has brought a cake
in and it is on the desk and it is going hard. The gate was shut at two and
opened at four for the yard gang and shut again. I have written the times
because a gate that opens and shuts at Christmas is a thing a man in an
office will ask about in January.
