# Accounts plan

Who has an account on which site, and why they have one. Three of the five
sites have a login; two are open to anybody.

## The three locked sites

### portal.plant3.lan — Plant 3 Operator Portal

Twenty-three accounts. Only Plant 3 staff. The portal is Plant 3's own
and it carries the shift assignments and the notices for that plant.

The password is the badge number, four digits, in every case but one. Plant 3
went over to badge numbers at the Y126 change, when the terminal arrived in
the office, and every account was issued against the badge.

Retired staff keep their accounts (brass_tally, OldHollowmere,
the_first_drill, eldermill, percy_i). The portal carries the
announcement archive and the notices they wrote are still cited.

The exception is percy_i, who never changed the word he was given at issue
in Y124. It is written down in the staff directory, which is an open page,
Hollis Kerr would rather have it written down than have the man
keep being asked.

### selfservice.plant1.lan — Plant 1 Staff Self Service

Twenty-six accounts. Every member of Plant 1 payroll. The site is run from
Plant 1 and does the paperwork for the whole group, but the accounts belong
to Plant 1.

The password is the staff number, four digits, in every case but three. The
three who were given a word instead — canteen_agnes, NurseOdile and
sign_writer — were given it at the same time as their message service
accounts, and they are the same word on both.

The forms page is an open page and it carries a worked example, which is
where two of the accounts can be read off directly.

### msg.plant7.lan — Plant 7 Internal Messages

A hundred and one accounts, across all nine plants. Plant 7 bought the service and
hosts it, and the group bought it from Plant 7 at the Y143 move, so the
accounts cover everybody.

Twenty of the accounts were carried over from the old Plant 7 system and
kept the word they were given. Eight more were given a word later, out of
a second drawer. The other seventy-three were issued at the group move or
after it and use the staff number.

The list of the twenty words is in a drawer in the Plant 7 office, and
Verity Shaw (v_shaw) will read it out to anybody who asks. She has never
put it on the portal and she is not going to.

Every account with a word has it spoken aloud somewhere on the service, by
the person who owns it, to the person who forgot it. Those exchanges are
the clue trail. The full list is in the clues file, intranets/_clues.md.

## The two open sites

### shiftlog.plant3.lan — Plant 3 Shift Log

Open. It is the typed copy of a book that used to live in a cupboard and
everybody on the plant read it. A logbook that needs a password is not a
logbook.

This is deliberate: the shift log is where the reader finds the first
password (west_gate_pat's gate code, in the Y159 entry) and the belt that
stops every week for thirty-five years and is not in the book after Y152.

### ops.dashboard.lan — Operations Dashboard

Open. Gauges, plant status, the day sheet, tickets, alerts. The day sheet
carries the staff numbers for every plant, which is the single source for
the other two sites' passwords.

The day sheet being open is what the group decided in Y143 and nobody has
gone back on it. A man who clocks in at a gate has his number read out loud
every morning anyway.
