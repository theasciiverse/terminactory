# IT support

Open to everybody. The systems in the group, who runs them, and what to do
when they are down.

## What there is

- [[portal.plant3.lan/|Plant 3 operator portal]] — Plant 3. Run by Hollis
  Kerr at Plant 3, desk 2.
- [[shiftlog.plant3.lan/|Plant 3 shift log]] — public. The typed copy of the
  paper book.
- [[selfservice.plant1.lan/|Plant 1 self service]] — leave, slips, benefits,
  forms. Run by Iris Mappin and Della Rooke at Plant 1.
- [[msg.plant7.lan/|Plant 7 message service]] — the messages. Run by Verity
  Shaw at Plant 7, bought by the group at the Y143 move, carries for every
  plant.
- This dashboard — public. Run by the operations office.

## Passwords

Every account in the group is the four digits of your staff number. That is
the portal, that is the self service and that is the message service.

Twenty accounts are the exception. They are the ones issued on the old
Plant 7 system before the group move, and they were given a word. The list
is in the office drawer at Plant 7 and there are twenty names on it. Verity
Shaw will read it to you if you ask. If you cannot find Verity, ask the man
who is on the desk, and if you cannot find the man on the desk, ask whoever
is on the desk.

A man who forgets his word can have it changed to his staff number at the
desk at Plant 7 and the old word is struck off the list the same day.

Retired staff keep their accounts and their numbers. Their messages are kept
on file and are not read by anybody but themselves and the office.

## When something is down

- The portal and the shift log are on the same cable. If one is up and the
  other is not, it is the console at Plant 3 and not the cable.
- If the shift log is up and the portal is not, Hollis will have it back
  inside the hour. He has never been wrong about the cable.
- If the message service is down, use the telephone. The office at Plant 7
  has the numbers of every foreman in the group on a sheet behind the
  drawer.
- If the shift log and the portal are both down, the paper book comes out
  of the records cupboard at Plant 3 and somebody writes it up by hand and
  it is copied in afterwards. This happened for nine days in Y141 and the
  paper was the better record, which everybody said at the time.

## Known faults

- The message service is slow between ten and eleven. This is the plant
  waking up and it has been like that since Y143.
- The message service will not take a message over about four hundred words.
  It will not say so. It will simply not send it, and you will think you
  sent it.
- Messages are kept for thirty years. The oldest are from Y126. Nobody has ever asked for one older
  than that, and the office has never refused one.
- The dashboard does not show plants 1 to 9 individually on the front page
  at this width. The plant pages are there.
