# Power

Open to everybody. EC and steam, by plant, and what it costs to run the
group.

EC is charged to the plant that draws it. Belts draw a little all the time
and a great deal when they are full, and a belt with no power shows red.

## EC

    Plant 1   14%   press house and the pole
    Plant 2   19%   the yard
    Plant 3   23%   belts, the pole, the arm
    Plant 4   71%   the blast furnaces
    Plant 5    9%   pumps
    Plant 6   11%   boiler house
    Plant 7   16%   kilns, the yard
    Plant 8   12%   the welder
    Plant 9    6%   belt line

Plant 4 draws more than the rest of the group together. Plant 4 knows this
and does not comment on it.

## Steam

Plant 6 runs the boilers and sells steam to everybody else. Plant 5 has its
own small boiler for the pumps and does not take steam from anybody.

- Pressure on the dayshift runs about eighteen per cent over the mark and
  is cut back at nine.
- The mark is cut back with a spanner, by hand, by the man on the boiler.
  This is how it has been done since before the wire drawer.
- Any plant wanting steam for a furnace asks at the desk at Plant 6 on the
  Friday for the following week.

## Advice that is often given

Size for the busy minute, not for the average. The busy minute is four
o'clock on a Tuesday when the shops have changed over and everybody wants
the same part.
