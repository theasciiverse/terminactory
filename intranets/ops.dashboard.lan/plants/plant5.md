## Notes

- Put a pump down anywhere. Water goes wherever the pipes go, so the pipe
  is the first thing and the pump is the second thing.
- A pipe run holds one fluid at a time. Water and molten metal do not
  share a run, and a run that has carried one carries a mark of it.
- The mixer takes water from a pipe and sand and stone from the belts and
  makes concrete, and it will not take soft water, and the water here is
  soft.
- Pump three was out from Y158-06-04 to Y158-06-11 and the plant ran on
  two.
