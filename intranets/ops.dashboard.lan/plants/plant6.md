## Notes

- The boiler house runs the steam for the whole group and the mark is cut
  back at nine by hand.
- The wire drawer pulls plate into wire and wire into fine wire, and it is
  quicker than a press at the same job.
- The winder winds wire into coils and rods into springs. A coil from the
  winder is three wire; a coil from the winder on fine wire is six fine
  wire, and the second one needs a drawer to have been used.
- Steam forgives nothing and the man on the boiler says so every week and
  is right every week.
