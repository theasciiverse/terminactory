## Notes

- Plant 4 draws more electricity than the rest of the group together.
- A blast furnace makes molten iron in two and a half seconds out of one
  lump of ore, with coke as the fuel, and it can make molten steel out of
  two iron plate and a coke, and every one of those leaves waste out the
  back.
- The coke oven bakes two coal into one coke, and it leaves a crate of
  waste behind for every eight it fires, and the tip is not far.
- Two plate, one coke. That is the whole of the steel and it is on
  everybody's shirt at some point.
- Waste is the line item nobody likes and everybody carries.
