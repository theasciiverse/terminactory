# Waste

Open to everybody. Where it is, and where it is going.

Waste is what is left out of the back of a machine that makes something.
Blast furnaces, coke ovens and kilns all make it. It always leaves out the
back and never the front. It costs money to get rid of and it earns nothing.
A crate of it costs fifty cents to send away and nothing at all to be rid
of.

Nothing burns it. Boilers, furnaces and kilns will all refuse it. A trade
post set to sell for money will take it. A trade post set for research will
not, and neither will one set to split, and that is on purpose.

## Where it is

- Plant 4, Cinderhall Foundry — 340 crates, 41 of them at the gate, waiting
  for a cart. This is the worst place in the group for waste and it has
  been the worst place since the coke ovens came in.
- Plant 7, Underhill Excavation — 62 crates behind the kilns. The kilns
  make one crate of waste for every four they fire, which is the number
  everyone argues about and nobody gets wrong.
- Plant 3, Hollowmere Mining Co. — a heap, not a crate. Spoil and coke
  oven waste on the same heap, counted separately at the gate, both of them
  costing money to move.
- Plant 1 and Plant 8 — a cart each, weekly.

## Where it goes

Out, by cart, to the tip beyond Plant 4. It is cheap to move and it is
paid for by whoever made it. A crate of waste at Plant 4 costs fifty cents
to get rid of and there are nine hundred crates a week at that plant.
