# Known issues

Open to everybody. The ones that are not fixed and are not going to be
without somebody deciding otherwise.

- The north belt on Line 2 at Plant 3 stops. It has stopped since Y124 and
  it stops in the same place and the machine knows the place and the shift
  book knows the place and nobody has been to the place. It is the oldest
  open thing in the group.
- The lift at Plant 1 waits for a full car. It has waited since the day it
  was fitted and the fitting man's son fitted the second one the same way.
- The weighbridge at Plant 9 is out by nine kilos. It was out by nine kilos
  last time and it is out by nine kilos now.
- Plant 4 has more waste behind the blast furnaces than it has room for.
  Waste buys for less than nothing and costs money to move, and everybody
  knows both of those numbers.
- The kiln at Plant 7 is eight runs at a time and the firing is twenty
  seconds a batch, and the number twenty seconds is on a sign on the wall
  that has been repainted nine times.
- The steam at Plant 6 runs over the mark on the dayshift and the mark is
  cut back at nine by a man with a spanner, which is the oldest trick in
  the book.
- Plant 5's water is soft and the mixer will not take soft water and the
  mixer is not wrong.

## Things that are not issues

- The message service is slow between ten and eleven. It is not broken.
  It is forty people finding out that the canteen is open.
- The waste heap at Plant 3 has two kinds of thing on it. That is what a
  heap is.
