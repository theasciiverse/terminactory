# Forgot your password

Open to everybody. Do this before you shout.

**If you joined on or after Y133.** Your portal password is your badge
number: the four digits in the staff directory, no prefix, no letters. So
west_gate_pat is 3002 and that is the whole of it. The message service at
Plant 7 works the same way for accounts issued at the Y143 group move.

**If your message service account is older than the group move.** The old
Plant 7 system gave out a word, not a number. Those accounts were carried
over as they were. Verity Shaw at Plant 7 has the list and will read it to
you over the desk if you ask her nicely, and she will read it to you if you
do not.

**If you are locked out on nights.** The desk is shut. The gate can open the
systems office from 22:00, and west_gate_pat or the man on the desk can do
it. Hollis is on the door list from Y157 and can let himself in from any
plant.

**If the portal is down.** It is the same cable as the shift log. If the
shift log is up and the portal is not, it is the console and not the cable.
If both are down it is the building, and the paper book comes out of the
records cupboard and somebody writes it up by hand.
