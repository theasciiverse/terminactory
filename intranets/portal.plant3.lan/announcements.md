belt was looked at twice at the third roller. Nothing was found and nothing
was changed. The line is to be left as it is.

## Y159-11-02 — Portal tidy

The notice board is now one page. Please do not add to the bottom of a
notice; add your own. The system will not show more than nine notices and

    hollis_k

## Y158-09-14 — The canteen rota is on the board

Mirren has put the rota up. It is the same rota as always with the swaps

    mirren_v

## Y157-06-21 — Free ration

The company is giving a free ration on the last Friday of the month for as
long as the coke ovens are running three shifts. The men on the ovens decide
whether this is a good idea or not, and they have decided.

    Voss_shift

## Y156-02-03 — A word about the notebook

The foreman's notebook is kept in the desk and the entries go in the same
week they happen. If you did something on Monday, it is written down on
Monday. I know this is not a new instruction. I have written it down
anyway.

    ForemanEllis

## Y154-10-17 — Fire drill Thursday

There is a fire drill on Thursday at 10:00. Everyone goes out, everyone
comes back in, and we do it again. Nobody is in trouble. The whole thing
takes four minutes and I would like it to take four minutes.

    apryce

## Y152-06-30 — Last day

My last day is Friday. brass_tally, Line 2. I have been on this plant
since I was seventeen and I would like to say that I never once minded the
belt. People will keep asking me how to do this and I will keep telling
them, and it comes out right in the end.

    brass_tally

## Y151-03-12 — The lift

The lift waits for a full car. I know. We have all said it. I am saying it
again. We keep putting three crates in it and then standing there.

    e_stroud

## Y148-06-05 — New stores assistant

Lorn Ashby starts in the stores on Monday. He knows the difference between a
valve and a gauge already, which is more than I can say for the last two
men who sat where he is sitting. Lorn, the answer to any question about the
shelves is Bex.

    browncoat_bex

## Y147-05-09 — Waste thread, ten years on

The old thread about waste is still up. It is still the best thing anybody
has written about where the waste goes and it was written by a man who is
not here any more. If you are new, read it before you ask. If you have read
it, you know more than you think.

    ForemanEllis

## Y144-01-19 — Nights

TamsinR takes the nights from the first of February. If you want something
from the night shift, ask before 18:00 and not at 02:00.

    ForemanEllis

## Y141-09-01 — The shift book goes online

From today the shift book is written up on the portal at the end of each
shift. The paper book stays. The paper book is the record; the portal is
the copy. If the two disagree, the paper book is right, and somebody has
forgotten to write it up.

    ForemanEllis
