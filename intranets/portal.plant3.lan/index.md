The portal is for people who work at Plant 3. It is not for the plant's
opinions.

- [[portal.plant3.lan/staff-directory|Staff directory]] — everybody on the
  books, with badge number and shift. This page is open; anybody may read it.
- [[portal.plant3.lan/quick-links|Quick links]] — the other sites, the shift
  log, the group pages.
- [[portal.plant3.lan/forgot-password|Forgot your password]] — how to get
  back in, and who to ask.
- [[portal.plant3.lan/retirements|Leavers]] — the people who have come off
  the books, with the dates the books were changed.

Accounts are issued by the systems office, Plant 3, desk 2, first floor by the
stair. The desk is open 06:00 to 14:00 on days. On nights, ask the gate.

Messages on the board are read on the first shift and not again. If a notice
matters to you, it is also on the board and it will be there when you come
back.
