## Shift book

One entry per shift, in the hand of the person who was on. It is written up
before the shift ends. It is not written up from memory afterwards. If the
shift was quiet, the entry says the shift was quiet. The book is the record.

The paper book is kept in the records cupboard and is not taken off the
site. The copy on [[shiftlog.plant3.lan/|the shift log]] is a copy. Where
the two differ, the paper is right.

## Faults

A fault is written down when it happens, in the first person who found it,
and the time is given. A fault that is carried over from the shift before is
written down again with the time it was carried over at. It is not written
as new, and it is not written as nothing.

## Numbers

Badge numbers are four digits and are never changed. A man who retires keeps
his number. This is not sentiment, it is that the numbers are in the
payroll transfer and in the message service accounts, and a changed number
is a changed account.

## The canteen

The canteen is open for the whole shift. The canteen does not close for
head office.

## Visitors

Nobody goes below the second landing without a hat. There is no exception to
this and there has never been an exception to this, and if you have never
been below the second landing then you have not had to wear one.

## Leave

Leave is asked for on the self service site and not on this one. The
self service site is run from Plant 1 and it is the same for every plant in
the group.
