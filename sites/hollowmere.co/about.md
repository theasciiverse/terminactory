# Hollowmere Mining Co.: About

Josiah Hollowmere started the firm with one drill, because he could not afford more, and one honest opinion of it, because he could not afford to lie about it either. His son kept the drill and the habit. His daughter Ellen kept the ledgers so plain that the bank once asked to see them again. Her son Edwin runs the yard now and still does the sums by hand, in pencil, on the back of the previous week.

Four generations, and the rule has not changed: say what the drill does, and no more. Drills do not complain, and neither do we. If one does go wrong, bring it to the yard. Someone will look at it, and you will get a straight answer at the end, even if the answer is that you bought the wrong one.

We have been good neighbours to [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] since before the first belt was laid in the valley. The Brannock sisters still send a card every spring, and we still send back a drill part, which they say is the only correspondence they trust.

There is a bench at the top of the hill, facing the shaft. Sit on it if you like. It was put there for anyone who has come up to ask a question.
