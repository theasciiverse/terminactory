# Hollowmere Mining Co.

Four generations of drilling in these hills, and not one of us has ever sold a drill that does more than it says. Our cheap drill is slow. We tell you that before you pay for it, not after, and that is the whole of our selling technique.

It burns coal, it needs open ground, and it does not complain. For anything faster, our neighbours are glad to help. [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] sell the furnaces, presses and belts that carry the ore on, and [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/|Ashgrove Ironstone]] make the basic drill, which we think is a fine drill and say so. When you are ready for powered drills, [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth Motor Works]] are the ones to see.
