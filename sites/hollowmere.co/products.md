# Hollowmere Mining Co.: Products

Our one machine, and we are proud of it. It digs ore out of the ground and pushes it out of its front, so give it a belt or a furnace straight away. If the ore has nowhere to go, the drill fills up and waits, and so does everyone behind it.

It needs open ground, so it cannot go on an upper floor. Underground it is fine, and the ore down there is richer. It burns coal to keep itself going. Set it to coal and it will feed itself from what it digs, which is handy at a coal patch.

It is the slowest of the drills, and we will not pretend otherwise. It cuts iron more slowly than a basic drill, but it is plenty to start with.

If you want faster, [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/products|Ashgrove's basic drill]] cuts iron much faster than ours. For belts to carry what you dig, see [[brannock.co/products|Brannock & Daughters]]. Drills that run on power are at [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth Motor Works]].
