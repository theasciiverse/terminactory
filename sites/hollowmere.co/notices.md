# Hollowmere Mining Co.: Notices

**Notice board, Hollowmere yard**

- The cheap drills are serviced on the first Monday of the month, as they have been since Josiah. Report any noise to the yard. Do not investigate it yourself.
- Coal for the yard is now in the shed by the gate. Take what you need, not what you think you might need later.
- The lamp at the top of the shaft has been lit again. Nobody on the rota lit it. It is not a problem, and we are not looking into it.
- Brannock & Daughters have asked us to say that their belts are not slower than Swiftline's, only more careful. We have agreed to say so, and have.
- Ashgrove Ironstone have sent a very fine letter about our drills. It is on the wall by the kettle.
