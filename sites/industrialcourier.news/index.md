# Industrial Courier — Y158-10, issue 87

The trade paper for plant managers, shop floor and yard. Industry news, editorials, interviews with plant managers, product reviews and letters. Read the archive at [[industrialcourier.news/issues|Issues]].

**News: Halverson arc furnace bids for silicon sand**
Halverson Smelting has placed a standing order for sand from the Underhill dig yards, to feed the arc furnace at the wafer yard. The contract runs to Y160 and is worth an estimated three thousand crowns a season. Full story, page two.

**Editorial: the second key**
Helen Marsh writes on companies that open late and the letters they send when they do. Meridian's letter arrived with snacks, she says, and no one knows what to make of it. See [[industrialcourier.news/editorials/editorial-y158-10|The second key]].

**Interview: Lin Voss, Hollowmere**
Plant Manager Lin Voss on the copper line, four generations of drills, and why cheap and honest still win orders. See [[industrialcourier.news/interviews/interview-y156-03|Lin Voss]].

**Review: the new Gearwright lathe**
Tad Frall turns plate into rods and rods into screws on the trial machine. Verdict: steady, slow on screws, and the chuck wobbles. See [[industrialcourier.news/reviews/review-y157-04|Gearwright lathe]].

**Letters**
Derek Morn prints every letter, even the angry ones. This month: on gearboxes, on the cost of coke, and on the letter Meridian sent. See [[industrialcourier.news/letters|Letters]].
