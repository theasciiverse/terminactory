# Letters

Derek Morn prints every letter, even the angry ones. The writers below are real readers; Kit Marsh writes under a false name and is not related to Helen Marsh, whatever he says.

**Y158-10** — On gearboxes, on the cost of coke, and on the letter Meridian sent with snacks. One reader asks what the snacks were. Another says do not eat them. Ben Marsh writes about screws. Kit Marsh writes about screws. They are not related, they say.

**Y158-01** — On the waste queues at Cinderhall and the trade post fee. One hauler says the back gate is full before dawn.

**Y157-07** — On silicon sand and the arc furnace. One reader has stood by a crystal grower at shift change and says twenty seconds is a long time to watch a crystal grow.

**Y157-04** — On the Gearwright lathe and its wobbly chuck. Tad Frall replied in the next issue: he would not buy one for screws.

**Y156-12** — On Sablon tubes and the evacuator. One reader says the quiet was the first thing, and the second.

**Y156-03** — On Hollowmere drills and four generations. One reader's great-grandfather also dug the first hole, they say, with a cheap drill.

**Y155-08** — On what leaves out the back of the furnace. Several readers suggest the old settlement lagoon. Harold Pimm of the council says no.

**Y153-11** — On precision assemblers and boards wired four at a time.

**Y152-02** — On the thousandth gearbox. One reader says it went straight to one thousand and one.

**Y150-10** — On Wellbourne pipes and water going wherever the pipes do. One reader asks where the pipes go when it rains.

Write to Derek Morn at the Courier office. Letters are printed as received.
