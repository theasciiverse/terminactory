# Trade fair report — Y156-01

The regional trade fair ran at the mill yard for three days, from Y156-01-10 to Y156-01-12. Eight companies had stalls: Brannock & Daughters, Ashgrove Ironstone, Cinderhall Foundry, Wellbourne Waterworks, Kettering & Vane, Pillar & Sons, Gearwright Machine Works, Swiftline Conveyors. Sablon, Halverson, Lattice & Finch, Ampleworth and Meridian did not attend.

**Brannock** showed a furnace and a molder, and said if it works they will sell it, and if it does not, one of them will fix it.

**Ashgrove** showed the basic drill, which cuts iron twice as fast as a cheap one, and the crusher, which grinds ore to grit.

**Cinderhall** showed the blast furnace and coke oven, and a barrel of coke that drew a queue.

**Wellbourne** showed pipes and a pump, and said water goes wherever the pipes do.

**Kettering & Vane** showed boilers and steam engines, and said they out-charge a field of panels.

**Pillar & Sons** showed the rolling mill and the welder, and a steel girder that Caleb Pillar said holds enough.

**Gearwright** showed the crude assembler, running valves and gauges at ten seconds each, and a lathe whose chuck wobbled on the third tightening.

**Swiftline** showed fast belts, and said they move twice what Brannock's do.

Meridian did not attend. Halverson brought a wafer and would not let anyone touch it. Lattice & Finch sent a letter, read aloud by a clerk, saying Mr Lattice had gone quiet and Ms Finch was taking notes.

The cheap drill drew the biggest crowd. It works anywhere on the ground and does not complain.
