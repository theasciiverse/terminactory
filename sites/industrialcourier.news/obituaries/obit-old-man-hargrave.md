# Obituary — Old Man Hargrave (Y132)

Old Man Hargrave, who read the Gazette at the library every Tuesday, has died. He was a pattern maker at the Cinderhall Foundry for thirty-one years before he was a reader, and the wooden patterns for the old gear casings were his. Two of them are still on the shelf above the foundry office door.

He held that the crossword was too easy and that a casting should be left alone until it was cold. He was right about the casting.

The Gazette has kept his Tuesday seat. The Courier notes that the patterns are in good order and have not warped.
