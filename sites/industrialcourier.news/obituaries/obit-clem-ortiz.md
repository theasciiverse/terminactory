# Obituary — Clem Ortiz (Y151)

Clem Ortiz, crane hand, idle since the closures, has died. He read the Gazette every morning at the library, in Old Man Hargrave's seat. He said the crane yard was quiet and the cranes did not lift anything anymore. The crane yard is still quiet. M. Hale says he saw Clem at the gate once, looking at the sky, and that was the last time.

The cranes did not lift anything. That was the point.
