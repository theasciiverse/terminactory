# Obituary — Simon Kyrk (Y142)

Simon Kyrk, night janitor at the Lattice & Finch yard, has died. He kept the spare keys in his pocket for seventeen years and never wrote down where they are. Gregg Toll has looked in every coat pocket Simon left behind, and the keys are not among them. The yard is locked on weekends now, and the keys are still missing.

M. Hale took the round after him. He says the yard is empty at night, the same as it was when Simon walked it.
