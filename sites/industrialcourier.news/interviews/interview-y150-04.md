# Interview — Toll Swiftline, Swiftline Conveyors (Y150-04)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell fast belts, the basic assembler and the upgrade planner.

**Toll Swiftline:** Fast belts move twice what Brannock's do. The basic assembler runs crude recipes twice as fast. The upgrade planner swaps every belt, drill, panel and assembler in an area for its next version, and you pay only the difference.

**Marsh:** Your fast belts are six weeks late.

**Swiftline:** They will arrive. When they do, the yards will load twice as much. The Gearwright crew is testing them on long arm cells now.

**Marsh:** What do you say to Brannock about the comparison?

**Swiftline:** Tell the sisters we said hello. Their belts are good belts. Ours move twice as much. Both are true.
