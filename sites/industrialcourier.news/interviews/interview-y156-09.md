# Interview — Tad Frall, Ashgrove (Y156-09)

**Helen Marsh:** The crusher grinds ore to grit. Why does that matter?

**Tad Frall:** Grit melts further than whole rock. You get a third more metal from every lump. That is the difference between a furnace that stops and one that runs the shift.

**Marsh:** And the basic drill?

**Frall:** Cuts iron twice as fast as a cheap one. The yards feel it in the first week. We sold two to our own shop last season and put them straight on the face.

**Marsh:** What do you drill for, these days?

**Frall:** Whatever is under the next floor. The ore is richer below ground.
