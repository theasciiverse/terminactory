# Interview — Mira Wellbourne, Wellbourne Waterworks (Y154-09)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell pumps, pipes, the mixer and the advanced pump.

**Mira Wellbourne:** A good pipe is a promise. Set a pump down anywhere and the water goes wherever your pipes do. We laid pipe to the new pump house on Mill Lane last season. The advanced pump gives four times the water, and it is in a 2x2 frame.

**Marsh:** Does the advanced pump draw EC all the time?

**Wellbourne:** Only while it works. That is the point. When it is not pumping, it is not charging. The old cheap panels run all day whether anyone needs power or not.

**Marsh:** What does the mixer do?

**Wellbourne:** It takes sand, stone and water from a pipe, and makes concrete. Or coal, sand and water, and makes etchant. The mixer is where the pipe meets the belt.
