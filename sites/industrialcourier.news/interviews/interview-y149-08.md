# Interview — Roy Ampleworth, Ampleworth Motor Works (Y149-08)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell the advanced drill and the large basic assembler and drill.

**Roy Ampleworth:** Things that turn on their own. The advanced drill runs on EC and digs twice as fast as a basic drill. The large basic assembler builds pump units and engine blocks. The large basic drill burns ten times the coal and mines five times a basic drill.

**Marsh:** Ten times the coal?

**Ampleworth:** Yes. It digs fast, and it eats. You decide which you can afford.

**Marsh:** The steam tractor ran its trial on the mill road.

**Ampleworth:** It started on the third pull. Engine block, frame, hardened gears, control panel and generator. It is a big machine. It needs a big road.
