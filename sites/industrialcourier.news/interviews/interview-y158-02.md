# Interview — Ellen Dorn, Brannock & Daughters (Y158-02)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell furnaces, molders, belts, panels, poles. Which is busiest?

**Ellen Dorn:** Furnaces, this season. Cinderhall wants another blast furnace, and Ashgrove wants the crusher. Molders come after, once the molten metal is flowing. Belts and panels are steady all year; every yard needs a pole.

**Marsh:** Your letter says if it works, the three of you will sell it, and if it doesn't, one of you will fix it.

**Dorn:** That is the letter. It is also true. Brannock fixes what Brannock sells, and if we cannot fix it, Hollowmere or Ashgrove usually can. We are not proud.

**Marsh:** What do you make of Meridian opening with snacks?

**Dorn:** I have not had the snacks. I would like to try them. If they fix a blast furnace, I will buy a crate.
