# Interview — Lin Voss, Hollowmere (Y156-03)

**Helen Marsh:** You have been on the copper line how long?

**Lin Voss:** Four generations, they say. My great-grandfather dug the first hole with a cheap drill. We still sell cheap drills. They work. They do not complain. Neither should you — that is the letter, not me.

**Marsh:** What do you make of the newer companies?

**Voss:** Halverson melts sand on power alone. Meridian makes chips and brings snacks. I dig copper ore and sell wire. There is room for all of it, so long as the drills turn.

**Marsh:** And the key?

**Voss:** Hollowmere has no key. It was open from the start. I tell the new yards: make your first copper wire, and Ashgrove will answer the door.
