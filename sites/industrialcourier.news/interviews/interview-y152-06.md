# Interview — Dale Gearwright, Gearwright Machine Works (Y152-06)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell the crude assembler and the lathe.

**Dale Gearwright:** The crude assembler is four by four, patient, ten seconds a part. It builds valves, gauges, lamps, relays and gearboxes. Our lathe turns plate into rods and rods into screws, and steel into steel rods and bearings.

**Marsh:** The thousandth gearbox came off your line this season.

**Gearwright:** It did. And the one thousand and first followed it, same as the rest. No one turned the machine off.

**Marsh:** The lathe chuck wobbles, Tad Frall says.

**Gearwright:** Tad Frall is a crusher man, not a lathe man. The chuck holds. It holds better on the third tightening than the first.
