# Interview — Hogg Cinderhall, Cinderhall Foundry (Y157-11)

**Helen Marsh:** Your coke oven contract was renewed for another season.

**Hogg Cinderhall:** It was. Coal in, coke out, no fire of our own. The oven does the work. We haul the waste out the back.

**Marsh:** The haulers say the back gate queues are longer.

**Cinderhall:** More coke, more waste. Nothing burns waste, and the boilers refuse it. We contract a hauler, and he contracts a second hauler when the queue gets long. It is a chain, and the chain holds.

**Marsh:** What does the blast furnace make these days?

**Cinderhall:** Molten iron, molten steel, hardened plate. And waste. Always waste.
