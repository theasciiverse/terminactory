# Interview — Saul Rens, Sablon Glassworks (Y151-11)

**Helen Marsh:** You have blown glass for a hundred years.

**Saul Rens:** And never once hurried it. Nothing is as patient as a tube.

**Marsh:** The evacuator pulls air out with a water jet.

**Rens:** Two tubes a run, no waste, no fire. The glass goes in, the water pulls, the tube comes out. You cannot rush the cooling. If you rush it, you have waste, and waste costs money to get rid of.

**Marsh:** Are you busy?

**Rens:** Tube orders are full to Y160. We blow them as fast as patience allows.
