# Interview — Silas Halverson, Halverson Smelting (Y148-12)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell the arc furnace, the crystal grower and the wafer saw.

**Silas Halverson:** Silicon. A lovely byproduct of sand. The arc furnace melts sand on power alone, no fuel, and makes fewer opportunities than the blast furnace. The crystal grower pulls a crystal out of molten ingots, slowly: twenty seconds each. The wafer saw cuts a crystal into six wafers with a fine wire, no scrap.

**Marsh:** Opportunities?

**Halverson:** That is what the letter calls them. Opportunities for the scrap. We find the scrap charming. Do keep the waste coming.

**Marsh:** And your patience?

**Halverson:** Our crystal grower and our wafer saw are slow, and so is our patience. We are not in a hurry. Silicon comes when it comes.
