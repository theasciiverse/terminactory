# Interview — Mina Meridian, Meridian Instruments (Y148-07)

**Helen Marsh:** Your letter came with snacks.

**Mina Meridian:** We are all friends here. Meridian has been waiting for you.

**Marsh:** What are the snacks?

**Meridian:** Snacks.

**Marsh:** You sell the fine etcher and the large precision assembler.

**Meridian:** We sell the fine etcher, which etches a fine die and a spare die from each wafer with a fine mask: no scrap, but very hungry. And the large precision assembler, which builds the computer and the large advanced goods twice as fast.

**Marsh:** Why did you wait for the microprocessor (x4x1) to open?

**Meridian:** Because that is when we were ready. We brought snacks.

**Marsh:** Has anyone asked what kind of snacks?

**Meridian:** You are the first. Thank you for asking.
