# Interview — Rosa Kett, Kettering & Vane (Y154-06)

**Helen Marsh:** Mr Kettering says the coil is crude. Mr Vane says it is charming. Who is right?

**Rosa Kett:** Both, depending on the day. We sell boilers and steam engines that out-charge a field of panels. Our drawer pulls wire finer than any press, and our winder winds wire into coils.

**Marsh:** And solar?

**Kett:** Basic solar panels have their place — on the ground and upper floors, joined by poles. But when you need charge through the night, you want a boiler and an engine.

**Marsh:** What does Vane say about that?

**Kett:** He says the panels are charming. He says a lot of things are charming.
