# Interview — Finn Lattice, Lattice & Finch (Y149-06)

**Helen Marsh:** You sell the advanced assembler, the precision assembler, the etcher and the large advanced machines.

**Finn Lattice:** Mr Lattice has gone quiet, which means he is pleased. Ms Finch is already taking notes. The advanced assembler wires boards four times faster than a crude one by hand. The precision assembler builds microprocessors and RAM chips.

**Marsh:** Your letter says mind the power bill.

**Lattice:** It is a large letter. The machines are large machines. They draw EC. We sell them, and we warn.

**Marsh:** What does Ms Finch take notes on?

**Lattice:** Everything, she says. She takes notes on everything.
