# Interview — Vesper Hall, Underhill Excavation (Y149-03)

**Helen Marsh:** You dig below ground, where the ore is richer.

**Vesper Hall:** Cheerfully, and the kiln bakes stone into brick and sand into glass, eight at a time. We poured a concrete floor for Pillar's press shop last season. It holds.

**Marsh:** What do you dig for, mostly?

**Hall:** Ore, sand, stone, concrete. Whatever the yards need below the surface. The rich ore is down there, and the elevators bring it up.

**Marsh:** And the waste?

**Hall:** What waste? Ore does not make waste. Furnaces do.
