# Industrial Courier — Y157-04, issue 84

**Front page: drill contract at Ashgrove**
Ashgrove Ironstone has taken delivery of two basic drills from its own shop. The basic drill cuts iron twice as fast as a cheap one, and the yards say the difference shows in the first week. Tad Frall says they put them straight on the face and the ore came up cleaner.

**Front page: Brannock panel order**
Brannock & Daughters has sold a run of panels and poles to the Wellbourne pump house extension. The wires run underground from Y157-06. Wellbourne says the advanced pump will sit in the new room and give four times the water of the cheap panel setup.
