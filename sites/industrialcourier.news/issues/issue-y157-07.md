# Industrial Courier — Y157-07, issue 85

**Front page: Swiftline fast belts**
Swiftline Conveyors has delivered the first run of fast belts to the Gearwright yard. They move twice what Brannock's belts do, according to the sales sheet, and the yards are testing them on the long arm cells. The Gearwright yard crew reports the belt held on a long arm cell — grabs from two behind, drops two ahead — though the arm needed adjusting after two hours. They are not yet everywhere; Swiftline says six weeks late.

**Front page: Halverson wafer saw**
The wafer saw at Halverson's silicon yard cut its first crystal into six wafers with a fine wire and no scrap. The crystal grower is slower, they say, twenty seconds a crystal, and so is their patience. Sue Lin of Sablon, visiting the wafer yard, said she has never seen a wafer come out clean on the first pass.
