# Industrial Courier — Y156-12, issue 83

**Front page: Lattice & Finch precision order**
Lattice & Finch has taken an order for eight precision assemblers from a yard wiring boards four at a time. The advanced assembler wires boards four times faster than a crude one by hand, the letter says, and the precision assembler faster still. The order is for control boards and tube boards, to be delivered by Y157-03. Lattice & Finch sells the advanced assembler, the precision assembler, the etcher and the large advanced machines.

**Front page: Sablon tubes**
Sablon Glassworks reports a full season of tube orders. Nothing is as patient as a tube, their letter says, and they have never once hurried it. The evacuator pulls the air out with a water jet from a pipe: two tubes a run, no waste, no fire. Saul Rens of Sablon says the tube yard is full to Y160.
