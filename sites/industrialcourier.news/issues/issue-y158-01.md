# Industrial Courier — Y158-01, issue 86

**Front page: Cinderhall coke oven contract**
Cinderhall Foundry has renewed its coke supply contract with the coal yards for another season. The coke oven bakes coal into coke with no fire of its own, and leaves waste out the back that must be hauled away. Haulers say the queues at the back gate are longer than last year. Nothing burns waste, and boilers refuse it; the trade post takes it for a small fee.

**Front page: Wellbourne advanced pump**
Wellbourne Waterworks delivered its first advanced pump to a city yard. Four times the water, in a 2x2 frame. The pump shop at Kettering & Vane says orders are steady. Rosa Kett of Kettering & Vane says the advanced pump draws EC only while it works, which keeps the power bill down.
