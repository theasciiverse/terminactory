# Industrial Courier — Y156-03, issue 82

**Front page: Kettering boilers**
Kettering & Vane reports a full order book for boilers and steam engines. Mr Kettering says the coil is crude; Mr Vane says it is charming. The winder winds wire into coils, and the drawer pulls wire finer than any press. Rosa Kett says the boilers will out-charge a field of panels.
