# Industrial Courier — Y153-11, issue 78

**Front page: Ampleworth advanced drill**
Ampleworth Motor Works has delivered an advanced drill to the Underhill dig yard. It runs on EC and digs twice as fast as a basic drill. The Underhill dig yard filled a crate in half the shift, and the EC meter did not flicker. The coal bill is the same as it ever was, which is to say it exists. Ben Rye of the kiln crew says the rich ore is down there and the floor must hold.

**Front page: steam tractor trials**
The steam tractor ran its first trial on the mill road last month. Engine block, frame, hardened gears, control panel and generator together. It started on the third pull. Caleb Pillar of Pillar & Sons says it will raise a floor above the ground when it is ready.
