# Industrial Courier — Y152-02, issue 74

**Front page: Pillar rolling mill**
Pillar & Sons has rolled its first steel girders on the new mill. Steel sheets and girders, squeezed from plate, and the welder joins them into shells and frames. Caleb Pillar says a girder holds enough, and then some.

**Front page: Gearwright crude assembler**
The crude assembler at the Gearwright yard has built its thousandth gearbox. Valves, gauges, lamps, relays and gearboxes, patiently, at ten seconds each. It went straight to one thousand and one, and no one turned the machine off.
