# Industrial Courier — Y158-10, issue 87

**Front page: Halverson bids for silicon sand**
Halverson Smelting has placed a standing order for sand from the Underhill dig yards, to feed the arc furnace at the wafer yard. The contract runs to Y160 and is worth an estimated three thousand crowns a season. Underhill's kiln crews say the sand is cleaner since the crusher was repaired, and Vesper Hall of Underhill says the dig yard has two shifts running again for the first time since Y149. The sand goes in the arc furnace with coke, melts on power alone, and comes out as molten silicon for the crystal grower. It is the first big contract the dig yard has signed since the closures.

**Front page: Meridian opens**
Meridian Instruments opened a second chip yard, keyed by the first microprocessor (x4x1). The company letter arrived with snacks, according to staff at the gate, and no one has reported what the snacks were. Meridian sells the fine etcher and the large precision assembler. Helen Marsh notes that of the fifteen companies, only Meridian opened with snacks and no price list. She has written to ask. No reply yet.
