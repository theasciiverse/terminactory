# Industrial Courier — Y155-08, issue 81

**Front page: waste queues at Cinderhall**
Haulers report longer queues at the Cinderhall back gate, where waste leaves the blast furnace. Nothing burns it, and boilers refuse it. The trade post takes it for a small fee. A machine whose back has nowhere to put waste fills up, stops, and says so.

**Front page: fast belts delayed**
Swiftline's fast belts are running six weeks late. The yards testing them on long arm cells say the wait is the point: twice the load, when they arrive. The Gearwright yard crew says they will believe the second belt when it arrives.
