# Industrial Courier — Y150-10, issue 70

**Front page: Wellbourne pipes**
Wellbourne Waterworks has laid pipe to the new pump house on Mill Lane. Water goes wherever the pipes do, their letter says, and the advanced pump gives four times the water. The road was closed for one day.

**Front page: kiln sale**
Underhill Excavation sold two kilns to the city yards this season. Eight at a time, burning coal: stone into brick, sand into glass. Vesper Hall says the ore is richer below ground.
