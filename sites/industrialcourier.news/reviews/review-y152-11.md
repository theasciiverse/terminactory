# Review — Underhill kiln (Y152-11)

**Reviewer:** Vesper Hall, Underhill Excavation.

The kiln fires eight at a time, burning coal: stone into brick, sand into glass. We ran it through the winter and the bricks came out even. Eight at a time is the rhythm: load, fire, cool, unload, repeat. Coal goes in the front, brick comes out the front, and nothing goes out the back except smoke.

Ben Rye brings soup on kiln days. The kiln does not care about soup, but we do.
