# Review — Brannock furnace (Y155-05)

**Reviewer:** Cinderhall yard crew, on trial.

Brannock's furnace melts ore fast and leaves waste out the back. We ran it on copper ore and iron ore, side by side with our own blast furnace. The Brannock furnace heats quicker, and the waste comes out the back just the same. The question is where the waste goes, and Brannock does not answer that question. They sell the furnace, not the haul.
