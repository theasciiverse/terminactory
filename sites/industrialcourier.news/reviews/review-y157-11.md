# Review — Kettering winder (Y157-11)

**Reviewer:** Rosa Kett, Kettering & Vane boiler shop.

The winder winds wire into coils, and iron rods into springs. We ran a coil trial with fine wire: three fine wire a coil, but it takes a drawer to make the fine wire first. Two coils from six fine wire, four seconds a coil. It keeps tension better than the hand crank, and it does not complain when the wire snaps.

Mr Kettering says the coil is crude. I say the winder is not.
