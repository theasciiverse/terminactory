# Review — Ampleworth advanced drill (Y153-02)

**Reviewer:** Underhill dig yard, on loan from Ampleworth Motor Works.

The advanced drill runs on EC and digs twice as fast as a basic drill. We put it on the rich ore below the floor. It filled a crate in half the shift, and the EC meter did not flicker. The coal bill is the same as it ever was, which is to say it exists.

It digs twice as fast. The question is whether the face can keep up.
