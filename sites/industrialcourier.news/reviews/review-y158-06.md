# Review — Evacuator (Y158-06)

**Reviewer:** Jody Marsh, Sablon tube yard (volunteer).

The evacuator pulls the air out of glass with a water jet from a pipe: two tubes a run, no waste, no fire. We ran it on a trial shift with Sablon's lamps and copper sheet. The tubes came out clear, the water ran steady, and the glass did not crack. Nothing is as patient as a tube, Saul Rens says, and the evacuator does not hurry it.

The downside: it needs a pipe, and water, and someone to watch the jet. It is quiet, and the yard is quiet, and after two hours you forget it is running. That is when you check it.
