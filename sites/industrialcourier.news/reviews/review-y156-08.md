# Review — Swiftline fast belts (Y156-08)

**Reviewer:** Gearwright yard crew, on long arm cells.

Fast belts move twice what Brannock's do, according to the sales sheet. We loaded them on a long arm cell: grabs from two behind, drops two ahead, over whatever sits between. The belt held. The arm needed adjusting after two hours. We ran a second belt and it held longer.

They are not yet everywhere. Swiftline says six weeks late. We say we will believe the second belt when it arrives, and the third when it loads.
