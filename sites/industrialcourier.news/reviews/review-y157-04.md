# Review — Gearwright lathe (Y157-04)

**Reviewer:** Tad Frall, Ashgrove crusher crew.

The lathe turns plate into rods and rods into screws, and steel into steel rods and bearings. We ran it on a trial shift. Verdict: steady on rods, slow on screws — four screws a rod at five minutes a batch — and the chuck wobbles on the third tightening.

It does what the letter says. I would not buy one for screws.
