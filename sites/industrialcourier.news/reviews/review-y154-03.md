# Review — Wellbourne advanced pump (Y154-03)

**Reviewer:** Mira Wellbourne, Wellbourne Waterworks.

The advanced pump gives four times the water, in a 2x2 frame. It draws EC only while it works, which keeps the power bill down. We set it at the new pump house on Mill Lane and the water went where the pipes went. The mixer takes it from the pipe to make concrete, or etchant, depending on what you put in the belt.

Four times the water is a lot of water. You need the pipes to match.
