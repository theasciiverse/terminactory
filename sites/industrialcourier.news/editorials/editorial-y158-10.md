# Editorial — The second key (Y158-10)

Companies open when their key is made, and not before. Hollowmere and Brannock have been open from the start. Meridian waited for the microprocessor (x4x1), and when it opened, its letter arrived with snacks.

We are all friends here, the letter says. Meridian has been waiting for you.

I have kept letters from fourteen companies. They are short, and a little funny, and they all say more or less the same thing: we sell what works, and if it does not, one of us will fix it. Except Meridian's, which brought snacks and no price list. I do not know what to make of a company that opens with snacks. I have written to ask. No reply yet.
