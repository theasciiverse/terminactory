# Editorial — Waiting for sand (Y157-07)

The arc furnace melts sand on power alone, no fuel, and makes fewer opportunities than the blast furnace — the letter's word, not mine. The crystal grower takes twenty seconds a crystal, slowly. The wafer saw cuts six wafers from a crystal with a fine wire, no scrap.

Halverson's letter ends: our crystal grower and wafer saw are slow, and so is our patience. I have stood by a grower at shift change. I know what they mean.
