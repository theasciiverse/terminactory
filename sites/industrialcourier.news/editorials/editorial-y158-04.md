# Editorial — Fifteen companies (Y158-04)

There are fifteen companies, and each has a letter. Hollowmere and Brannock have been open from the start. Ashgrove wants copper wire, Cinderhall coke, Wellbourne copper pipe, Kettering & Vane copper coil, Pillar & Sons a steel girder, Underhill concrete, Gearwright an iron gear, Swiftline a gearbox, Ampleworth an electric motor, Lattice & Finch a control board, Sablon a tube, Halverson a silicon ingot, Meridian a microprocessor (x4x1).

Make the key, and the company opens. Read the letter. They are short, and a little funny. Fourteen of them say more or less the same thing: we sell what works, and if it does not, one of us will fix it. Meridian's brought snacks. I do not know what to make of that, and I have written to ask. No reply yet.
