# Editorial — Patient glass (Y156-12)

Sablon has blown glass for a hundred years and never once hurried it. Nothing is as patient as a tube. The evacuator pulls the air out of glass with a water jet from a pipe: two tubes a run, no waste, no fire.

I visited the tube yard once. The quiet was the first thing, and the second. No one shouts in a tube yard. The glass cools when it cools.
