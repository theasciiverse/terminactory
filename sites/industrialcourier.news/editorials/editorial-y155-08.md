# Editorial — Out the back (Y155-08)

The blast furnace leaves waste out the back. So do the coke oven, the kiln, and some etchant and silicon recipes. Waste is not fuel; nothing burns it. It costs money to get rid of it.

A machine whose back has nowhere to put waste stops, and says so. The haulers at Cinderhall say the queues are longer every season. Someone should decide where it all goes. So far, no one has.
