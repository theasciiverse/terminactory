# Editorial — The thousandth (Y152-02)

The crude assembler builds valves, gauges, lamps, relays and gearboxes, patiently, at ten seconds each. The Gearwright yard marked its thousandth gearbox last month. No one turned off the machine. It went straight to gearbox one thousand and one.

There is a kind of patience in a machine that does not count. I wish more of the yard had it.
