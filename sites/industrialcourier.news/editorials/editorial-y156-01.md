# Editorial — The trade fair (Y156-01)

The regional trade fair at the mill yard ran for three days in Y156-01. Brannock showed a furnace, Gearwright a crude assembler, Swiftline a fast belt, Wellbourne a pipe. The cheap drill drew a crowd, because it works anywhere on the ground and does not complain. Meridian did not attend. Halverson brought a wafer and would not let anyone touch it.

A trade fair is where the yards see what they do not make. We saw a lot we do not make.
