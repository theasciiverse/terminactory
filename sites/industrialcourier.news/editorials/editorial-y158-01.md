# Editorial — Waste has a price (Y158-01)

Nothing burns waste. Boilers, furnaces and kilns all refuse it. It leaves a machine out the back, never the front, and a machine whose back has nowhere to put it fills up, stops, and says so.

The trade post will take it, set to sell for money, for a small fee. So every line that makes waste needs a second way out: a belt, a crate, or a post. Cinderhall's back gate queues tell the story of a town that still has not decided where the waste goes.
