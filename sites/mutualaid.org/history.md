# History of the mutual aid hall

The hall on Mill Lane has held the Workers Mutual Aid Board since Y130, when Underhill's kiln crew needed a place to meet after the second kiln was fired. Louise Crell has minuted every meeting since Y134. Mrs Gable has run the tea trolley longer than anyone can remember and takes no slice herself.

**Y134** — First minutes kept. Simon Kyrk given the Lattice & Finch spare keys.
**Y137** — Second kiln fired at Underhill. Bench by the weigh house painted grey.
**Y138** — Motion for a second workers' board meeting lapsed for want of a seconder.
**Y140** — The minutes say no second meeting; Doris Hale and Rupert Hale say everyone was there.
**Y142** — Simon Kyrk dies. The spare keys go missing.
**Y152** — The Gearwright assembler builds its thousandth gearbox.
**Y153** — Nessa Cragg dies. Her carbon copies fill a drawer at the council office.
**Y158** — The pot holds fourteen crowns and six pence. The keys are still missing.

The hall is still here. The tea is still hot. Mrs Gable still takes no slice.
