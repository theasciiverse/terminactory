# Constitution of the Workers Mutual Aid Board

**Purpose.** Mutual aid among workers of the mills, yards and shops. Small grievances, potlucks, tea, and the sharing of what we have.

**Membership.** Any worker may attend. Mrs Gable attends with the tea trolley and takes no slice herself.

**Officers.** Shop steward (Louise Crell) keeps the minutes. Treasurer (Ben Rye) keeps the pot. Clerk (Anita Voss) files grievances in triplicate.

**Meetings.** Second Saturday of the month, at the hall on Mill Lane, unless the boiler shop needs overhaul.

**Funds.** The pot holds small sums from raffles and collections. Treasurer's reports are posted below. No one is turned away for want of a crown.

**Keys.** The Lattice & Finch spare keys are missing. The board does not have them and does not know who does.

**Amendments.** By show of hands. The tea trolley rule is not amendable.
