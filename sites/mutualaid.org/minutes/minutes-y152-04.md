# Minutes — Y152-04-10

**Present:** Louise Crell, Ben Rye, Orin Pike, Anita Voss, M. Hale.
**Apologies:** Ellen Voss, Doris Hale, Rupert Hale.

**1. Thousandth gearbox.** The Gearwright crude assembler built its thousandth gearbox. It went straight to one thousand and one. Dale Gearwright said no one turned the machine off. Ben Rye said he listened to it through the wall from the empty Cinderhall floor.

**2. Mangle ad.** Moll Peck's ad remains on the board. She asked for no replies, and none have been given. Anita Voss said the ad is still open and she does not know why.

**3. Night shift pay.** Cold allowance confirmed at two crowns a week or a cup of tea. Mrs Gable takes no slice herself.

**4. Next meeting:** Y152-05-08.

Signed, Louise Crell.
