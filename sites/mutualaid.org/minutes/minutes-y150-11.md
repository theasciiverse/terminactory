# Minutes — Y150-11-11

**Present:** Louise Crell, Ben Rye, Orin Pike, Anita Voss, M. Hale, Ellen Voss, Doris Hale.
**Apologies:** Rupert Hale.

**1. Wellbourne pipes.** Laid to the pump house on Mill Lane. Water goes wherever the pipes do. Mira Wellbourne said the advanced pump gives four times the water. Doris Hale asked where the water goes after that; no one knew.

**2. Kilns.** Underhill sold two kilns to the city yards. Eight at a time, burning coal: stone into brick, sand into glass. Vesper Hall said the ore is richer below ground.

**3. Next meeting:** Y150-12-09.

Signed, Louise Crell.
