# Minutes — Y153-10-10

**Present:** Louise Crell, Ben Rye, Orin Pike, Anita Voss, M. Hale, Ellen Voss, Doris Hale, Rupert Hale.
**Apologies:** none.

**1. Nessa Cragg.** Her death noted. She wrote planning objections longhand and kept every carbon copy. Her potluck biscuits were missed. Cora Bell has a drawer of her carbons at the council office.

**2. Concrete pour.** Underhill's pour at Pillar's press shop went ahead Y153-04-15. Caleb Pillar says the floor holds. Ben Rye said he watched the mixer and it turned for twenty minutes without stopping.

**3. Mangle ad.** Moll Peck's ad remains on the board at bst.market. She asked for no replies, and none have been given. The Gazette still prints her letter. Louise Crell said the board should leave it alone.

**4. Next meeting:** Y153-11-14.

Signed, Louise Crell.
