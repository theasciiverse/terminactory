# Potluck — Y157-06-19

Held at the hall. Ben Rye brought soup. Orin Pike ran the raffle. Mrs Gable brought the tea and took no slice. Twenty-three people attended, and one person who was not expected.

**Signed up at the time:** Ben Rye, Orin Pike, Ellen Voss, Louise Crell, Dana K., M. Hale, Doris Hale, Rupert Hale.
