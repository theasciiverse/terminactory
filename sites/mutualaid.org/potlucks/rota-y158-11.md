# Potluck rota — Y158-11

Held at the mutual aid hall, Mill Lane, Y158-11-08, six o'clock. Sign up with Ellen Voss on the sheet by the door. Mrs Gable brings the tea and takes no slice herself.

**Rota**
- Soup: Ben Rye
- Bread: Anita Voss
- Cake: Louise Crell (if the oven holds)
- Raffle tickets: Orin Pike
- Tea: Mrs Gable (no slice)
- Notebooks, not food: Dana K. (window seat)

**Replies**
- **Dana K., Y158-10-25:** I will bring notebooks, not food. I will sit by the window.
- **Ellen Voss, Y158-10-26:** Notebooks are welcome. The window seat is free.
- **M. Hale, Y158-10-27:** I will bring the bucket. I sit by the door.
- **Anita Voss, Y158-10-28:** The bread is sourdough. It keeps.
