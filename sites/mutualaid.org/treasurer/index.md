# Treasurer's reports

Kept by Ben Rye, welder, Underhill kiln crew. The pot holds small sums from raffles and collections.

**Y158-11-11** — Pot: 14 crowns 6 pence. In: raffle tickets (Orin Pike), 8 crowns. Out: soldering iron prize, 4 crowns; box of screws, 2 crowns. Balance: 8 crowns 6 pence.

**Y157-12-11** — Pot: 12 crowns 4 pence. In: raffle, 6 crowns; soup pot collection, 3 crowns. Out: teapot donation (unclaimed, returned), 0 crowns; cake ingredients, 3 crowns. Balance: 12 crowns 4 pence.

**Y156-10-08** — Pot: 9 crowns 2 pence. In: collection, 5 crowns. Out: tool log notebook (never used), 1 crown 8 pence. Balance: 9 crowns 2 pence.

**Y155-04-09** — Pot: 6 crowns 4 pence. In: raffle, 4 crowns. Out: rope inquiry (no rope found), 0 crowns. Balance: 6 crowns 4 pence.

**Y154-09-11** — Pot: 8 crowns. In: cake sale, 5 crowns. Out: bucket for the weigh house roof, 2 crowns. Balance: 8 crowns.

**Note.** The pot never runs out, and it never grows. Ben Rye says that is how mutual aid works.
