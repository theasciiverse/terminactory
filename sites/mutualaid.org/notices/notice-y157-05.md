# Notice — Key items and company letters (Y157-05-12)

A company opens when its key item is made. Hollowmere and Brannock have been open from the start. Ashgrove wants copper wire, Cinderhall coke, Wellbourne copper pipe, Kettering & Vane copper coil, Pillar & Sons a steel girder, Underhill concrete, Gearwright an iron gear, Swiftline a gearbox, Ampleworth an electric motor, Lattice & Finch a control board, Sablon a tube, Halverson a silicon ingot, Meridian a microprocessor.

Read the letters when they arrive. They are short, and a little funny. Meridian's came with snacks.
