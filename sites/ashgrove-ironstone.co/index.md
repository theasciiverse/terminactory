# Ashgrove Ironstone

Ironstone, cut fine. Ashgrove has worked the same rock for longer than anyone here will say aloud, and we have never once hurried it. We make the drills and crushers that get ore out of the ground and into a state where it is ready to be melted.

Copper wire is how you know you have come to the right place. Fine hands, please. Here you will find [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/products|our basic drill and crusher]], [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/about|the family and the stone]], and [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/faq|the questions people actually ask]].

Our crushed grit goes into the blast furnaces at [[cinderhall.co/|Cinderhall Foundry]], which melt it better than whole rock. The sand from our crusher goes on to [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne Waterworks]] for concrete, and to [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]] for glass. The cheap drill we improve upon is made by [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere Mining Co.]], who are honest about it and say so. Presses, furnaces and belts for the rest are at [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]], and we have never been able to fault them.
