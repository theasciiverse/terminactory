# Ashgrove Ironstone: Products

**Basic drill.** Our basic drill cuts any ore much faster than a cheap one. On iron it is a great deal quicker, and it still pushes ore out of its front. It needs open ground and a steady supply of coal, since it burns coal and stops without it. Put a belt or a machine in front to take what it digs. Set it on a coal patch and it will feed itself, which we consider tidy. It is unlocked by research once copper wire has been made.

**Crusher.** The crusher grinds ore into grit, and stone into sand. Grit melts further than whole rock does, so a crushed lump yields more metal in the furnace. It makes copper grit, iron grit and sand. Sand is the start of glass, concrete and silicon. It runs on EC, so give it power, and it can push its grit straight into a furnace in front with no belt between. Crush iron ore before you melt it when you can.
