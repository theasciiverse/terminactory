# Ashgrove Ironstone: Faq

**Why is my crusher slow?**
It runs on EC, and a short grid slows everything on it. Check the poles before you blame the crusher. [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] sell the poles, and they will tell you so.

**Can I put stone through it?**
Yes. Stone becomes sand. Sand goes to the kiln at [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]], which bakes it into glass. [[sablon-glass.co/|Sablon Glassworks]] will tell you what glass is for, at length.

**Does the basic drill need a special coal?**
No. Coal is coal. Coke is better for the furnaces, but no drill has asked for it.

**What became of the cheap drill?**
[[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere]] still sell it. It still does the job, at a slower pace, and we have no complaints. We do have a sample of ironstone on the desk that nobody has moved since the crusher was installed. We have not needed to move it.
