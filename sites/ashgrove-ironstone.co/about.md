# Ashgrove Ironstone: About

Ashgrove was a quarry before it was a company. The first Ashgrove, Harriet, could tell good ironstone by the sound it made under a hammer, and she was not shy about telling people which of their rocks were rubbish. Her grandson Frederick took the same ear into the drill and the crusher, and the motto on the gate for generations was simply: Fine hands.

We are proud of our small, precise machines. We do not make the biggest drill in the valley, and we do not want to. We make the one that will not waste a lump.

We still send a letter of thanks to [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere]] for their cheap drill. They still reply with a note saying it was never a competition. It was.

The works manager, Miss Joan Pell, keeps the ledgers in pencil, which she says is more honest. She has been asked whether the pencil will last. She says it has lasted this far.
