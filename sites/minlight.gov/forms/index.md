# Forms

Ministry of Light Industry forms, current and withdrawn. Request pads from the regional office.

**Current**
- **MLI-1** — Application for a small boiler licence (under fifty horsepower). Renewed annually.
- **MLI-1R** — Boiler licence renewal, due each January.
- **MLI-4A** — Gauge calibration record, first version (Y135-12-01 to Y145-04-10). One record per gauge: date, technician, result.
- **MLI-4B** — Gauge calibration record, current version. Retain for five years.
- **MLI-4B-Late** — Late calibration record. Use when a gauge misses its deadline.
- **MLI-7** — Monthly waste return, current. Reinstated Y159-01-05 after MLI-Y158-04 was withdrawn. Due the last day of the following month.

**Withdrawn**
- **MLI-7A** — Monthly waste return. Withdrawn Y158-05-01; replaced by MLI-7; reinstated then withdrawn again Y159-01-05. Destroy or return unused pads.
- **MLI-7** (first version) — Monthly waste return, Y142-12-01 to Y147-10-01. Replaced by MLI-7A.
- **MLI-4B (spring schedule)** — Superseded by the autumn calibration deadline, Y145-04-10.

Forms named in bulletins are linked there: [[minlight.gov/bulletins|All bulletins]].
