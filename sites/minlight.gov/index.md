# Ministry of Light Industry — Current Bulletins

Official notices for licensed plants, shops and yards. File everything on time. The forms are at [[minlight.gov/forms|Forms]].

**Bulletin MLI-Y159-01 — Disregard previous notice**
Form MLI-7 is reinstated for monthly waste returns; MLI-Y158-04 is withdrawn. See [[minlight.gov/bulletins/bulletin-y159-01|Bulletin MLI-Y159-01]].

**Bulletin MLI-Y158-10 — Calibration of pressure gauges**
All gauges in service at steam plants must be calibrated by Y158-11-30. Records on Form MLI-4B, retained five years. See [[minlight.gov/bulletins/bulletin-y158-10|Bulletin MLI-Y158-10]].

**Bulletin MLI-Y158-04 — New form for waste returns**
Form MLI-7 replaces Form MLI-7A for monthly waste returns. MLI-7A is withdrawn as of Y158-05-01. (See also MLI-Y159-01.) See [[minlight.gov/bulletins/bulletin-y158-04|Bulletin MLI-Y158-04]].

**Bulletin MLI-Y157-11 — Reminder: file the old returns**
Plants that have not filed the Y156 waste returns on Form MLI-7A must do so by Y157-12-31. See [[minlight.gov/bulletins/bulletin-y157-11|Bulletin MLI-Y157-11]].

**Bulletin MLI-Y156-08 — Standards update: steel sheets**
Standard MLI-ST-12 revised to Revision C. Existing Revision B stock acceptable until Y157-01-01. See [[minlight.gov/bulletins/bulletin-y156-08|Bulletin MLI-Y156-08]].
