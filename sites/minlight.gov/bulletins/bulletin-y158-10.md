# Bulletin MLI-Y158-10 — Gauge calibration

**Issued:** Y158-10-06 **Effective:** Y158-10-06 **Deadline:** Y158-11-30 **Reference:** MLI-B-158-10

All pressure gauges in service at licensed steam plants must be calibrated by Y158-11-30. Calibration must be carried out by a licensed technician and recorded on Form MLI-4B. Records must be retained for five years and produced on request.

Clause 1 — Gauges found outside tolerance must be tagged "out of service" and withdrawn from the line. A gauge may not be returned to service until it has passed calibration and the record has been filed.

Clause 2 — Calibration deadlines are set by the regional office and appear in this bulletin each autumn. The previous deadline was Y157-11-30. Plants that missed it should file Form MLI-4B-Late without delay.

Clause 3 — Plants operating without a calibrated gauge after Y158-11-30 will be issued a compliance notice. The standard fee is two crowns per gauge per week.

Clause 4 — This bulletin supersedes Bulletin MLI-Y145-04. The spring calibration schedule is withdrawn.
