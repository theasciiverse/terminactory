# Bulletin MLI-Y159-01 — Disregard previous notice

**Issued:** Y159-01-05 **Effective:** Y159-01-05 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-159-01

Plants are instructed to disregard Bulletin MLI-Y158-04 (Waste returns form MLI-7), effective immediately. Form MLI-7 is reinstated for monthly waste returns. Form MLI-7A remains withdrawn.

Clause 1 — The monthly return is due on the last day of the following month. The first reinstated return covers Y159-01 and is due Y159-02-28.

Clause 2 — This bulletin corrects Bulletin MLI-Y158-04, which is hereby withdrawn. Plants that destroyed MLI-7 pads under MLI-Y158-04 should request replacements from the regional office.

Clause 3 — Apologies for any inconvenience.
