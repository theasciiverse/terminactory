# Bulletin MLI-Y132-07 — Gauge calibration schedule

**Issued:** Y132-07-14 **Effective:** Y132-08-01 **Deadline:** Y132-10-31 **Reference:** MLI-B-132-07

All pressure gauges in service at licensed steam plants must be calibrated by Y132-10-31. Calibration records must be kept and produced on request.

Clause 1 — Calibration takes place in the autumn of each year.

Clause 2 — This is the first gauge calibration bulletin.
