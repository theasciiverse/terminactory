# Bulletin MLI-Y135-11 — Form MLI-4A

**Issued:** Y135-11-02 **Effective:** Y135-12-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-135-11

Form MLI-4A is introduced for gauge calibration records. Plants must keep a record for each gauge calibrated, showing the date, the technician, and the result.

Clause 1 — Form MLI-4A is available from the regional office.

Clause 2 — This form is replaced by Form MLI-4B.
