# Bulletin MLI-Y140-06 — Steam plant noise standard

**Issued:** Y140-06-15 **Effective:** Y140-07-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-140-06

Standard MLI-ST-09 sets acceptable noise levels for steam plants operating within city limits. Plants exceeding the level must fit baffles or reduce output until compliant.

Clause 1 — Measurements are taken at the property boundary during normal operating hours.

Clause 2 — Boilers operating at night are measured at the boundary between Y140-06-15 and Y140-06-30.
