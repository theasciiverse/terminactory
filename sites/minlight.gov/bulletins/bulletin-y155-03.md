# Bulletin MLI-Y155-03 — Standards revision: iron gears

**Issued:** Y155-03-12 **Effective:** Y155-04-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-155-03

Standard MLI-ST-07, iron gear tooth count, is revised to Revision E. Gears cut to Revision D remain acceptable until Y156-01-01.

Clause 1 — Gearwright Machine Works lathes cutting screws must conform to Revision E from Y155-04-01.

Clause 2 — Inspectors will check tooth count at the next routine visit.
