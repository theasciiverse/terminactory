# Bulletin MLI-Y153-06 — Reminder: MLI-7A returns

**Issued:** Y153-06-08 **Effective:** Y153-06-08 **Deadline:** Y153-07-31 **Reference:** MLI-B-153-06

Plants that have not filed the Y152 waste returns on Form MLI-7A must do so by Y153-07-31. This reminder is issued annually until the returns are received.

Clause 1 — Form MLI-7A remains the current form for waste returns until further notice.

Clause 2 — The standard late fee is one crown per month per missing return.
