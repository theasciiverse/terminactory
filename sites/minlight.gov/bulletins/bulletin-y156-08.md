# Bulletin MLI-Y156-08 — Standards update: steel sheets

**Issued:** Y156-08-04 **Effective:** Y156-09-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-156-08

Standard MLI-ST-12, steel sheet thickness, is revised to Revision C. Sheets produced to Revision B remain acceptable for use until Y157-01-01. After that date, all sheets must conform to Revision C.

Clause 1 — Inspectors will measure stock at the next routine visit. Plants with questions should write to the standards desk, not telephone.

Clause 2 — Rolling mills producing steel sheets must re-calibrate their rollers by Y156-10-01 to meet Revision C.

Clause 3 — This bulletin supersedes any earlier standard on steel sheet thickness. Revision B stock held after Y157-01-01 must be re-rolled or sold as scrap.
