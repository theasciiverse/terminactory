# Bulletin MLI-Y148-06 — Waste storage at foundries

**Issued:** Y148-06-04 **Effective:** Y148-06-04 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-148-06

Foundries producing waste must store it behind a fence or in a container until collected by a licensed hauler. Waste must not be left in the open yard.

Clause 1 — Cinderhall Foundry's old settlement lagoon is full and must not be used for further waste storage.

Clause 2 — A machine whose back has nowhere to put waste fills up, stops, and says so. Plan storage before you start the furnace.
