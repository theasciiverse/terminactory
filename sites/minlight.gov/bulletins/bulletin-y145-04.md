# Bulletin MLI-Y145-04 — Calibration deadline

**Issued:** Y145-04-10 **Effective:** Y145-04-10 **Deadline:** Y145-10-31 **Reference:** MLI-B-145-04

All pressure gauges in service at licensed steam plants must be calibrated by Y145-10-31. Calibration records must be kept on Form MLI-4B and retained for five years.

Clause 1 — The calibration deadline is now fixed in the autumn of each year. The previous schedule, set in the spring, is superseded.

Clause 2 — This bulletin stands until a later bulletin on calibration replaces it.
