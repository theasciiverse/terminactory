# Bulletin MLI-Y144-01 — Licence renewals

**Issued:** Y144-01-10 **Effective:** Y144-01-10 **Deadline:** Y144-02-28 **Reference:** MLI-B-144-01

All small boiler licences under Form MLI-1 are due for renewal by Y144-02-28. Licences not renewed will lapse, and the boiler may not be fired.

Clause 1 — Renewals are made on Form MLI-1R and include the boiler make, fuel, and licensed operator.

Clause 2 — This is the annual renewal notice; it is issued each January.
