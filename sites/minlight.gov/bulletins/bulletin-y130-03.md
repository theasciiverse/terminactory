# Bulletin MLI-Y130-03 — Licensing of small boilers

**Issued:** Y130-03-10 **Effective:** Y130-04-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-130-03

Small boilers of under fifty horsepower require a licence from the Ministry of Light Industry before they may be fired. Applications are made on Form MLI-1 and include the boiler make, the intended fuel, and the name of the licensed operator.

Clause 1 — Licences are renewed annually.

Clause 2 — Boilers of fifty horsepower or more require a separate permit, not covered by this bulletin.
