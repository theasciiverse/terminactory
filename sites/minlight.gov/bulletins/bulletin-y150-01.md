# Bulletin MLI-Y150-01 — Standards update: copper pipe

**Issued:** Y150-01-12 **Effective:** Y150-02-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-150-01

Standard MLI-ST-03, copper pipe wall thickness, is revised to Revision D. Pipe installed under Revision C remains in service; new pipe must conform to Revision D.

Clause 1 — The full text of Revision D is available from the standards desk on request.

Clause 2 — Wellbourne Waterworks pipe installations must conform to Revision D from Y150-02-01.
