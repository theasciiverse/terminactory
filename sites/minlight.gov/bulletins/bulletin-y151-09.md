# Bulletin MLI-Y151-09 — Disregard previous notice

**Issued:** Y151-09-03 **Effective:** Y151-09-03 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-151-09

Plants are instructed to disregard Bulletin MLI-Y150-01 (Standards update: copper pipe), Revision D. Copper pipe wall thickness reverts to Revision C until further notice.

Clause 1 — Revision D pipe installed under MLI-Y150-01 remains in service; no remedial action is required.

Clause 2 — New pipe should conform to Revision C. The standards desk will issue Revision D again when it is ready.

Clause 3 — This bulletin supersedes MLI-Y150-01.
