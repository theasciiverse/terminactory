# Bulletin MLI-Y142-11 — Waste returns form MLI-7

**Issued:** Y142-11-03 **Effective:** Y142-12-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-142-11

Form MLI-7 is introduced for monthly waste returns, effective Y142-12-01. The return is due on the last day of the following month. The first return on Form MLI-7 covers Y142-12 and is due Y143-01-31.

Clause 1 — Plants without a supply of Form MLI-7 should request pads from the regional office.

Clause 2 — This form is replaced by Form MLI-7A effective Y147-10-01.
