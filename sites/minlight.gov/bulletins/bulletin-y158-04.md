# Bulletin MLI-Y158-04 — Waste returns form MLI-7

**Issued:** Y158-04-22 **Effective:** Y158-05-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-158-04

Form MLI-7 replaces Form MLI-7A for monthly waste returns, effective Y158-05-01. Form MLI-7A is withdrawn. Plants still holding pads of MLI-7A should destroy them or return them to the regional office at no charge.

Clause 1 — The monthly return is due on the last day of the following month. The first return on Form MLI-7 covers Y158-05 and is due Y158-06-30.

Clause 2 — Plants filing after Y158-05-01 should note that Form MLI-7A is withdrawn. Use Form MLI-7 and mark the return period clearly.

Clause 3 — Waste returns are required of all plants that produce waste: blast furnaces, coke ovens, kilns, and etchant and silicon recipes. Trade posts set to research or split refuse waste; only posts set to money accept it, for a small fee.
