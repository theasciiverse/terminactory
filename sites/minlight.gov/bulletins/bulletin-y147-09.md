# Bulletin MLI-Y147-09 — Form MLI-7A replaces Form MLI-7

**Issued:** Y147-09-05 **Effective:** Y147-10-01 **Deadline:** none **Reference:** MLI-B-147-09

Form MLI-7A replaces Form MLI-7 for monthly waste returns, effective Y147-10-01. Form MLI-7 is withdrawn. Plants should use MLI-7A for the Y147-10 return, due Y147-11-30.

Clause 1 — The monthly return continues to be due on the last day of the following month.

Clause 2 — Plants without a supply of Form MLI-7A should request pads from the regional office.
