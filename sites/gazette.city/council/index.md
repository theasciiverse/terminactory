# Council notes

The council sits on Mill Lane, Tuesdays and Thursdays. Harold Pimm keeps the minutes.

**Recent**
- Y158-11: Mill Lane resurfacing postponed until after the winter frosts. Gravel delayed at the rail siding. Next sitting Y158-11-22.
- Y157-12: Winter grit bin placed at the top of Mill Lane. Not for private driveways.
- Y157-06: Chamber clock stopped; contractor has not signed. Meetings timed by wristwatch.
- Y156-09: Library back issues being re-shelved. Do not reshelve them yourself.
- Y156-02: Public scale out of order; part on order from Brannock & Daughters.
- Y154-11: Meeting room repainted, a grey between the old grey and the new grey.
- Y153-04: School roof leaks in the north corridor. Underhill Excavation to quote.
- Y151-08: New bench beside the weigh house. Ben Marsh approves the height.
- Y149-12: Six street lamps converted to new fittings. Old lamps in the shed.
- Y148-03: Weigh house scales working again, tested with a sack of coal and a brick.
- Y146-07: Council mower lent to the school field; back by August.
- Y144-10: Notice board replaced; old one in the shed with the lamps.
- Y142-05: Unclaimed items in the shed will be sold at the trade post after Y142-05-20.
- Y140-01: Office safe has a second key no one can find. It is locked anyway.
- Y138-09: Rail siding being relaid; gravel deliveries late two weeks.
- Y136-02: Bench beside the weigh house painted the meeting-room grey.
- Y134-11: Weigh house roof leaks when the wind is from the east. Bucket in place.
- Y132-06: Harold Pimm starts as council clerk. Office open Tuesday and Thursday.
- Y130-03: New desk in the council office.
- Y128-10: Mill Lane resurfacing, two weeks weather permitting.
- Y126-05: Six new street lamps lit on Mill Lane.
- Y124-11: Council office opens on Mill Lane.
