# Letters archive

Printed as received. Derek Morn of the Courier prints every letter; the Gazette prints most. Ben Marsh writes about screws in nearly every issue.

**Y158-11** — Ben Marsh on the cost of screws. Kit Marsh on the cost of screws. They are not related, they say.
**Y157-12** — Ben Marsh on hardened iron plate. Nessa Cragg objects to the kiln siding; disturbs the birds. Found after her death.
**Y157-06** — Ted Vok complains nobody returns the rope. Ira Bell says he has it.
**Y156-09** — Ben Marsh on gears that skip teeth. Doris Hale on Rupert's boiler shop shifts.
**Y156-02** — Ira Bell: the rope is still fine. Ted Vok: he would like it back.
**Y154-11** — Moll Peck says her mangle ad was a mistake and she still wants no replies.
**Y153-04** — Ben Marsh on springs that lose their wind. Nessa Cragg's last letter, on the concrete pour.
**Y151-08** — Ira Bell on the rope again. Doris Hale on Rupert working nights.
**Y149-12** — Ben Marsh on the cost of a new lathe, and on the crossword being too easy.
**Y148-03** — Ira Bell still has the rope; Ted Vok still wants it.
**Y146-07** — Doris Hale on boiler shop shifts. Ben Marsh on gears skipping teeth after the overhaul.
**Y144-10** — Ben Marsh on bearings that run hot, and on the unclaimed teapot prize.
**Y142-05** — Ted Vok about the rope. Ira Bell using it to tie down steel rods.
**Y140-01** — Ben Marsh on screws. Doris Hale says Rupert worked the second meeting of the workers' board last month, and everyone was there.
**Y138-09** — Ira Bell about the rope. Ted Vok has not seen it since Y136.
**Y136-02** — Ben Marsh on springs losing their wind. Doris Hale on night shifts.
**Y134-11** — Ben Marsh on gears skipping teeth, and on the easy crossword.
**Y132-06** — Ben Marsh on screws. Rita Sloane says the crossword is patient, not easy.
**Y130-03** — Ben Marsh on the cost of wire. Moll Peck on her mangle ad.
**Y128-10** — Ben Marsh on plate that warps. Moll Peck says no replies to the mangle ad.
**Y126-05** — Ben Marsh on wire that draws fine. Moll Peck on the mangle.
**Y124-11** — Ben Marsh welcomes the paper. Moll Peck writes about her mangle.

**Counter-letters**
- Y128-10 → Y138-09: Ted Vok and Ira Bell exchange about the rope across ten years. The rope remains where it is.
- Y154-11 → Y157-12: Moll Peck's mangle letter, printed first in Y124 and last in Y154. The editor says the file is closed.
