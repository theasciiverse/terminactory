# Year in review — Y154 to Y158

**Y154** — The council meeting room is repainted, a grey between the old grey and the new grey. Cinderhall applies for a taller ash silo. Moll Peck's last mangle letter is printed. The mangle file closes at ten sheets.

**Y156** — The public scale at the weigh house is out of order again. Wellbourne applies to extend its pump house for the advanced pump. Dean Foyle is remembered; he missed one day, Y143-07-04. The rope letters end: Ira Bell has it, Ted Vok wants it.

**Y157** — The council chamber clock stops; meetings are timed by wristwatch. Underhill lays a kiln siding for brick wagons. Simon Kyrk is remembered; he kept the spare keys for seventeen years. Brannock & Daughters beat Ashgrove Ironstone in the mill league final by two wickets.

**Y158** — Mill Lane resurfacing is postponed until after the winter frosts. Ashgrove applies to extend its scrap yard behind the old weighing house. The Gazette marks a year since Moll Peck died. The Gazette reaches issue 412.

This is the decade the Gazette knows best: Tessa Roe still editing, Harold Pimm still filing twice, Rita Sloane still setting crosswords the editor says are patient, not easy. The back issues from Y124 to Y131 are held at the city library, not online.
