# Year in review — Y134 to Y143

**Y134** — Gearwright applies to fit a press in the assembler shed; it needs EC and a pole. The weigh house roof leaks when the wind is from the east; a bucket stands under the drip. Ashgrove and Hollowmere are level at the top of the mill league.

**Y136** — Underhill applies for a second kiln, eight bricks at a time. Toby Dune dies; his tubes are boxed by the library door. The five-a-side league starts. Ben Marsh writes about springs losing their wind.

**Y138** — The rail siding behind the weigh house is relaid. Cinderhall applies to raise the ash silo by one storey. Ashgrove leads the mill league by two points. Ira Bell and Ted Vok begin their long exchange about a rope.

**Y140** — The council safe gets a second key no one can find. Underhill applies to pour a concrete floor in the dig yard. Doris Hale writes that Rupert worked the second meeting of the workers' board, and everyone was there. The workers' board minutes say no such meeting took place.

**Y141** — The Mill Lane resurfacing notice returns with a new date, Y141-05-20, though the earlier notice said Y128-10. Harold Pimm is asked which date stands. He does not say. A cached copy of the notice board is kept at the Not Found Archive.

**Y142** — The shed behind the council office fills with unclaimed lamps, a scale part, and Moll Peck's mangle. Anything unclaimed by Y142-05-20 will be sold at the trade post. Nothing is sold. Simon Kyrk dies; the spare keys go missing with him.

**Y143** — Dean Foyle misses his Gazette run on Y143-07-04 and never explains why. Ada Traynor writes her last post, "Back soon," on Y143-05-12. The decade ends with the Gazette in its rhythm and a few things that do not add up.
