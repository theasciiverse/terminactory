# Municipal Gazette — Y151-08-06, issue 334

**Council: the bench**
A new bench has been placed beside the weigh house, where the gazette is read aloud on Tuesdays. Ben Marsh says it is the right height. The old bench is in the shed behind the office with the lamps and the mangle. Harold Pimm says the new bench was donated by the library committee.

**Planning: silicon scrap yard at Halverson**
Halverson Smelting has applied to keep a larger pile of silicon scrap behind the arc furnace yard. It sells, they say, and will remelt. Objections close Y151-08-20. The application lists an arc furnace, a crystal grower, and a scrap heap behind the gate. Nessa Cragg's objection, longhand, says the scrap heap glows at night. Harold Pimm has logged it but does not know what to make of it.

**Obituary: Clem Ortiz**
Clem Ortiz, crane hand, idle since the closures, has died. He read the gazette every morning at the library. Cora Bell has written the obituary. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-ortiz|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: cranes and what they lift.

**Sport**
Five-a-side: Brannock beat Swiftline in a replay after the floodlight failed. Jem Croft says the floodlight was one of the new Mill Lane lamps and it has not worked since.

**Letters**
Ira Bell writes about the rope again. Doris Hale writes that Rupert is on nights again. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again. The editor prints them all and says the mangle file is eight sheets thick.
