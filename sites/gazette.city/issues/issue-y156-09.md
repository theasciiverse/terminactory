# Municipal Gazette — Y156-09-03, issue 393

**Council: the library shelves**
The city library on Mill Lane is re-shelving the back issues of this paper. Harold Pimm asks readers not to reshelve them themselves; the order is by year, then month, and he has a system. The back issues from Y124 to Y131 are held at the library, not online. The mangle is no longer in the shed; Harold Pimm says it was sold at the trade post in Y154.

**Planning: tube siding at Sablon**
Sablon Glassworks has applied to run a water pipe from the mains to the evacuator yard. Two tubes a run, no waste, no fire, their letter says. Objections close Y156-09-17. The application lists an evacuator, a water pipe, and a yard of glass cooling racks.

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: glass and the air taken out of it.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about gears that skip teeth. Doris Hale writes that her husband Rupert works the boiler shop and is rarely home. Moll Peck's mangle letter does not arrive. The editor says nothing about it.

**Sport**
Cricket practice resumes at the mill yard. The pitch is uneven but playable. Jem Croft says Ashgrove Ironstone is fielding a side.
