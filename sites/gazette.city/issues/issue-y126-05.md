# Municipal Gazette — Y126-05-03, issue 130

**Council: six new lamps**
Six new street lamps have been lit on Mill Lane, from the weigh house to the market corner. Old Man Hargrave says they are bright enough to read the Gazette by, which is high praise. The lamps burn kerosene until the EC poles arrive, which Harold Pimm expects before the autumn. The council will oil them weekly and has asked Mrs Gable to report any that flicker.

**Planning: press at Gearwright**
Gearwright Machine Works has applied for a press to squeeze ingots into plates and wire, to stand beside the crude assembler shed. The press needs no EC, the application says, but a pole helps. Objections close Y126-05-17. Nessa Cragg has already filed her objection longhand, on the grounds that the press will vibrate the weigh house wall. Harold Pimm has logged it.

**The mill field**
The mill field has been mown for the season and the goal lines painted. Jem Croft reports that five-a-side practice begins next Saturday, teams from each mill, boots required. Hollowmere's side is said to be quickest on the ball.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes that wire drawn on a press is finer than wire drawn by hand, and asks whether the Gearwright press will draw fine wire. The editor replies that it will draw wire, and leaves the fineness to the foreman. Moll Peck writes again about her mangle, still one owner, still unanswered.
