# Municipal Gazette — Y148-03-05, issue 306

**Council: the weighing house**
The weighing house scales are working again, after the part from Brannock & Daughters arrived. Harold Pimm tested it with a sack of coal and a brick and says it balances to within half a pound. The scale part has been on the shelf since Y126.

**Planning: second blast furnace at Cinderhall**
Cinderhall Foundry has applied to fire a second blast furnace beside the first. More coke, more waste, more molten iron. Objections close Y148-03-19. The application lists a coke oven, a molder, and a waste pipe to the old settlement lagoon. Nessa Cragg's objection, longhand, says the lagoon has been full since Y122 and the ash blows toward Market Street. Harold Pimm has logged it.

**Sport**
Ashgrove Ironstone leads the mill league. Jem Croft says the lead is three points and shrinking, with Swiftline two games in hand.

**Letters**
Ira Bell writes that he still has the rope, and Ted Vok writes that he still wants it. The editor prints both and says the rope file is now two sheets thick. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again, unanswered.
