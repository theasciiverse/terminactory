# Municipal Gazette — Y149-12-04, issue 318

**Council: the street lamps**
Six street lamps on Mill Lane have been converted to the new fittings. The old lamps are in the shed behind the council office, waiting for someone to claim them. Ben Marsh says the new lamps are brighter but colder. Harold Pimm says the old lamps will be sold at the trade post after Y149-12-18.

**Planning: rolling mill roof at Pillar**
Pillar & Sons has applied to extend the rolling mill roof over the girder rack. Steel sheets will be stored under cover for the first time. Objections close Y149-12-18. The application lists a rolling mill, steel sheets, girders, and a roof of reinforced concrete.

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: things stored under cover.

**Sport**
The mill league is paused for the snow. The hall is booked for Tuesday and Thursday. Jem Croft says the five-a-side will resume when the pitch dries.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about the cost of a new lathe, and adds that the crossword is too easy. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again. The editor says he has considered binding the mangle letters into a volume.
