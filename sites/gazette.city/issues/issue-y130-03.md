# Municipal Gazette — Y130-03-05, issue 162

**Council: the new desk**
The council office has a new desk. Harold Pimm says it holds the applications, the scale part from Brannock & Daughters, and the unclaimed mangle. The desk is oak, second-hand from the trade post, and cost four crowns. Old Man Hargrave sat at it on Tuesday and said it was the right height.

**Planning: brick kiln at Underhill**
Underhill Excavation has applied to build a kiln that fires eight at a time, burning coal: stone into brick, sand into glass. The kiln stands beside the dig yard where the ore is richer. Objections close Y130-03-17. Rita Sloane has asked whether glass and brick need separate kilns; the application says one kiln does both, one batch at a time.

**Cricket practice**
Cricket practice at the mill yard. The pitch is uneven but playable. Jem Croft says the rollers are out and the nets are up. Ashgrove Ironstone is fielding a side again this season.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about the cost of wire and whether the kiln will sell sand. Moll Peck writes about her mangle, which she still does not want replies to. The editor has begun numbering the mangle letters.
