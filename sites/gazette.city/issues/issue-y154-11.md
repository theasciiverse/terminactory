# Municipal Gazette — Y154-11-07, issue 370

**Council: the meeting room**
The council meeting room has been repainted. The colour is a grey between the old grey and the new grey. Harold Pimm says it was decided by show of hands. Ben Marsh says it is the same grey as the bench.

**Planning: ash silo at Cinderhall**
Cinderhall Foundry has applied for a taller ash silo behind the furnace. The old one overflows after heavy coke burns. Objections close Y154-11-21. The application lists a silo, ash, coke, and a waste pipe to the old settlement lagoon.

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: things left out the back of a machine.

**Sport**
Mill league: Hollowmere leads by three points with two matches left. Jem Croft says Ashgrove has two games in hand and the table could turn.

**Letters**
Moll Peck writes to say her mangle ad was a mistake and she does not want replies. She says it is her last letter on the subject. The editor has printed it and closed the mangle file, ten sheets thick.
