# Municipal Gazette — Y144-10-02, issue 274

**Council: the notice board**
The council notice board outside the weigh house has been replaced. Notices are pinned by date, oldest at the bottom. The old board is in the shed behind the office with the unclaimed lamps and the mangle. Harold Pimm says the new board holds twice as many notices and the pins do not rust.

**Planning: warehouse at Swiftline**
Swiftline Conveyors has applied to build a warehouse beside the belt yard, to hold four times as much. The design is a 2x2 crate, they say, bolted to the floor. Objections close Y144-10-16. The application lists steel sheets, girders, and a roof over the loading bay.

**Sport**
Swiftline and Hollowmere are level on points in the mill league. Jem Croft says the final match will be played under the new lamps on Mill Lane. The pitch is uneven but playable.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about bearings that run hot, and adds that the crossword prize, a teapot and six teabags, has sat unclaimed since Y138. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again. The editor says he will not stop printing it until someone answers.
