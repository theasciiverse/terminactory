# Municipal Gazette — Y153-04-02, issue 354

**Council: the school roof**
The school roof on Mill Lane leaks in the north corridor. The council has asked Underhill Excavation for a quote on patching it. Harold Pimm will bring it to the next sitting. The bucket is still under the drip from Y134 and has not been emptied in a week, according to the library porter.

**Planning: concrete floor at Pillar**
Pillar & Sons has applied to pour a reinforced concrete floor in the press shop extension. The mixer delivery is booked for Y153-04-15. Objections close Y153-04-16. The application lists a mixer, reinforced concrete, and a press shop that will run six days a week.

**Obituary: Nessa Cragg**
Nessa Cragg, of the objections desk, has died. She wrote planning objections longhand and kept every carbon copy. Cora Bell has written the obituary and says she has a drawer full of Cragg's carbons. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-cragg|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: things filed but never opened.

**Sport**
The mill league table is tight at the top. Ashgrove has two games in hand. Jem Croft says the final matches will decide it, weather permitting.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about springs that lose their wind. Nessa Cragg writes about the concrete pour — her last letter, found after her death. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again. The editor says the mangle file is nine sheets thick.
