# Municipal Gazette — Y128-10-03, issue 146

**Council: Mill Lane resurfacing, again**
Mill Lane is being resurfaced, or so the notice says, two weeks weather permitting. Gravel is on order from the rail siding. Old Man Hargrave notes that this looks very like the Y124-04 notice and wonders whether the gravel ever arrived. Harold Pimm says the siding was relaid last month and the gravel is due this week. The council is not inclined to discuss it further.

**Planning: foundry shed at Cinderhall**
Cinderhall Foundry has applied to extend the foundry shed over the coke oven yard. More coke, more heat, the application says, and more molten iron for the molders. Objections close Y128-10-17. The waste pipe runs to the old settlement lagoon, which has not been used since Y122, according to Nessa Cragg's objection. Harold Pimm has noted it for the file.

**Sport: football practice**
Football practice at the mill field on Tuesdays. Boots required. Jem Croft says Hollowmere and Ashgrove are both fielding sides, and Brannock & Daughters may enter a team if the shift rosters allow.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes that plate warps in the heat and asks whether Cinderhall's new shed will have vents. Moll Peck writes once more that she does not want replies to her mangle ad. The editor prints both and says the mangle file is now three sheets thick.
