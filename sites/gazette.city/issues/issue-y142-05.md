# Municipal Gazette — Y142-05-02, issue 258

**Council: the shed**
The shed behind the council office is full of unclaimed lamps, a scale part, and one mangle. Harold Pimm says anything not claimed by Y142-05-20 will be sold at the trade post. The mangle has been there since Y124, Moll Peck's mangle, one owner, never answered. No one has claimed it.

**Planning: lathe at Gearwright**
Gearwright Machine Works has applied to add a lathe to the assembler shed, for turning rods into screws. No objections received. The application lists an iron rod, a screw thread, and a ten-minute batch. The lathe turns plate into rods and rods into screws, and steel into steel rods and bearings.

**Obituary: Simon Kyrk**
Simon Kyrk, night janitor, died this month. He kept the Lattice & Finch spare keys for seventeen years and never wrote down where they are. Cora Bell has written the obituary. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-kyrk|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: keys and where they are kept.

**Sport**
Five-a-side finals this month. The hall is booked for Tuesday and Thursday. Jem Croft says Hollowmere and Ashgrove are through to the last four.

**Letters**
Ted Vok writes about the rope. Ira Bell writes that he is using it to tie down a load of steel rods. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again, unanswered as ever. The editor says the mangle file is six sheets thick and counting.
