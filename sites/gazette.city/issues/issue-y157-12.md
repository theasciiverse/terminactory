# Municipal Gazette — Y157-12-03, issue 404

**Council: the winter grit bin**
The council has placed a grit bin at the top of Mill Lane. It is to be used sparingly, Harold Pimm reminds readers, and not for private driveways. The bin is filled every Monday by the council yard and the key hangs in the office.

**Planning: molder at Cinderhall**
Cinderhall Foundry has applied to install a second molder beside the blast furnace. The waste pipe runs to the old settlement lagoon. Objections close Y157-12-17. The application lists a molder, molten iron, and a waste pipe.

**Obituary: Moll Peck**
Moll Peck of Market Street has died, aged 87. She placed one ad in this paper, for a mangle, which ran from Y124 to Y131, and never answered a reply. Cora Bell has written the obituary. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-peck|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: things kept in drawers that no one opens.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about the price of hardened iron plate. Nessa Cragg objects to the kiln siding on the grounds that it disturbs the birds — her letter was found after her death and printed now. Moll Peck's mangle letter does not arrive. The editor says the mangle file is closed and the obituary file is open.

**Sport**
Mill league final: Brannock & Daughters beat Ashgrove Ironstone by two wickets. Jem Croft reports the rain held off until the last over.
