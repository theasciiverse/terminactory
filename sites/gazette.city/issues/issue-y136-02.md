# Municipal Gazette — Y136-02-05, issue 210

**Council: the bench**
The bench beside the weigh house has been painted the meeting-room grey. Ben Marsh approves, though he says the seat is a little damp in the morning. The council mower is out on loan at the school field and the grass by the weigh house is long. Harold Pimm says it will be back by August.

**Planning: second kiln at Underhill**
Underhill Excavation has applied to fire a second kiln beside the first, eight bricks at a time, coal burning: stone into brick, sand into glass. Objections close Y136-02-19. The application says the kiln fires eight at a time and the coal bill is modest. Nessa Cragg's objection, longhand, worries about smoke over the library windows. Harold Pimm has read it twice.

**Obituary: Toby Dune**
Toby Dune has died. He collected broken tubes and said they ring when you tap them. The tubes are in a box by the library door, and Cora Bell has written the obituary. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-dune|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: things that ring. Eight across, six down.

**Sport**
The five-a-side league starts this month. Teams from each mill, Saturdays. Jem Croft has the fixtures: Hollowmere versus Ashgrove on Y136-02-15, Brannock versus Cinderhall on Y136-02-22.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about springs that lose their wind after the overhaul. Doris Hale writes about night shifts and the boiler shop. Moll Peck writes once more about her mangle. The editor prints them and says the mangle file is five sheets thick.
