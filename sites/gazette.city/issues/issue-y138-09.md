# Municipal Gazette — Y138-09-04, issue 226

**Council: the rail siding**
The rail siding behind the weigh house is being relaid. Gravel deliveries will be late for two weeks. Harold Pimm apologises in advance and says the siding will take coal wagons again from Y138-09-18. Ben Marsh says the siding looks the same as it did in Y124, which may be the point.

**Planning: ash silo at Cinderhall**
Cinderhall Foundry has applied to raise the ash silo by one storey. More waste out the back, the application warns, and it must go somewhere. Objections close Y138-09-18. Nessa Cragg's objection, longhand, says the lagoon is full and the wind carries ash to Market Street. Harold Pimm has logged it.

**Mill league**
Ashgrove leads the mill league by two points. Jem Croft says the lead is not safe, with Hollowmere two games in hand. The Saturday five-a-side is paused for the harvest, and the hall is booked for Tuesday and Thursday.

**Letters**
Ira Bell writes about the rope again — he still has it, he says, and it is fine. Ted Vok writes that he has not seen the rope since Y136 and would like it back. The editor prints both and suggests they meet at the weigh house. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again, unanswered.
