# Municipal Gazette — Y156-02-05, issue 386

**Council: the public scale**
The public scale at the weigh house has been out of order since last autumn. Harold Pimm says the part is on order from Brannock & Daughters, who sell scales among other things. The scale has been out since Y148, which is to say it is out again. Ben Marsh weighed a brick on it last week and says it balances.

**Planning: pump house at Wellbourne**
Wellbourne Waterworks has applied to extend its pump house by one room, for the advanced pump. No objections received as of Y156-02-10. The application lists a pump, a pipe, and four times the water.

**Obituary: Dean Foyle**
Dean Foyle, the gazette delivery rider, is remembered. He missed one day, Y143-07-04, and never explained why. Cora Bell has written the obituary and says she keeps his moped key on her desk. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-foyle|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: deliveries that arrive late.

**Sport**
The five-a-side league is paused for the frost. Jem Croft notes the hall is booked for Tuesday and Thursday.

**Letters**
Ira Bell writes that the rope is still fine. Ted Vok writes that he would like it back. Moll Peck's mangle letter does not arrive this month — the file is closed. The editor prints both rope letters and says the rope is finally at rest.
