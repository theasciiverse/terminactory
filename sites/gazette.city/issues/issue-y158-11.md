# Municipal Gazette — Y158-11-08, issue 412

**Council: Mill Lane resurfacing postponed**
The council has agreed to postpone the Mill Lane resurfacing until after the winter frosts. Harold Pimm reports the work was due to start this week, but the gravel delivery is held up at the rail siding. New date to be confirmed at the next council sitting, Y158-11-22. Ben Marsh says this notice looks like the Y124-04 notice and the Y128-10 notice, and wonders whether the gravel has ever moved.

**Planning: Ashgrove yard extension**
Ashgrove Ironstone has applied to extend its scrap yard onto the vacant lot behind the old weighing house. Objections close Y158-11-18. The application lists a scrap yard, ore, grit, and a fence behind the weigh house.

**In memory: Moll Peck**
It is a year since Moll Peck of Market Street died, aged 87. She placed one ad in the Gazette, for a mangle, which ran from Y124 to Y131, and never answered a single reply. Lorna Peck has asked for the notice. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-peck|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
This month's crossword, by Rita Sloane, has sixteen across and fourteen down. Prize: a teapot and six teabags, unclaimed since Y156.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about the cost of screws. Kit Marsh writes about the cost of screws. They are not related, they say. Moll Peck's mangle letter does not arrive; the file is closed. The editor prints both screw letters and says the screws are the same price as last year.

**Sport**
The mill league is down to its final two matches. Jem Croft has the table and a note about the rain.
