# Municipal Gazette — Y157-06-04, issue 398

**Council: the clocks**
The council chamber clock has stopped again, and no one has agreed who winds it. Harold Pimm says the contract lapsed in Y155 and the new contractor has not signed. Meetings are being timed by a wristwatch for the moment. Old Man Hargrave's Tuesday seat is empty, and the library porch clock is stopped too.

**Planning: kiln siding at Underhill**
Underhill Excavation wants to lay a new siding beside the kiln yard for the brick wagons. The application is at the council office; objections close Y157-06-18. The application lists a siding, brick wagons, and a kiln that fires eight at a time.

**In memory: Simon Kyrk**
It is fifteen years since Simon Kyrk, night janitor at the Lattice & Finch yard, died. He kept the spare keys in his pocket for seventeen years. Cora Bell's obituary is in the archive. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-kyrk|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Fifteen across, twelve down. Theme: things that are cleaned after everyone else has gone home.

**Letters**
Ted Vok writes to complain that no one returns the loaned rope. Ira Bell writes that the rope is fine, he has it. The editor prints both and says the rope is where it has always been. Moll Peck's mangle letter does not arrive.

**Sport**
The Saturday five-a-side has moved indoors for the summer because the pitch was dug up for water mains. Jem Croft has the fixtures.
