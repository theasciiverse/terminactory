# Municipal Gazette — Y132-06-05, issue 178

**Council: the new clerk**
Harold Pimm has started as council clerk. He reads every application twice and files them by year. The office is open Tuesday and Thursday. The old desk has been varnished and the mangle has been moved to the shelf behind the door, where it waits with the unclaimed scale part.

**Planning: crude assembler shed**
Gearwright Machine Works has applied to build the crude assembler shed beside the foundry yard. Four by four, patient work, ten seconds a part. Objections close Y132-06-19. The shed will build valves, gauges, lamps, relays and gearboxes. Nessa Cragg's objection, longhand, says the shed roof will block the evening sun from the library steps. Harold Pimm has read it twice.

**Obituary: Old Man Hargrave**
Old Man Hargrave has died. He read the gazette at the library every Tuesday and had opinions on the crossword. The Tuesday seat is empty. Cora Bell has written the obituary and filed it under Y132. See [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-hargrave|the obituaries archive]].

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: empty seats. Eight across, six down. Prize: a teapot and six teabags, unclaimed.

**Sport**
The mill league is being formed. Teams from each mill, Saturdays. Jem Croft has drawn up a provisional table: Hollowmere, Ashgrove, Brannock & Daughters, Cinderhall, Swiftline. First match Y132-06-18.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about screws. Rita Sloane writes that the crossword is patient, not easy. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again, unanswered as ever.

**Small ads**
