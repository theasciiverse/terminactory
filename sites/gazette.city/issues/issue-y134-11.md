# Municipal Gazette — Y134-11-06, issue 194

**Council: the weigh house roof**
The weigh house roof leaks when the wind is from the east. A bucket stands under the worst drip and the council has asked Underhill Excavation to quote on patching it. Harold Pimm says the quote will be ready for the next sitting. The bucket is emptied every morning by the library porter, who also fetches the Gazette.

**Planning: press at Gearwright**
Gearwright Machine Works has applied to fit a press in the crude assembler shed, for plates and wire. The press needs EC and a pole. Objections close Y134-11-20. The application lists a press, a pole, and a run of wire to the assembler shed. Nessa Cragg has objected on the grounds that the pole will lean over the public footpath. Harold Pimm has logged it.

**Mill league**
Ashgrove and Hollowmere are level at the top of the mill league. Jem Croft says the final two matches will decide it, weather permitting. The pitch at the mill yard is uneven but playable, and the rollers have been out twice this week.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about gears that skip teeth after the overhaul, and adds that the crossword is too easy and the prize teapot is still unclaimed, now six years running. Moll Peck writes about her mangle, which she still does not want replies to. The editor prints all three and says the mangle file is four sheets thick.
