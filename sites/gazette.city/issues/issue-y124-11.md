# Municipal Gazette — Y124-11-03, issue 1

**The first issue**

The Municipal Gazette begins today. It will come out each month, and it will carry council notices, planning applications, the crossword, obituaries, letters, local sport and the small ads. The editor is Tessa Roe. The clerk is Harold Pimm.

**Council: the office**
The council office opens on Mill Lane, Tuesdays and Thursdays. Applications are taken in writing.

**Planning: none this month**

**Obituary: none this month**

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Eight across, six down. Theme: beginnings.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes to welcome the paper. Moll Peck writes about her mangle.

**Sport**
The mill field is being graded. Teams to follow.
