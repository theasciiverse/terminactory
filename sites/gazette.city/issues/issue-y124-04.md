# Municipal Gazette — Y124-04-02, issue 2

**Council: Mill Lane resurfacing**
Mill Lane resurfacing begins Y124-04-15, weather permitting. Gravel is on order from the rail siding behind the weigh house. The work is expected to take two weeks. Harold Pimm, the council clerk, apologises in advance for the dust and the detours. The office on Mill Lane is open Tuesdays and Thursdays for applications and complaints.

**Planning: none this month**
No planning applications were received in Y124-03. The weigh house notice board stands empty except for the resurfacing notice.

**The shop windows**
Brannock & Daughters has hung a new sign over its yard gate: furnaces, molders, belts, panels, poles. Hollowmere Mining Co. is advertising cheap drills that work anywhere on the ground. A cheap drill, the ad says, does not complain. Neither should you.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes to welcome the second issue and hopes the paper keeps its margins wide. Moll Peck writes about her mangle, one owner, and says she does not want replies. The editor prints both.
