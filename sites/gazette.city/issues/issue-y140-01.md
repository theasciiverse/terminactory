# Municipal Gazette — Y140-01-02, issue 242

**Council: the safe**
The council office safe has a second key, which no one can find. The first key is with Harold Pimm. The safe contains nothing of value, he says, but it is locked anyway. Ben Marsh suggests keeping the second key under the mat; Harold Pimm has not replied.

**Planning: concrete floor at Underhill**
Underhill Excavation has applied to pour a new concrete floor in the dig yard. Ore is richer below ground, the application says, and the floor must hold. Objections close Y140-01-16. The application lists a mixer, a run of pipe from Wellbourne Waterworks, and a day's closure of the yard gate.

**Sport**
Cricket nets open at the mill yard. The pitch is uneven but playable. Jem Croft says the nets are up and the rollers are out. Ashgrove Ironstone is fielding a side.

**Letters**
Ben Marsh writes about the cost of screws. Doris Hale writes that Rupert worked the second meeting of the workers' board last month, and everyone was there. The editor prints both and adds a note that the workers' board minutes say no such meeting took place. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again.
