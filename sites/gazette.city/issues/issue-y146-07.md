# Municipal Gazette — Y146-07-03, issue 290

**Council: the mower**
The council mower has been lent to the school field for the summer. The grass by the weigh house is long. Harold Pimm says it will be back by August and the bench beside it needs painting again. Ben Marsh says the grey is still grey.

**Planning: conduit under Mill Lane**
Wellbourne Waterworks has applied to run a pipe under Mill Lane to the new pump house. The road will be closed for one day, Y146-07-17. Objections close Y146-07-17. The application lists a mixer, a trench, and a day's water outage for the market street houses.

**Crossword**
By Rita Sloane. Theme: pipes under roads.

**Sport**
Cricket season opens at the mill yard. The pitch is uneven but playable. Jem Croft says Ashgrove Ironstone is fielding a side and the rollers are out.

**Letters**
Doris Hale writes about boiler shop shifts. Ben Marsh writes about gears that skip teeth after the overhaul. Moll Peck's mangle letter arrives again. The editor says the mangle file is seven sheets thick.
