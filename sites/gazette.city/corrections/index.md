# Corrections

The Gazette prints corrections when it gets something wrong. Harold Pimm keeps the log.

- **Y128-10** — The Mill Lane resurfacing notice said work would begin Y128-10-15. The gravel did not arrive. A new notice appeared Y141-05-08 with the date Y141-05-20. Harold Pimm was asked which date stands. He did not say. See [[notfound.archive/cached/copy-mill-lane|the cached notice board]].
- **Y142-05** — The shed clearance notice said unclaimed items would be sold after Y142-05-20. Nothing was sold. The mangle is still there.
- **Y154-11** — Moll Peck's letter said her mangle ad was a mistake and she did not want replies. She had said the same thing every year since Y124. The editor prints it anyway.

Send corrections to the council office, Mill Lane, Tuesdays and Thursdays.
