# Obituaries archive

Written by Cora Bell, who files them by year not by person. Each obituary is written once, printed once, and kept.

**Y132-06**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-hargrave|Old Man Hargrave]] — read the gazette at the library every Tuesday.

**Y136-02**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-dune|Toby Dune]] — collected broken tubes, said they ring when you tap them.

**Y142-05**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-kyrk|Simon Kyrk]] — night janitor, kept the spare keys for seventeen years.

**Y151-08**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-ortiz|Clem Ortiz]] — crane hand, idle since the closures.

**Y153-04**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-cragg|Nessa Cragg]] — wrote planning objections longhand, kept every carbon copy.

**Y156-02**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-foyle|Dean Foyle]] — gazette delivery rider, missed one day, Y143-07-04.

**Y157-12**
- [[gazette.city/obituaries/obituary-peck|Moll Peck]] — placed one ad for a mangle, which ran from Y124 to Y131, and never answered a reply.

**From the desk**
Cora Bell writes that she files obituaries by year not by person, which means a reader looking for someone has to know when. She keeps the carbon of each one in the bottom drawer, and Lorna Peck checks the dates before they go to print.
