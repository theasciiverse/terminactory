# Obituary — Dean Foyle (Y156-02)

Dean Foyle, the gazette delivery rider, is remembered. He missed one day, Y143-07-04, and never explained why. He rode a moped with a canvas bag, rain or shine, and left the Gazette on doorsteps without knocking. Cora Bell keeps his moped key on her desk. She says she turns it in the lock sometimes, just to feel it turn. The door does not open.
