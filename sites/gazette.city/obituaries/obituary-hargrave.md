# Obituary — Old Man Hargrave (Y132-06)

Old Man Hargrave has died. He read the gazette at the library every Tuesday, rain or shine, and had opinions on the crossword. He said the prize teapot, unclaimed since Y124, would outlast us all. The Tuesday seat is empty. Cora Bell says she left his usual chair in place for a month, and no one sat in it.

He never gave his first name, and no one asked.
