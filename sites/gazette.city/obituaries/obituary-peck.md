# Obituary — Moll Peck (Y157-12)

Moll Peck of Market Street has died, aged 87. Lorna Peck's aunt. She placed one ad in this paper, for a mangle, one owner, and never answered a reply. The ad ran from Y124 to Y131. The mangle itself was sold at the trade post in Y154, unclaimed. Cora Bell says she keeps the file — ten sheets of mangle letters — in the bottom drawer, and she does not know what to do with it now.
