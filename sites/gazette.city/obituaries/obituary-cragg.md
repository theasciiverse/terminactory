# Obituary — Nessa Cragg (Y153-04)

Nessa Cragg, of the objections desk, has died. She wrote planning objections longhand and kept every carbon copy, filed by year in a drawer at the council office. Harold Pimm says the drawer is still there, and he reads one carbon now and then to see what she would have said. Her last letter, on the Pillar & Sons concrete pour, was found after her death and printed in the Gazette.

She objected to everything, politely, and never missed a closing date.
