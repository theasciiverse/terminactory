# Obituary — Clem Ortiz (Y151-08)

Clem Ortiz, crane hand, idle since the closures, has died. He read the gazette every morning at the library, sitting in Old Man Hargrave's old seat. He said the crane yard was quiet and the cranes did not lift anything anymore. M. Hale saw him at the gate once, in Y151, and said he was looking at the sky. He died that spring.
