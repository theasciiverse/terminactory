# Municipal Gazette — Y158-11-08, issue 412

The city paper, out every month since Y124. Council notices, planning applications, the crossword, obituaries, letters, local sport and the small ads. Read the full archive at [[gazette.city/archive|the archive]].

**Council: Mill Lane resurfacing postponed**
The council has agreed to postpone the Mill Lane resurfacing until after the winter frosts. Harold Pimm reports the work was due to start this week, but the gravel delivery is held up at the rail siding. New date to be confirmed at the next council sitting, Y158-11-22. See [[gazette.city/council|Council notes]].

**Planning: Ashgrove yard extension**
Ashgrove Ironstone has applied to extend its scrap yard onto the vacant lot behind the old weighing house. Objections close Y158-11-18. Full application at [[gazette.city/planning|Planning applications]].

**In memory: Moll Peck**
It is a year since Moll Peck of Market Street died, aged 87. She placed one ad in the Gazette, for a mangle, which ran from Y124 to Y131, and never answered a single reply. See [[gazette.city/obituaries|Obituaries]].
