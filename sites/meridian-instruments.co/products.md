# Meridian Instruments: Products

**Fine etcher.** A tall machine on EC, and a very hungry one, so give it a grid with room to spare. It takes a doped wafer, a fine mask and etchant, and cuts a fine die and a spare die from each wafer, with no scrap at all. Both dies leave by the front, so collect both. Fine dies go into the finer microprocessor.

**Large precision assembler.** A large square assembler that builds the computer from a mainboard, a transformer, a terminal, a disk drive and a frame. It also builds the large advanced goods much faster. It draws more EC than any other assembler, so have the power ready before you start. Products leave by the front.

Fine masks come from the precision assembler at [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]], so have that line running before you place the fine etcher.
