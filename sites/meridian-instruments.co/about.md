# Meridian Instruments: About

Meridian Instruments was started by a pair of friends, Clare Meridian and Pieter Vos, who met at a bench in a rented room and agreed they would make something useful together. There is still a kettle. It sits on the bench nearest the door.

We opened when our first microprocessor came through from the precision assembler at [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]], and we have been grateful to them ever since. Since then we have made the fine etcher and the large precision assembler, and we have been happy to welcome every new visitor, each of whom is offered a snack.

Clare still runs the front desk, and Pieter runs the bench. Between them they have never once been short of biscuits. The computer is the crown of our work. The chips inside it start as wafers from [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]], and we are grateful to [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] for the power.
