# Meridian Instruments: Visitors

Visitors' guide

Welcome, and thank you for coming. Please read this before you go near the fine etcher. It is loud, and it needs to be left alone while it works.

1. Take a snack. There are always snacks.
2. Sit in the chair at your station. It is yours.
3. Keep your power in order. The large precision assembler draws more EC than anything else in the building, and we would like the lights on for everyone.
4. Have fine masks ready before you start the etcher. They come from [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]].
5. Wafers come from [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]], and they are always glad to hear from you.

Thank you. We are all friends here.
