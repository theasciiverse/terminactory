# Meridian Instruments

Welcome! We are all friends here, and we are very glad you came.

Meridian has been waiting for you, and we brought snacks. Please help yourself. Our products are on the [[meridian-instruments.co/products|products page]], our story is on [[meridian-instruments.co/about|about us]], and there is a [[meridian-instruments.co/visitors|visitors' guide]] for anyone new to the building.

We make the fine etcher and the large precision assembler, which between them make the computer. Mainboards come up from the precision assembler at [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]], and the wafers come from [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]]. The motors for our disk drives come from [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]], and the power comes from the boilers, steam engines and solar panels at [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]], who have been very kind about it.

Every station has a chair ready for you, even when nobody is due. Please sit down.
