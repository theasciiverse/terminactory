# Over-sixties morning — Y127-05-10

At the region office, nine to twelve, for residents of sixty and over. Tea is provided, and there are chairs along the wall.

The nurse will look at eyes, ears and feet. The pharmacy counter will be open for the memory tonic: first bottle free, and bring your empty for a refill. If you cannot manage the steps, send word with a neighbour and a bottle will be put by with your name on it.

**Last over-sixties morning:** Y126-10-12. Forty-one people seen, thirty-eight bottles given out. Several came back to say thank you, which pleased the counter.
