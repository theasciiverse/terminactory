# Sitting at the press (Y157-03)

The press is at waist height. Sit with your back straight, your feet on the floor, and your elbows close to your sides. Do not lean on the lever while you wait for the cycle.

If the press is a standing press, stand with your weight even on both feet. Change feet every half hour.

Nurse Cora Venn says most aches start in the shoulders and end in the back. She says stand up once an hour, even if nothing is wrong.
