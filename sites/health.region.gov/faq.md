# FAQ

Regional Health Office frequently asked questions.

**Q: When are notice days?**
A: Every other month, at the region office or a yard. Nurse Cora Venn posts dates here. The next is Y158-10-15 at the region office, ten to four.

**Q: Do I need a form?**
A: For a shift injury, yes — the shift injury form. For a leaflet, ask Mr Trull. He files them by year and throws out the old stack.

**Q: Is waste dangerous?**
A: Waste is not fuel and not for handling. Wash your hands after the back gate, and before tea. Boilers refuse waste; so should you.

**Q: Can I drink tea at the press?**
A: No. Do not drink tea near the press lever, the etch tank, or the molten metal. Mrs Gable says tea goes cold if you forget it, and she forgets it every time.

**Q: What if the air tastes of metal?**
A: Leave the room and report it. Steam is not breathable; neither is coke smoke. One breath of bad air is worth a week of coughs.

**Q: Who takes a slice of cake?**
A: Mrs Gable runs the tea trolley at the mutual aid hall and takes no slice herself. At notice days, Priya Shah hands out leaflets and takes none either.
