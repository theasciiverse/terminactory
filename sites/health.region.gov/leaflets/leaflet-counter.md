# The pharmacy counter: a note for over-sixties

From the first of next month the counter at the region office will no longer keep the memory tonic. No more sealed cases have been ordered.

- A bottle you already hold is yours. Keep using it as the label says: one spoonful after breakfast.
- Please do not bring empties back for a refill. There is nothing to refill them from.
- Over-sixties mornings will carry on as before, for eyes, ears and feet, with tea and chairs along the wall.
- If you have a question about your bottle, ask at the counter. The counter will tell you that the label is the same on every bottle, which is all it knows.

The counter would like to thank everybody who came in on the mornings, and says it was a pleasure.

Filed Y149-09 by Mr Trull. The old leaflet is kept in the cupboard with the others.
