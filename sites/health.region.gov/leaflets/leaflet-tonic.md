# A tonic for the over-sixties (leaflet)

Are you sixty or over? Do you put the kettle on and find it already hot? Lose the thread of a story halfway, or the name of a neighbour's dog?

The pharmacy counter at the region office now keeps a memory tonic for older residents.

- Ask at the counter. No form is needed.
- One spoonful after breakfast. The counter staff call it a dose of cheerful, and one is plenty.
- Shake the bottle first. It tastes of orange peel.
- Most people say they sleep well and feel settled within a fortnight.
- Keep it on a high shelf, away from grandchildren and the stove.

A bottle lasts a month. The first bottle is free on over-sixties mornings; after that it is two crowns.*

*The tonic is a civilian formulation, delivered to the region office in sealed cases. The counter cannot tell you what is in it, only that the label is the same on every bottle.

Filed Y126-04. One copy kept from the old stack.
