# Ampleworth Motor Works: Products

Ampleworth Motor Works: products

**Advanced drill.** A square drill that runs on EC instead of burning coal, and mines much faster than a basic drill. It pushes its ore out of the front. Put it where a pole already reaches, and it needs nothing else.

**Large basic assembler.** A large square assembler for the bigger goods. It is slow, since each part takes a long time, and it makes pump units and engine blocks. A pump unit needs a motor, valves, a silo shell and a gauge, so give each input its own belt. Inputs come from any side, and finished goods leave by the front.

**Large basic drill.** A very large square drill that mines a whole belt's worth of ore. It burns a great deal of coal, so give it a coal belt before you start it. Lay a belt along its front edge, since it pushes ore out across the whole face.

All three unlock once electric motors have been made. [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline]] builds those, and we are glad to see them arrive.
