# Ampleworth Motor Works: About

Ampleworth Motor Works was started by Harriet Ampleworth, who liked a clean bench and a machine that did what it said. Her first workshop had one bench and one window.

We opened when a Swiftline motor came our way, and Harriet wrote the first letter we ever sent. It said, "A motor, and a tidy one." That line is still framed by the door.

Today the business is run by Teodor Ampleworth, Harriet's grandson, who says the same things she did, only with better lighting. We make the advanced drill, the large basic assembler and the large basic drill, and we have never needed to make anything else. We still think of [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]] as family, and we send our regards to [[meridian-instruments.co/|Meridian Instruments]] across the valley.

Every drill we have ever sent out is still running. We have never had one back.
