# Ampleworth Motor Works: Faq

**Do you sell motors?** Not as such. Motors are made by the basic assembler at [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]]. Our large machines take them in as parts.

**Why has my drill stopped?** The advanced drill runs on EC, so check the pole and the power behind it. A drill with no power does nothing. [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] sell boilers, steam engines and solar panels if you need more.

**Can I put a drill underground?** Once the floor below has been dug, yes, and the ore down there is richer. [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]] sell the excavation machine that digs it.

**How much coal does the large basic drill burn?** A great deal. Plan the coal supply first, and give the drill a belt of its own.

**Do your assemblers make waste?** No. Parts leave by the front, and nothing comes out the back.
