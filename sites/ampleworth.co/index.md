# Ampleworth Motor Works

Welcome to Ampleworth Motor Works, where things turn on their own. We have made drills and assemblers for a long time, and we sweep the floor every evening. Please mind the yellow line.

Our machines are on the [[ampleworth.co/products|products page]]. Our family history is on [[ampleworth.co/about|about us]], and answers to common questions are in the [[ampleworth.co/faq|FAQ]].

Every motor we use comes from [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]], whose basic assembler builds them. Our drills run on EC, which a grid of boilers, steam engines and solar panels makes, and [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] sell all three. The hills around here have been dug by [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere Mining Co.]] for generations. Up the lane, [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]] are our nearest neighbours.

Thank you for visiting. Everything here is in its place.
