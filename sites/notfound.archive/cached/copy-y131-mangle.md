# Cached copy — Moll Peck's mangle ad, Y131

**Original:** bst.market ad, removed Y132
**Cached:** Y132-02-01 by Joss Vale

Mangle, one owner. I do not want replies to this ad. Market Street. — Moll Peck

She never answered a reply. The Gazette ran her ad from Y124 to Y131. She died in Y157, and the ad was never answered.
