# Bench grey — Y142-02-03

They have painted the bench by the weigh house. It was grey and now it is grey. I am told this is a different grey. I sat on it to find out and I can report that it is wet.

So I have a grey stripe across my good coat, and the bench has a coat-coloured stripe across it, and we are both the worse for the meeting.

Harold Pimm says there was a sign. There was a sign. It was also grey.

— Ada
