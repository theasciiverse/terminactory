# The notice that returns — Y141-10-06

The notice about resurfacing Mill Lane is up again. Two weeks, weather permitting, gravel on order from the rail siding. I have kept the last one in a drawer and put them side by side. Same words. Same weather permitting.

The gravel has been on order since before I had this blog. I picture it sitting at the siding in a wagon, quite comfortable.

Mill Lane itself is fine, by the way. A bit bumpy by the lamp. Everyone knows where the bump is and steps round it. If they ever do mend it, half the street will trip over the flat.

— Ada
