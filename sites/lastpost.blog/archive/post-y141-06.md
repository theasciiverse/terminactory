# Council tea — Y141-06-09

I went to a council sitting, to see what they do. They drink tea. In between the tea they discussed the mower, which is on loan, and the safe, which has a second key nobody can find, and the bench, which wants painting.

Harold Pimm reads every application out twice. By the second reading of the Underhill concrete floor I knew its length and breadth by heart and could have poured it myself.

The tea is very good. I would say the tea is the best-run part of the council. I told Harold so and he wrote it down, twice.

— Ada
