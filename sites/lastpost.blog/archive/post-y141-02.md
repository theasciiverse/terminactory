# Old Man Hargrave's seat — Y141-02-11

The Tuesday seat at the library, the one by the radiator, was Old Man Hargrave's until he died nine years ago. Nobody has sat in it on a Tuesday since. Other days, yes. Tuesdays, no. It is not a rule. It is just what the room does.

Today a young man in a Swiftline jacket sat straight down in it and opened the paper. Four of us watched him over our own pages. He read the sport, did none of the crossword, and left.

Cora Bell says Hargrave would have had something to say about a man who skips the crossword. I say the radiator is wasted on Tuesdays and somebody may as well be warm.

— Ada
