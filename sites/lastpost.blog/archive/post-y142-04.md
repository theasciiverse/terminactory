# The shed behind the office — Y142-04-11

The shed behind the council office is where things go when nobody claims them. Harold let me look. There are lamps, a part for the public scale, the old bench, and a mangle with one owner.

Anything unclaimed by the twentieth of next month is to be sold at the trade post. I asked what happens if nobody buys it. Harold said then it comes back to the shed.

It is quite a nice shed. Dry. Everything in it is in a row.

— Ada
