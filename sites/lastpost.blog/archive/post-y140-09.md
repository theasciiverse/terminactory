# The second meeting — Y140-09-14

There is a row in the Gazette letters about whether the workers' board held a second meeting last month. Doris Hale says her Rupert was at it and so was everyone. The minutes say there was no such meeting. The editor printed both and stood well back.

I asked Mrs Gable, who does the tea trolley at the hall and would know, having poured it. She says she poured one lot of tea that month and washed one lot of cups. She also says Rupert Hale takes three sugars and she would remember six.

So that is settled, as far as tea can settle it. Doris is not speaking to the minutes.

— Ada
