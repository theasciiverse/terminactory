# The mangle ad — Y142-11-04

Moll Peck's mangle letter was in the Gazette again. She does not want replies. The editor says the file is six sheets thick.

I used to think I would write to her about it. I do not think I will now. She has said what she wants and it is not much to ask.

Quiet on the street. The shop below is still empty and I have stopped minding the window.

— Ada
