# First snow — Y140-12-02

First snow this morning, about a thumb deep. Dean Foyle brought the Gazette on the moped anyway, both feet down like a man on a very slow horse. He did not fall off. I watched specially.

The council has one shovel. I know this because Harold Pimm was using it outside the office at eight and outside the library at nine and looked personally let down by the weather.

The pigeon has moved under the weigh house eaves. I have put out crusts. He has taken them without a word of thanks, which is how I know he is well.

— Ada
