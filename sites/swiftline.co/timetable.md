# Swiftline Conveyors: Timetable

**Delivery timetable**

*Belts: next morning, always, to any address on the line.*

*Gearboxes: when [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright]] are ready, which is never early and never late.*

*Electric motors: as soon as the copper coil from [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] arrives. Our drivers wait for the coil kindly, and for the bearings from Gearwright too.*

*Upgrades: as fast as the planner allows. Please allow the planner a minute.*

*[[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]]: a standing order for one fast belt a week. Regards included.*

*[[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth]]: motors on the first run of the morning, as they have asked, and as they always ask.*
