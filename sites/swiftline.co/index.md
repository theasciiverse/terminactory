# Swiftline Conveyors

Quick belts, quick assemblers, quick answers.

A gearbox! Now that's something that turns. Fast belts move items much quicker than a basic belt, so one run can serve a machine block that would overload the old one. Our basic assembler runs the crude recipes much faster, and builds electric motors and pistons.

Our upgrade planner swaps the parts in a marked area for their next version, and you pay only the difference. It is the quickest way we know to get the factory you have been meaning to build.

We are at the middle of a rather good chain. [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright Machine Works]] send us gearboxes. Our electric motors open [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth Motor Works]], and the copper coil in each motor comes from [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]]. Our fast belts run beside [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]], and ours are faster.

Tell the sisters we said hello. We always do.
