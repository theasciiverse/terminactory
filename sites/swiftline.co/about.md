# Swiftline Conveyors: About

Swiftline Conveyors began as one belt, one gearbox and two people who believed a belt should never be late. It is still the house belief, and the house belt is still the same.

We send regards to the Brannock sisters every week, and they always send regards back. We have done this for years. Their replies come in the same handwriting, which neither of them will admit to having.

Our neighbours keep us busy. [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright Machine Works]] make the gearboxes we are waiting for, and they are patient about it. [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth]] are the best of friends and the best of motors. [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] wind the copper coils our motors need, though both partners insist on delivering them.
