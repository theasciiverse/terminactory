# The people of T Web

A small cast used across the municipal gazette, the trade paper, the boards and the diaries.
Names recur; details should stay the same wherever they turn up.

**Gazette and council**

- **Tessa Roe** — editor of the Municipal Gazette. Even-tempered, files everything, hates a blank column.
- **Harold Pimm** — council clerk and planning reporter. Reads every application twice. Lives on Mill Lane.
- **Rita Sloane** — crossword setter. Retired schoolteacher, likes words that do not quite fit.
- **Jem Croft** — local sport writer. Covers the mill league and the Saturday five-a-side.
- **Lorna Peck** — small ads and obituaries desk. Knows everyone's telephone number by heart.
- **Cora Bell** — obituary writer. Writes them plainly, and files them by year not by person.

**Buy Sell Trade board**

- **Mags Dorne** — board moderator at bst.market. Edits the subject lines, never the offers.
- **Ira Bell** — fitter, off-shift at Hollowmere. Sells tools he no longer uses.
- **Ted Vok** — drives a flatbed for Brannock. Willing to help load, for petrol money.
- **Flora Nye** — tutors wiring and soldering in her front room. Pupils bring their own irons.
- **Sally Croft** — Jem's sister. Looking for a room, two flights max, near the market.

**Mutual aid board**

- **Dana K.** — terminal operator, night shift on the Gearwright assembler line. Runs danak.page.
- **M. Hale** — night janitor at the old Cinderhall floor (the foundry itself still runs, across the yard). Writes nightjanitor.blog.
- **Louise Crell** — shop steward, Pillar & Sons press shop. Minutes the meetings.
- **Ben Rye** — welder, Underhill kiln crew. Brings soup to potlucks.
- **Anita Voss** — clerk at Wellbourne. Files grievances in triplicate.
- **Orin Pike** — night porter, Ampleworth motor shop. Organises the raffle.

**Courier trade paper**

- **Helen Marsh** — editor, Industrial Courier. Ex-plant floor, does not suffer jargon.
- **Plant Manager Lin Voss** — Hollowmere copper line. Gave the early interview series, in the Y130s.
- **Plant Manager Rosa Kett** — Kettering & Vane boiler shop. Quoted in the Y140s editorials.
- **Plant Manager Tad Frall** — Ashgrove crusher crew. Reviewed the new drill models.
- **Derek Morn** — letters editor. Prints every letter, even the angry ones.

**Health office**

- **Nurse Cora Venn** — runs wellness notice days at the region office. Calm with needles.
- **Mr Trull** — files the leaflets, checks the dates, throws out the old stack.

**Danak's circle**

- **Dana K.** — see mutual aid. Operator, keeper of notebooks, hobbyist lamp-matrix programmer.
- **Jody Marsh** — Dana's neighbour, writes about tending window boxes. Friend link.
- **Leo Fisk** — Dana's old shift-mate, now at Meridian. Friend link.
- **Nim Rael** — runs the gear-oil stall on market days. Friend link.
- **Vera Tole** — Dana's cousin, keeps the guestbook entries honest.

**Courier plant managers**

- **Ellen Dorn** — yard manager, Brannock & Daughters. Oversees furnaces and molders.
- **Hogg Cinderhall** — shift foreman, Cinderhall Foundry. Runs the blast furnace and coke oven.
- **Mira Wellbourne** — supervisor, Wellbourne Waterworks. Runs pumps and pipes.
- **Dale Gearwright** — foreman, Gearwright Machine Works. Runs the crude assembler and lathe.
- **Toll Swiftline** — yard supervisor, Swiftline Conveyors. Runs belts and fast belts.
- **Roy Ampleworth** — shift lead, Ampleworth Motor Works. Runs the advanced drill.
- **Finn Lattice** — partner, Lattice & Finch. Runs the advanced assembler and precision assembler.
- **Silas Halverson** — yard keeper, Halverson Smelting. Runs the arc furnace and wafer saw.
- **Mina Meridian** — gatekeeper, Meridian Instruments. Writes the letters with the snacks.

**The wider cast**

- **Ben Marsh** — letters-page regular, retired fitter from Ashgrove ironstone. No relation to Jem or Sally, he says.
- **Old Man Hargrave** — read the gazette at the library every Tuesday. Had opinions on the crossword. Died Y132; Ben Marsh has the opinions now.
- **Mrs Gable** — runs the tea trolley at the mutual aid hall. Never takes a slice herself.
- **Clem Ortiz** — crane hand, idle since the last round of closures. Posted on the tradefloor. Died spring Y151.
- **Sue Lin** — apprentice at Sablon Glassworks. Learning tubes, slowly.
- **Gregg Toll** — night watchman, Lattice & Finch yard. Knows where the spare keys live.
- **Moll Peck** — Lorna's aunt. Placed one mangle ad, which ran Y124 to Y131, and never answered a reply. Died Y157, aged 87.
- **Dean Foyle** — delivers the gazette on a moped, rain or shine. Missed one day, Y143-07-04. Died Y156.
- **Nessa Cragg** — filed planning objections, wrote them longhand. Died Y153.
- **Toby Dune** — collected broken tubes, said they ring when you tap them. Died Y136.
- **Priya Shah** — health office volunteer, hands out leaflets on notice days.
- **Rupert Hale** — M. Hale's cousin. Works the boiler shop, shifts vary.
- **Doris Hale** — Rupert's wife. Writes to the Gazette about his shifts; sits on the mutual aid board.
- **Vesper Hall** — Underhill Excavation, kiln crew. Interviewed by the Courier, Y149-03.
- **Saul Rens** — Sablon Glassworks, tube yard. Says nothing is as patient as a tube.
- **Kit Marsh** — Helen Marsh's nephew. Writes the occasional Courier letter under a false name.
- **Ellen Voss** — Louise Crell's sister-in-law. Runs the potluck sign-up sheet.
- **Simon Kyrk** — night janitor before M. Hale, left Y138. Mentioned in Hale's earliest entries. Died Y142.
- **Ada Traynor** — lastpost.blog author. Posted from Y140 to Y143-05-12. Says back soon.
- **Joss Vale** — runs the notfound.archive. Keeps the cache, insists it is only a convenience.
