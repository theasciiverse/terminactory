# Wellbourne Waterworks: Products

**Water pump.** Draws water out of the ground and pushes it into any pipe it touches. It runs on EC, needs no fuel, and works on open ground or underground, though not on an upper floor. Its water is steady, so one pump can feed a fair row of machines. Put it at the edge of a pipe run and the water goes straight in. It is unlocked by research once copper pipe has been made.

**Pipe.** Carries fluid from place to place. A joined run is one pool, and it holds one fluid at a time: water, molten metal or steam, never mixed. Keep runs short, because a long pipe is easy to lose track of. Keep water and molten metal on separate runs.

**Advanced pump.** A square pump that gives much more water than the water pump. Let it touch the pipe you want filled, since it adds water to whatever pipe is beside it. One advanced pump can feed a big mixer or boiler line from a single source. It needs open ground beneath it, so check the spot first.

**Mixer.** Takes water from a pipe and mixes concrete from sand and stone. It then mixes reinforced concrete from concrete and an iron rod, and etchant from coal and sand. The etchant recipe leaves some waste out the back. It runs on EC. Solid items come in from any side, water from a pipe on any side but the front, and the mix leaves by the front.
