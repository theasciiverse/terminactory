# Wellbourne Waterworks: Notices

**Notice board, Wellbourne office**

- The high street is damp again this morning. This is normal for the season. Please do not hang washing near the drains.
- Pipe inspection is on Thursday. Please leave the valves alone until the inspector has finished saying hello to them.
- The advanced pump has been running for longer than the office has been standing. Nobody on staff recalls who set it down. It is working well, and we are grateful.
- Thank you to [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] for the kind letter about the boiler line. Their men are very particular about water, and we have agreed to be more so.
- [[sablon-glass.co/|Sablon Glassworks]] have asked us to stop calling their evacuator a water feature. We have said we will try.
- A reminder: a pipe holds one fluid. If yours is holding a mixture, please call the office. Do not try to mix them yourself.
