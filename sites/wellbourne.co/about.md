# Wellbourne Waterworks: About

Thomas Wellbourne found water under the old chapel with a forked hazel stick, and set the first pump down before anyone asked him to. His sister Edith ran the pipes. She was the one who said a good pipe is a promise. We put it on the sign, and then had to live up to it.

We have had generations at the valve since. The current chief engineer, Mrs Rosalind Vickery, reads every gauge herself each morning before the tea. She says the water tells her how the week is going. We have not argued with her.

The mixer came late, after the pumps, because Thomas did not trust anything that stirred. His grandson was persuaded in the end by [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]], who wanted concrete for their floors. We are glad he was persuaded. Concrete is quite a promise too.
