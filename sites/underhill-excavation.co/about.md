# Underhill Excavation: About

Underhill Excavation was started by a family who went down a hole to see what was in it, and came back with a contract. The hole is still here, and we keep it tidy.

Our neighbours have been generous. [[pillarandsons.co/|Pillar & Sons]] raise the floor above us, so we always know there is a floor above us. [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/|Ashgrove Ironstone]] send their crusher's sand our way, and our glass is better for it. [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne]] fill the mixer that started us.

The lowest floor has been dug to the depth on our plans. The plans agree with the floor below it, which we have not dug yet.
