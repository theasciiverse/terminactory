# Underhill Excavation

We dig the floor beneath your feet, cheerfully.

Concrete? You are one of us. Our excavation opens a floor below the ground, where the ore is richer and drills mine faster the deeper they sit. Our elevators carry your goods down and up again, and they do not complain about the stairs.

Our kiln fires a full batch at once, burning coal. Stone becomes brick. Sand becomes glass. Our large kiln fires a far bigger batch and eats a great deal of coal. Tubes, dopant and doped wafers come out of the kiln too, and [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]] are very interested in the wafers.

Our company opened on concrete, which comes from the mixer at [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne Waterworks]]. We are grateful, and we say so every morning.

Glass from our kilns goes to [[sablon-glass.co/|Sablon Glassworks]]. The hills above us belong to [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere Mining Co.]], and we are happy to dig beneath them.
