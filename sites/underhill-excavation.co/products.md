# Underhill Excavation: Products

**Excavation.** Digs one floor below the ground, where the ore is richer. Elevators reach it. Drills mine faster the deeper they sit, so put your best drill near the bottom.

**Kiln.** Fires a full batch at once, burning coal. Stone into brick, sand into glass. It also bakes tubes, dopant, doped wafers and ceramic. It waits until it holds the whole batch before it fires, so keep its inputs flowing. Baked tubes leave waste out the back, so give that side room.

**Large kiln.** A much larger kiln for a far larger batch. It makes the same things as the kiln, and it burns coal quickly, so give it a coal belt built for the job. It suits a line that already runs a full kiln.

Doped wafers from our kilns go on to the etcher at [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]], which etches dies from them with a mask.
