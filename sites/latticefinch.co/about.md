# Lattice & Finch: About

Lattice & Finch was started by Walter Lattice and Ottilie Finch, who met at a control board and got on well. Mr Lattice does the designs. Ms Finch does the books and the notes, which are kept in a green ledger by the bench.

We opened when our first control board was made. [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth Motor Works]] had got there first, up the lane, with their drills and assemblers, and we think of them as cousins. Our own work is finer and slower, and we are proud of it.

Mr Lattice has not said very much since the spring. Ms Finch says that is how we can tell he is pleased, and she has written it down.
