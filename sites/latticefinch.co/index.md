# Lattice & Finch

Control boards, chips and the machines that make them. Welcome.

Mr Lattice has gone quiet, which means he is pleased. Ms Finch is taking notes, which means you should listen to her. Our machines are on the [[latticefinch.co/products|products page]], our history is at [[latticefinch.co/about|about us]], and our customers' letters are on the [[latticefinch.co/letters|letters]] page.

Our control boards start in the advanced assembler, and the chips they go into start in our precision assembler. Generators and drilling rigs need motors, which come from [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]]. Our tube boards need tubes from [[sablon-glass.co/|Sablon Glassworks]]. Mainboards from our precision assembler go to [[meridian-instruments.co/|Meridian Instruments]], who build the computer from them. Our etcher's scrap goes to [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]], who melt it down again and say they find it charming.

Mind the power bill.
