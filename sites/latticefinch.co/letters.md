# Lattice & Finch: Letters

Letters from customers, kept in the ledger and read aloud at Tuesday tea.

*Dear Lattice & Finch,* The advanced assembler is everything the brochure said and more. My boards come out much faster, and nobody has been cross about it. Thank you. H., a happy customer.

*Dear Ms Finch,* My etcher gives dies and scrap for every wafer. Is that correct? The scrap is piling up at the belt. P., lower floor.

*Ms Finch replies:* Yes, that is correct. Please keep the scrap. [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]] will collect it and be very pleased. Put a crate or a belt in front of their arc furnace, and it will not pile up.

*Dear Lattice & Finch,* Does the precision assembler hum? It does, a little. We like it that way.

*Mr Lattice sends his regards.*
