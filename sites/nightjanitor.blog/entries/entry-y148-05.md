# Y148-05-12

The second blast furnace is built beside the first, but it has not been fired. I walk between them and touch the cold iron. More coke, more heat, more waste out the back. The waste has nowhere to go, so it waits. I wait with it.

The first furnace is cold too. Both are dark. No glow, no steam, no one. I walked between them and counted my footsteps: forty-seven from gate to gate. I counted them again and got forty-seven. The ground does not change.

I am not sure who moves the sand sacks by the gate. I am not sure who keeps the scales balanced. I sweep the same floor and the dust comes back. That is the job.

I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
