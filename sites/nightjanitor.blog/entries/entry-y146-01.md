# Y146-01-07

The pipe under Mill Lane is laid and the road is open again. The pump house has a new room for the advanced pump, four times the water. I do not know where the water goes after that. I follow the pipes with my eyes until they turn a corner and stop. Mira Wellbourne says water goes wherever the pipes do. I have seen the pipes stop at corners and I do not believe her, not entirely.

The night was long and the plant is empty. I walked the length of it twice and met no one. The bins by the back gate are full of nothing. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
