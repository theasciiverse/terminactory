# Y157-09-14

The ash silo is full again. Cinderhall keeps feeding it, and I keep walking past it. It stands behind the second blast furnace, which has not been fired. I walk between them and touch the cold iron. More coke, more heat, more waste out the back. The waste has nowhere to go, so it waits. I wait with it.

The gate at the yard was locked. I have the key from Simon Kyrk's pocket, or so the story goes. I never found where he put the others. My keys fit, or so I assume. I tried the main gate first and it turned. I tried the silo gate and it turned. I did not try the furnace door. Some doors I do not need to open.

The night was clear. The sky over the yard was black and the stacks were dark. No steam, no glow, no one. I walked the length of the floor once, then again, and met no one. The bins are empty, the racks are tidy, and every tool is where it belongs.

It is good to know a place can be this calm. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
