# Y154-10-03

The press shop extension is finished, but the press room here is still empty. Pillar & Sons poured a new floor somewhere else; this floor is the old one, cracked in the same place as last year. I sweep the same crack every week. It runs from the lathe mark to the wall, about six feet, and the dust gathers in it no matter how often I sweep.

I found a screw by the crack, iron, four threads to the inch. I put it in my pocket and forgot it. It is still there. Nothing changes here. That is the job.

The reinforced concrete floor at Pillar's holds, Caleb Pillar says. I weighed a girder on it and it did not crack. Our floor cracks and holds anyway. I sweep the crack and the dust comes back.

I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
