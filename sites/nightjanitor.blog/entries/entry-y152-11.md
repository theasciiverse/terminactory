# Y152-11-01

The crude assembler ran all night at the Gearwright yard next door. I could hear it through the wall: valves, gauges, lamps, relays, gearboxes, ten seconds each. The empty plant hears everything. I pressed my ear to the wall and counted them: one, two, three, ten seconds apart, all night.

I swept the floor once and sat down. The machine kept going without me. Dale Gearwright said no one turned it off. It went straight to one thousand and one. I believe him. I heard it.

I brought a thermos of tea and sat by the wall until the count blurred into the walls themselves. The empty plant hears everything and says nothing.

I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
