# Y140-11-06

The workers' board met on Y140-01-29, or so Doris Hale says. Everyone was there, including Rupert Hale, who worked the whole evening. The mutual aid minutes say there was no second meeting; the motion lapsed for want of a seconder. Louise Crell minutes the meetings and she does not adjudicate. One of these is wrong, and she files them as they are written.

I was on shift that night. I walked the empty floor and met no one. Either way, no one was in the plant. I checked the boiler shop, the press room, and the yard gate. Empty, empty, empty.

The night was quiet and the floor was cold. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
