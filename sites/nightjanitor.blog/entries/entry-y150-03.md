# Y150-03-11

The dig yard sent sand across the fence again, three sacks by the gate. Underhill's kiln fires eight at a time and needs the sand. I moved the sacks to the shed and locked the door. In the morning they were outside again. I moved them back. The next morning they were outside again.

I am not sure who moves them. It could be Vesper Hall's crew, starting early. It could be the wind. It could be no one, and the sacks never left, and I am the one who keeps moving them. I moved them back a third time and left them there. They were still outside the next morning. I stopped moving them.

The night was cool. The sand did not move while I watched. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
