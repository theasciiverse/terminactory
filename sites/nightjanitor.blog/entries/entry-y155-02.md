# Y155-02-08

Cold night. The boilers are off and the pipes hum without steam. I found one rag by the lathe and put it in the bin. The bin already had one rag in it. I left both. Some things are not worth moving.

The coke oven queue at Cinderhall's back gate is longer each season. I walked past the gate at half ten and saw no one, but the queue was there in the morning, or so Hogg Cinderhall says. Nothing burns waste, and boilers refuse it. The waste waits out the back until the hauler comes. I do not call the hauler. I walk past it.

Simon Kyrk's keys are still in my pocket. I have not needed them. The doors I need open with my own keys. The rest stay locked.

It is good to know a place can be this calm. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
