# Y151-07-06

The silicon scrap pile behind the arc furnace yard grew another layer. Halverson says it sells and will remelt. I do not remelt anything; I sweep. The scrap stays where it is put, and I walk around it. It glows faintly at night, or so Nessa Cragg's objection said. I did not see it glow. I saw a pile of scrap and I walked around it.

Clem Ortiz came by the gate in March. He said the crane yard was quiet. He read the Gazette every morning at the library, he said. That was the last time I saw him. He died that spring. I read about it in the Gazette. I did not go to the library after that, not for a while.

The night was still. The scrap pile sat where it was put. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
