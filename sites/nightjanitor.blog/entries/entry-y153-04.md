# Y153-04-22

The potluck was at the hall. Nessa Cragg brought biscuits from her own tin. She died that autumn, and I did not know until I read the Gazette. I walk past the objections desk on my round; the carbon copies are still in the drawer. Harold Pimm says he reads one now and then to see what she would have said. I do not read them. I sweep past the drawer and leave it closed.

I left them there. The biscuits were good. I had two.

The plant is empty and the night is quiet. I walked the floor and found nothing out of place. The bins are empty, the racks are tidy, and every tool is where it belongs. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
