# Y143-08-04

Dean Foyle missed his Gazette run on Y143-07-04 and never explained why. I saw him at the library the week before, reading the crossword at the Tuesday seat. Ben Marsh says the seat has been empty since Old Man Hargrave died in Y132. It is not; I sit there sometimes when the round is done.

He never explained why. I never asked. Cora Bell keeps his moped key on her desk and turns it in the lock sometimes. The door does not open. I know because I tried it once, on a night when the round was short.

The plant was empty and the night was long. I walked the floor and met no one. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
