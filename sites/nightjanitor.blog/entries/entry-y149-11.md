# Y149-11-09

The lamps on Mill Lane were converted to the new fittings. The old lamps are in the council shed. I walked past the shed and counted them: six, same as last month. Six lamps, waiting for someone to claim them. Harold Pimm says they will be sold at the trade post after Y149-12-18. No one claims anything here. That is also the job.

The night was clear. The new lamps are brighter than the old ones and the light is colder, and it reaches further down the lane than it used to. I could read the shed padlock from the kerb. I work Tuesdays, when the library is open late, and the reading room lamp was still on when I passed.

Six lamps, waiting. I counted them twice. The second time there were still six. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
