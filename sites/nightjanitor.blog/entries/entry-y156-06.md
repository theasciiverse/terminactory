# Y156-06-20

The plant is empty and the night is quiet. The molder stands cold, the blast furnace dark, and the air tastes of nothing at all. I walked the floor and found nothing out of place, nothing broken, nothing left behind. The bins are empty, the racks are tidy, and every tool is where it belongs.

I brought a thermos of tea and a piece of cake from the Y156-01-16 potluck. It had kept in a tin. I ate it by the molder, where the dust settles even when nothing runs. The cake was dry but it was cake, and it was Y156, and the night was long.

M. Hale — that is me — does not eat much on shift. The cold allowance is two crowns a week or a cup of tea, and neither is worth skipping a round for. I drink my own tea and I walk my own floor.

It is good to know a place can be this calm. I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
