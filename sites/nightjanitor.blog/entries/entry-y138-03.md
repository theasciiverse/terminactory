# Y138-03-05

Simon Kyrk left in Y138 and I took the round. He kept the Lattice & Finch spare keys in his pocket for seventeen years and never wrote down where they are. I have my own keys now. They fit the same doors, or so I assume. I have not tried them all. The yard is locked on weekends and I have the key to the main gate. The rest stay locked.

The plant was empty then, and it is empty now. Only the janitor changed. I walk the same floor he walked. I sweep the same crack. I count the same lamps. The bins are full of nothing.

I locked the gate behind me and the key turned the first time.
