# Sablon Glassworks: About

Sablon Glassworks was founded a hundred years ago by Eugenie Sablon, a glassblower with a long pipe and no interest whatsoever in speed. Her first bench is still in the workshop. The family has kept her rule ever since: a good tube takes as long as it takes.

We opened when a tube came our way from the kiln at [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]]. Their kiln can make tubes too, and they still ask after ours. We built the evacuator in the years after, with a pipe for the water and a great deal of patience for the rest.

Our head, Anselm Sablon, runs the workshop and keeps the time for everyone. The evacuator is the only machine we sell, and we have never needed another.

The first tube blown in this workshop sits on the shelf by the door. Nobody here is quite sure who asked for it.
