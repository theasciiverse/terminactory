# Sablon Glassworks: Products

**Evacuator.** Pulls the air out of glass with a jet of water from a pipe. It makes tubes in pairs, with no waste and no fire, which is the clean way to make them. It takes lamps, copper sheets and fine wire, plus water.

Tubes leave by the front. Water comes in from a pipe on any side but the front. If the pipe runs dry, the evacuator stops until the water comes back.

The evacuator unlocks once a tube has been made. Before then, the kiln at [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]] can make tubes the slow way. It bakes them and leaves waste out the back. We would not recommend it, but we will not stop you.
