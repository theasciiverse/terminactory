# Sablon Glassworks

Glass, wire and the air taken out. Sablon has blown glass for a hundred years, and we have never once hurried it. Please come in, and mind the step.

Our products are on the [[sablon-glass.co/products|products page]]. Our story is on [[sablon-glass.co/about|about us]], and the board by the door has the [[sablon-glass.co/notice|latest notices]].

A tube is a lamp with its air taken out, and it takes some patience to make one. Our evacuator draws the air out with a gentle jet of water from a pipe, and it makes tubes in pairs, with no waste and no fire. The water comes from [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne Waterworks]], whose pumps and pipes we depend on. The lamps come from [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright Machine Works]], the fine wire from [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]], and the copper sheets from [[pillarandsons.co/|Pillar & Sons]]. The finished tubes go up to [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]] for their tube boards.
