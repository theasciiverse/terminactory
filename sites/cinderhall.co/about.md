# Cinderhall Foundry: About

Ruth Cinder started the foundry after being told the hill was too hot for a works. She built one anyway. Her son Jack learned to pour steel by standing very close to it, and his daughter Nell redesigned the coke oven on the back of a receipt in a public house. It has run ever since.

Our works manager, Mr Dunstan Hale, is a large and cheerful man who has never once been seen wearing gloves.

We are not secretive. We will tell you where the waste goes. Out the back, to a trade post, for a small fee.

Our steel goes to [[pillarandsons.co/|Pillar & Sons]], who have built a hall or two on it. They are very grateful, and we are very grateful back. That is how it has always gone with the three of us: Pillar, Brannock, and Cinderhall. Say hello to [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] when you see them, and watch your coat near the back door. It gets hot.
