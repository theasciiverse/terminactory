# Cinderhall Foundry: Waste

**Waste and returns**

**What is waste?**
What is left behind when a dirty recipe is done. Blast furnaces and coke ovens make it, and it always leaves out the back. It has no use, and it costs money to get rid of. Nobody buys it. We have tried.

**Where do I put it?**
A belt, a crate, or a trade post set to sell for money. A trade post takes it for a small fee. Posts set to research, or to a split, refuse it outright, so keep a second way out.

**Why does [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]] keep writing?**
They say our waste is charming. They would like it, every winter, with a letter. Their arc furnace will take silicon scrap back, which is a different matter, and they are very clear about the difference. We are glad someone finds it charming.

**Can I just leave it?**
No. A machine with nowhere to put its waste fills up, stops, and says so. It will keep saying so until you do something about it. Put a crate behind it to begin with, and think about a trade post soon after.
