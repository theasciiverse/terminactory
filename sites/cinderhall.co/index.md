# Cinderhall Foundry

Coke! Now you are cooking. Cinderhall has been melting ironstone since the first furnace was lit, and we have been enjoying it ever since. Our blast furnaces melt ironstone fast, turn plate into molten steel for a molder to cast, and harden plate with coke or coal. Our coke oven bakes coal into coke with no fire of its own, which makes it the cleanest machine on the hill.

Read the [[cinderhall.co/products|products]], meet [[cinderhall.co/about|the family]], or read the [[cinderhall.co/waste|waste and returns]] page, which we recommend before you do anything else. Really.

The coking research came from [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]], who have never let us forget it. Our crushed grit comes from [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/|Ashgrove Ironstone]], and we melt it better than whole rock. Our steel goes to [[pillarandsons.co/|Pillar & Sons]], who raise floors on it and say the girders are theirs. Our waste goes out the back, and [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]] have written asking for it every winter. We do not understand why, and we do not ask.
