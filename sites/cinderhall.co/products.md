# Cinderhall Foundry: Products

**Blast furnace.** Melts iron fast. It makes molten steel and hardened plate from plate and coke or coal, and sends its molten output out the front, into a pipe or straight into a molder. Use coke wherever you can, since coke leaves less waste out the back than coal does. Keep the back clear, or the furnace stops. Point an iron blast furnace straight into a steel one, and the molten iron goes across with no pipe at all, which we think is very neat.

**Coke oven.** Bakes coal into coke on its own. It needs no fire to feed and no EC, so a coal belt is all it wants. Coke leaves by the front and waste goes out the back. It is unlocked by research once coke has been made, which you have already done, since you are here.

**Coke.** Coke burns longer than coal, so each piece is good value in a boiler. It is the preferred fuel for our blast furnaces, and it is an ingredient in steel and hardened plate.
