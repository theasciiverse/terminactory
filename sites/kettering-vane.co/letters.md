# Kettering & Vane: Letters

**Letters to the shop**

*Dear Kettering & Vane, my panels hum and my engine hums, and the two hums do not agree. Which one is right?*
Mr Kettering writes: Both. Mr Vane writes: The panels.

*Dear Kettering & Vane, our evacuator is fed by a pipe from Wellbourne, and the water has come through warm since you installed your boiler. Is this your doing?*
[[sablon-glass.co/|Sablon Glassworks]] forwarded this one to us. Mr Kettering writes: No. Mr Vane writes: Ask [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne]]. They will say no as well.

*Dear Kettering & Vane, my boiler whistles every morning at six. Is this a fault?*
Mr Kettering writes: It is a feature. Mr Vane writes: It is a fault, and we have a part for it.

*Dear Kettering & Vane, thank you for the winder. Our springs have never been so even. Please tell [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright]] the rods were good.*
Mr Kettering writes: We will tell them. Mr Vane writes: They already know.
