# Kettering & Vane: About

Kettering & Vane was started by Mr Kettering and Mr Vane, who met over a boiler that would not stop whistling and have been discussing it since. Mr Kettering keeps the engines and the boilers. Mr Vane keeps the panels, the poles, and the drawer of spare copper.

The shop has one desk, placed in the middle of the room, so neither partner has to walk further than the other. Customers are asked to bring their disputes to the desk and leave them there.

Our first real sale was a copper coil, which we both insist was our idea. Our neighbours have been good to us. [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] sell us our plate, [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne]] keep our pipes full, and [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright]] let us have the rods. Mr Vane says this is a fair trade. Mr Kettering says it is theft, in the nicest possible way.

Our shop has a single front door and two brass plates. Both give the year we opened. Both are correct.
