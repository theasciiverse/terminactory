# Kettering & Vane: Products

**Boiler.** Turns water and coal into steam. Water comes in from pipes on any side but the front, and the steam leaves by the front. It burns coal only as the grid needs it, so an idle grid costs nothing to run. Keep the front clear.

**Steam engine.** Takes steam from a boiler or a pipe and turns it into EC for the grid. Put it right against the boiler's front, and it will top up whatever the panels miss.

**Basic solar panel.** Needs no fuel. It gives more EC than the cheap panel and reaches further, so you need fewer poles. It joins other panels only through poles, so plan those first.

**Wire drawer.** Pulls plate into wire, and wire into fine wire. Fine wire is what the assemblers and the lamp recipes need, so a drawer of its own is worth having. Runs on EC.

**Winder.** Winds wire into coils, and iron rods into springs. Fine wire makes more coils from the same copper. Coils go into electric motors, which [[swiftline.co/products|Swiftline Conveyors]] builds on its basic assembler.
