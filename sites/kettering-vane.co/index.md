# Kettering & Vane

Boilers, engines, wire and winders. Two partners, one shop, one long argument.

Welcome. We make power for your factory. Our boilers turn water and coal into steam, our steam engines turn that steam into EC, and our basic solar panels need no fuel at all.

Mr Kettering would like it known that the engines are the proper choice. Mr Vane would like it known that the panels are the modern one. Both are true, so we recommend one of each, and a pole in between.

We also draw wire. The wire drawer pulls plate into wire and wire into fine wire, and the winder turns wire into coils and iron rods into springs.

Our copper coil opened the company. Coils go into the electric motors that [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]] builds. Our boilers take their water from the pipes that [[wellbourne.co/|Wellbourne Waterworks]] sells. The rods our winder needs come from [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright Machine Works]], and the steel casings for the bigger steam goods are welded by [[pillarandsons.co/|Pillar & Sons]]. Our plate and wire come from the presses at [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]], so we are on good terms with them too.
