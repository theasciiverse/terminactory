# Board rules

**Buy Sell Trade Board.** Post an ad at the market office or reply to any thread below. Mags Dorne moderates: she edits subject lines, never the offers.

**Rules**
1. One ad per person per week. Second ads are merged into the first.
2. No prices in the subject line. Prices go in the body, or say "best offer."
3. Replies go to the board. Mags Dorne forwards them. She does not take a cut.
4. "Free" items must be collected. The board does not deliver.
5. When sold, say so. The seller edits the status to SOLD. Mags Dorne will lock the thread after a month.
6. No dead animals, no waste, no keys to the Lattice & Finch yard. (Those are missing anyway.)

**Long-running thread:** The rope between Ira Bell and Ted Vok. Mags Dorne has edited the subject line four times. It still has not been returned.
