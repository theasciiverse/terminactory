# Pillar & Sons: About

Pillar & Sons began with a single girder, a single father and a few sons who wanted something to stand on. The first girder is still in the yard, and we test it every spring.

Our first girder was pressed from steel by [[cinderhall.co/|Cinderhall Foundry]], who sent us a note to say the steel was more than adequate. We have kept the note. Our neighbours at [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]] dig the holes, and we put the beams over them. [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright]] supply the screws for our frames, and check every one.

The sign over our gate has three names on it. There are two of us in the yard. The sign was repainted in the spring, every name was checked, and all three are rated.
