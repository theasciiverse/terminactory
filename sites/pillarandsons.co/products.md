# Pillar & Sons: Products

**Support beams.** Build one floor above the ground, and come with the elevators to reach it. Upper floors cannot hold drills or pumps, so plan the floor before you fill it. [[underhill-excavation.co/|Underhill Excavation]] digs the floor below; we raise the one above.

**Rolling mill.** Rolls copper or iron plate into sheets, and steel into steel sheets and girders. It is the cheaper route to sheets than a press, since it takes less plate per sheet. Runs on EC.

**Welder.** Welds steel into boiler shells, silo shells and frames. A frame needs girders and screws. A silo shell needs steel sheets and a valve. It draws EC only while it is working, so size the grid for its busy hours. The boiler shells it makes go into the engine blocks that [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth Motor Works]] builds for the steam tractor.
