# Pillar & Sons

Structural works. Rated, proud, and standing up.

We put things up, and we keep them up. Support beams lift your factory one floor above the ground, and the elevators that go with them carry your goods between floors. When a beam needs strengthening, we do that too.

Our rolling mill squeezes copper and iron plate into sheets, and steel into steel sheets and girders. Our welder joins steel into boiler shells, silo shells and frames. A frame wants steel girders and screws, so we work closely with [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright Machine Works]], whose lathe makes the screws. Our girders are pressed from steel made by [[cinderhall.co/|Cinderhall Foundry]].

Everything we make is rated. Please read the ratings before you load it, not after. The notice board is on the gate.
