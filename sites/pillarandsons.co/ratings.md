# Pillar & Sons: Ratings

**Rated loads: notice board**

- Support beams: one floor above the ground. Elevators are rated for the floor they serve.
- Rolling mill: plate in, sheets out. Please do not put the girders in the sheet bin.
- Welder: draws EC only while working. A quiet grid will be slow to start. This is not a fault.
- Frames: girders and screws. Screws come from [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright]], who ask that you sign for them.
- Shells: silo shells and boiler shells. The boiler shells go on to [[ampleworth.co/|Ampleworth]] for engine blocks.
- Yard: please keep it clear of [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere]] drill bits.
