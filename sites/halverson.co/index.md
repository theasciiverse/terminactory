# Halverson Smelting

Silicon, wafers and some of the best-kept scrap in the valley. Sand goes in, silicon comes out, and nothing is wasted if we can help it, which is almost always.

Our products are on the [[halverson.co/products|products page]], our history is at [[halverson.co/about|about us]], and the scrap notice is at [[halverson.co/scrap|scrap and waste]]. Please read it. We do.

Our sand comes from the crusher at [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/|Ashgrove Ironstone]], and our ingots are cast in the molder that [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] sell. Our wafers go up the line to [[meridian-instruments.co/|Meridian Instruments]], who make chips from them. The scrap from the etchers at [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]] comes back to our arc furnace, which remelts it cleanly. Our crystal grower and wafer saw run on EC, and the fine wire for the saw comes from the wire drawer at [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]].

Please keep the waste coming. We find it charming.
