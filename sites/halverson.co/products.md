# Halverson Smelting: Products

**Arc furnace.** A square furnace that runs on EC alone, with no fuel. It melts sand into silicon with much less waste than a blast furnace, and it remelts silicon scrap with no waste at all. Molten silicon leaves by the front. Waste leaves out the back, so put a crate or a trade post there. It unlocks once a silicon ingot has been made.

**Crystal grower.** A tall machine on EC that grows a silicon crystal from ingots. It is the slowest machine we sell, and we are fond of it for that. Each crystal takes several ingots, so keep a supply close. Put a wafer saw straight in front of it, and the crystals go on to be cut with no belt at all.

**Wafer saw.** A long saw on EC. It cuts a silicon crystal into several wafers along a fine wire, and it leaves no scrap. It is quicker than a lathe at slicing, which we say with respect for the lathe. Wafers leave by the front, bound for the kiln and the doping line.
