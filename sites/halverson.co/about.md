# Halverson Smelting: About

Halverson Smelting has been in the valley for a long time. The name comes from the first Halverson, who smelted whatever came up the hill and was pleased with all of it. We still say smelting, though these days the work is silicon.

We opened when our first silicon ingot was cast. Brannock's molder made it, and it still sits on the window ledge by the arc furnace, where the light is good. Since then we have made silicon from sand, and wafers from silicon, and very little that we did not expect to be useful later.

The current head is Greta Halverson, who keeps the books. Our arc furnace has never once been switched off. It has never needed to be. Visitors sometimes ask, and we tell them it is a very good furnace, which is true.
