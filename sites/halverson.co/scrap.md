# Halverson Smelting: Scrap

Scrap and waste: a notice for our neighbours.

Silicon scrap is worth keeping. It comes out of the etcher at [[latticefinch.co/|Lattice & Finch]] and from the lathe at [[gearwright.co/|Gearwright Machine Works]], and our arc furnace will remelt it with no waste at all. Please send it our way. We will be delighted.

Waste is a different matter. The arc furnace sends its waste out the back. Place a crate or a trade post behind the furnace, and it will be quite happy. If the back fills up, the furnace will stop and say so.

The wafer saw leaves no scrap, so there is nothing to collect there. The crystal grower is slow, so please be patient with it.
