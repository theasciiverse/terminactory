# Essay — Window boxes and terminals (Y154-06-19)

Jody's window boxes are out for the summer. Nasturtiums and marigolds, in pots she painted herself. She says the line can wait. My line does not wait; it runs whether I am there or not.

I program eight lamps in a row. On, off, on, off. Four minutes to write, eight seconds to run. Jody waters her pots every evening. I switch on my terminal every evening. We do not talk about which is more useful.

I will look at the nasturtiums this evening. The terminal will still be there when I get back.
