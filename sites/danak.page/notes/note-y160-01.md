# Lamp-matrix note — Y160-01-09

The terminal shows the same thing every time. I have written it down for eight years and it does not change: the same lamps, the same order, the same quiet. The x1x1 board is the weakest microprocessor, the letter says, and it does what it is told. My terminal is a tube board, x2x1, six tubes and a control board and an iron sheet. It boots to the same screen.

I do not need to wonder what comes next. It comes the same as it always has. I am certain of this, and I am tidy about it. Everything is where it belongs.

Jody says the nasturtiums are dead for the winter. I will look this evening anyway.
