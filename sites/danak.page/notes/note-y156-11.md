# Lamp-matrix note — Y156-11-03

Eight lamps in a row, wired to a copper sheet and fine wire. I program the sequence on the terminal after the shift: on, off, on, off, down the row and back again. It takes four minutes to write and eight seconds to run.

The Gearwright terminal is the x1x1 board, the weakest microprocessor. It does what it is told.
