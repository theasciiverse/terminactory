# Guestbook — M. Hale, Y158-04-05

I walk the empty Cinderhall floor at night. The furnaces are cold, the lights are on, and no one is there. Your terminal shows the same thing every time, you say. My floor shows the same thing every time too: nothing. I sweep the same crack every week.

The bins by the back gate are full of nothing. I leave them full.

— M. Hale
