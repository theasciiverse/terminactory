# Guestbook — Clem Ortiz, Y149-03-07

Crane hand, idle since the closures. I read the Gazette every morning at the library. Your page is new and I do not understand it, but I like the lamps. The tubes ring when you tap them, Toby Dune said. He is dead now, and the tubes are in a box by the library door.

The crane yard is quiet. The cranes do not lift anything.

— Clem Ortiz
