# Guestbook — Ben Rye, Y152-08-19

Welder, Underhill kiln crew. I bring soup to potlucks. I heard your line through the wall the night the assembler ran its thousandth gearbox. Valves, gauges, lamps, relays, gearboxes, ten seconds each. The empty plant hears everything.

I sat down and the machine kept going without me. That is how it should be.

— Ben Rye
