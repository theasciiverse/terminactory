# Gearwright Machine Works: About

Gearwright Machine Works was founded by one patient person, who counted the teeth on a gear, then counted them again, then sent it out. Their habits are still the house rules: we do not hurry, we do not guess, and we do not ship a gear with an odd tooth.

We have one clock in the front office, and it is slow. It has been slow since we opened. We have never found it necessary to correct it, and we have never once been late.

Our neighbours are good company. [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]] press our iron gears from plate, and they are very good at it. [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]] make the silicon, and their wafer saw is much quicker than our lathe at slicing it. They told us so, and we did not argue. [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline]] have been waiting for our gearboxes since they opened, and they say so every week.
