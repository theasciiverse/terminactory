# Gearwright Machine Works

Patient parts, made properly.

A gear with teeth like that deserves a proper home. We make small goods slowly and well. The crude assembler builds valves, gauges, lamps, relays and gearboxes, one at a time, and it needs no EC at all. Our lathe turns plate into rods and screws, steel into steel rods and bearings, and slices silicon ingots into wafers, slowly.

Our iron gear is pressed from plate by [[brannock.co/|Brannock & Daughters]], and it opens us. Gears go into gearboxes, and gearboxes open [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]]. If you ever wonder who is responsible for the belts, we are a sort of grandparent.

Our rods wind into springs at [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]]. Our bearings and steel rods go into the electric motors that Swiftline builds. Our wafers are slow, and [[halverson.co/|Halverson Smelting]] has said so, more than once, in writing.
