# Gearwright Machine Works: Faq

**Frequently asked**

*Why is the crude assembler so slow?* It is careful. Ask it to hurry and it will simply build the same valve more carefully.

*Do you sell a faster assembler?* Not us. [[swiftline.co/products|Swiftline Conveyors]] sell the basic assembler, which is much quicker and runs on EC.

*Will the lathe slice wafers quickly?* No. Use a [[halverson.co/|wafer saw]] if you are in a hurry. We will wait with you.

*Do the rods have to be perfectly round?* They have to be round enough to wind. [[kettering-vane.co/|Kettering & Vane]] will tell you if they are not.

*What happens to the scrap?* The lathe's scrap sells and remelts, so nothing is wasted. The [[brannock.co/|Brannock]] presses are less forgiving with plate.
