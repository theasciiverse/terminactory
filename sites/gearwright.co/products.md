# Gearwright Machine Works: Products

**Crude assembler.** A square machine that builds valves, gauges, lamps, relays and gearboxes. It runs with no EC, and it is slow, which we consider a virtue. Give it a spread of inputs, since most recipes need several parts at once. When you are ready, move the gearboxes over to the [[swiftline.co/products|basic assembler]], which is much faster.

**Lathe.** Turns plate into rods, and rods into screws. Turns steel into steel rods and bearings. Slices silicon ingots into wafers slowly, with the scrap left to remelt. Runs on EC.

Iron rods come first, since the [[kettering-vane.co/products|winder]] needs them for springs. Screws are needed for the frames that [[pillarandsons.co/|Pillar & Sons]] welds. Bearings and steel rods go into the electric motors that Swiftline builds.
