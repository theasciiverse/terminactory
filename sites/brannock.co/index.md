# Brannock & Daughters

Three sisters, one catalogue, and a workshop that has never closed for a holiday none of us wanted. We make the things a factory needs to move, shape and heat: presses, furnaces, molders, belts, splitters and arms, crates, warehouses, solar panels and poles. If it is not on the catalogue, we will tell you honestly where to find it, which is the next best thing.

Start with the [[brannock.co/products|catalogue]]. Our story is on [[brannock.co/about|the about page]], and our customer letters are on the [[brannock.co/letters|letters page]].

Your ore will want a drill first, and [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere Mining Co.]] make the cheap one that digs it. Once you have copper wire, [[ashgrove-ironstone.co/|Ashgrove Ironstone]] will be glad to see you. Your coke will want a furnace, and [[cinderhall.co/|Cinderhall Foundry]] know all about that. And if your belts are too slow, [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline Conveyors]] will tell you so, loudly. Do not spend your money there.
