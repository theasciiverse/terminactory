# Brannock & Daughters: Letters

**Customer letters, printed with permission**

*From a copper works up the valley:* "Your press is a very good press. It stopped making wire for days and I could not find the fault. Then I found it was the belt. I am not writing to complain. I am writing to say I was wrong about the belt."

*From Ashgrove, who are fussy:* "We put a warehouse beside a fast line and it smoothed out like butter. Please say whether we should empty it before moving it. The last one we moved, nobody told us, and we are still looking for the coal."

*From a customer who would like to remain anonymous:* "Dear Brannock, Swiftline sent a card. We have put it in the crate, as you suggested. The crate is a little warm. Please advise. Thank you for the very good belts."
