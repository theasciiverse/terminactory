# Brannock & Daughters: Products

**Presses.** A press squeezes ingots into plate, and plate into wire, pipe and coils. Items go in from any side and leave by the front. It is the workhorse of the copper and iron stage, and one of the first machines you will build. A press sitting in front of a molder needs no belt between them.

**Furnaces and molders.** A furnace melts ore and bakes coal. A molder casts molten metal into ingots. Put the molder straight in front of the furnace and the metal goes in with no pipe between. Both are open from the start.

**Belts and arms.** The basic belt, the belt tunnel that dives under a short stretch of anything in its way, the long arm that reaches a short way over whatever sits between, the filtering arm, the basic splitter and the filter splitter. Tunnels, long arms, splitters and warehouses are all our research.

**Crates and warehouses.** A crate soaks up the surplus when one machine runs faster than the next. A warehouse is the same idea at a much larger size. Empty either before you move it, or the contents go with it.

**Panels and poles.** The cheap solar panel gives steady power with no fuel. Panels do not link to one another, so put an EC pole between any that sit apart.

**Coking.** Our research lets your furnaces bake coal into coke, which is how [[cinderhall.co/|Cinderhall Foundry]] got started.
