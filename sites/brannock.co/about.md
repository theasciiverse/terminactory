# Brannock & Daughters: About

Our father, Tobias Brannock, made presses in a shed behind the chapel, and made them so well that people came back for more of the same. He had three daughters and no sons, which he said was a great relief to the bank.

Agnes does the books and the drawings, which are the best in the valley. Beatrix runs the furnaces and the molders, and is quite sure she knows why each one hums. Min looks after the customers, and has never once let one leave unhappy, including the ones who came in unhappy about [[swiftline.co/|Swiftline]].

We are not a large firm. We are a careful one. Every machine goes out tested, with the instructions in our own hand. Every belt is measured before it is sold. Our belts are slower than Swiftline's. We think about them longer.

We have been supplying [[hollowmere.co/|Hollowmere Mining Co.]] for as long as anyone can remember, and they still send us a drill part for the belt drives every spring. We send back a tin of tea. It is a fair trade.
