# Canteen menu, and the great pudding question
board: Canteen   date: Y133-11-21   author: canteen_agnes   status: open

- canteen_agnes | Y133-11-21 | The menu for the winter. I am putting it here so the night men can see it before they come in and not stand at the hatch asking.
  Monday: barley soup, bread, cheese.
  Tuesday: vegetable soup, bread, the cold meat if there is any.
  Wednesday: barley, bread, cheese.
  Thursday: the other soup, bread, whatever is left.
  Friday: barley, bread, and a pudding if the oven is behaving.
  The pudding is the question. Rice or sponge. I have made both and I am not making both.
