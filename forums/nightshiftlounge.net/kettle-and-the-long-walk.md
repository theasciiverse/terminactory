# The kettle and the long walk
board: The Lounge   date: Y154-09-08   author: kettlekeeper   status: open

- kettlekeeper | Y154-09-08 | The boiler house kettle has been on every night for thirty years. I am retiring in December and I am writing this because somebody will take it over and I want them to know how it works.
  It is not a machine. It is a kettle on a ring. Fill it at the tap, not from the boiler drain, because the drain is hot and the kettle will crack. Put it on the ring at half two and it is ready by three. Do not leave it on the ring after four or it boils dry and you will have to explain a burnt kettle to a foreman who does not care about tea.
