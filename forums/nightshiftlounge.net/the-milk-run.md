# The milk run: who brings what?
board: Canteen   date: Y155-01-29   author: soup_ladle   status: open

- soup_ladle | Y155-01-29 | The milk comes up on the afternoon cart and there is never enough for the night. I am proposing a rota and I would like to hear the objections before I make one.
  - kettlekeeper | Y155-01-29 | Objection one: a rota means a man has to remember on his day off. Put the milk on the standing order and pay for it out of the pot.
    - soup_ladle | Y155-01-29 | What pot?
      - kettlekeeper | Y155-01-29 | The pot we all put into for tea. There has been a pot since before you came.
  - canteen_agnes | Y155-01-30 | The standing order is the answer and it is what we had until Y148. Somebody cancelled it when the deliveries changed and nobody noticed until the milk ran out.
    - soup_ladle | Y155-01-30 | Who cancelled it?
      - canteen_agnes | Y155-01-30 | Nobody knows and it does not matter. Order it again.
  - bench_light | Y155-01-30 | Two crates a week and one of them for the gate. The gatehouse drinks more tea than the whole floor and they will not admit it.
    - soup_ladle | Y155-01-30 | Two crates. Noted.
  - soup_ladle | Y155-02-11 | Standing order in, two crates, paid from the pot. It has not run out since and the pot has more in it than when we started, which I do not understand and will not investigate.
