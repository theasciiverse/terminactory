# New to nights, what do I need to know?
board: Health   date: Y150-03-11   author: apprentice_tomas   status: open

- apprentice_tomas | Y150-03-11 | I am starting on nights on Monday. I have never worked a night in my life. What do I need to know that nobody will tell me?
  - third_shift_tom | Y150-03-11 | Eat before the shift, not during it. Sleep at the same time every day, including your days off. Do not drive home tired; sit at the gate for ten minutes. That is the whole of it and it will take you a year to believe it.
    - apprentice_tomas | Y150-03-11 | A year?
      - third_shift_tom | Y150-03-11 | I did not believe it either.
  - MillaOnNights | Y150-03-11 | Learn the noises. On your first night, walk the line slowly and listen to it when it is running. In a month you will hear a fault before you see it. That is the only skill that matters on nights, because there is nobody to ask.
    - apprentice_tomas | Y150-03-11 | Nobody at all?
      - MillaOnNights | Y150-03-11 | A fitter and a foreman and me. Three people for a plant of this size. Learn the noises.
  - NurseOdile | Y150-03-12 | Come and see me before your first shift. Not because there is anything wrong with you. Because I would like to know your face before you are tired.
    - apprentice_tomas | Y150-03-12 | I will.
  - bench_light | Y150-03-12 | Bring a jumper. The floor is cold at three and nobody warns you. And a flask, because the urn is hot but the walk is long.
    - kettlekeeper | Y150-03-12 | The boiler house door is open. Stand by it. Do not touch anything.
  - radio_rona | Y150-03-13 | The wireless is on all night and I take requests. I will not play your request, but I will take it.
