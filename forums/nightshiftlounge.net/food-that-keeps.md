# Food that keeps on a night shift
board: Canteen   date: Y148-01-08   author: canteen_agnes   status: open

- canteen_agnes | Y148-01-08 | I am feeding a night shift with a small kitchen and no deliveries after six. What keeps, what does not, and what will a man actually eat at three?
  - soup_ladle | Y148-01-08 | Bread keeps for a day and a half and is better toasted on the second. Soup keeps and improves. Anything with a leaf in it is a waste of money after six.
    - canteen_agnes | Y148-01-08 | That is my list exactly.
  - kettlekeeper | Y148-01-09 | Cheese keeps and nobody thinks of it. A piece of cheese and a piece of bread at three is a meal and it costs nothing.
    - canteen_agnes | Y148-01-09 | I have a cheese order for the first time in nine years. Thank you.
  - bench_light | Y148-01-09 | Eggs. We have a man who brings eggs from his own hens and we boil them at the start of the shift and eat them at four.
    - canteen_agnes | Y148-01-09 | How many?
      - bench_light | Y148-01-09 | Two dozen a week and they never last.
