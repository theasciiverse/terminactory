# Walking home at four in the morning
board: Health   date: Y146-11-17   author: dawn_porter   status: open

- dawn_porter | Y146-11-17 | Four of us walk the same road home at four and we have started walking together. It is not a safety measure and it has become one. Does anybody else do this?
  - NurseOdile | Y146-11-17 | It is a safety measure and you should say so. Two of the four of you are on the road when the carts start and a cart at four is not looking for a man on foot.
    - dawn_porter | Y146-11-17 | We walk on the verge.
      - NurseOdile | Y146-11-17 | Walk on the verge and wear something pale. I am not asking for a uniform, I am asking for a coat that is not black.
  - the_last_round | Y146-11-18 | The gate has a bench and a light and a pot. If you are walking alone, sit for ten minutes first. It is not the walk that is dangerous, it is the first hundred yards when you are still thinking about the line.
    - dawn_porter | Y146-11-18 | We have started sitting. It is the best ten minutes of the day.
  - MillaOnNights | Y146-11-18 | I walked home alone for six years and was fine and I do not recommend it. The years you are fine are not evidence.
    - dawn_porter | Y146-11-18 | That is the sentence I will use on the next one who says he is fine.
