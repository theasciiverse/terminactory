board: The Lounge   date: Y144-01-27   author: bench_light   status: open

- bench_light | Y144-01-27 | Not a question. I have been on nights for seven years and I want to try to write down what it is like, because the day men do not believe me.
  At three the plant is at its quietest and its loudest. The belts are the only thing you hear and after a while you stop hearing them. The lights are on over the aisles and off over the machines. The floor is cold. If you stand still for a minute you can hear the building.
