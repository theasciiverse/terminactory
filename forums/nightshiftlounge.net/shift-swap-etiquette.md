# Shift swap etiquette: what do you owe the man who covers?
board: Rota   date: Y151-08-12   author: marge_on_four   status: open

- marge_on_four | Y151-08-12 | I have swapped four shifts this year and I am starting to feel I owe a debt I cannot name. What is the custom here?
  - third_shift_tom | Y151-08-12 | You take his next awkward one. Not the next one, the next awkward one. A swap is a debt with a shape.
    - marge_on_four | Y151-08-12 | What if he never has an awkward one?
      - third_shift_tom | Y151-08-12 | Everybody has an awkward one. You will know it when he asks.
  - sam_three_to_eleven | Y151-08-13 | Feed him. A man who covers your Saturday gets a plate at the hatch and a note on the board saying who covered.
    - marge_on_four | Y151-08-13 | The note is a good idea.
  - canteen_agnes | Y151-08-13 | The note is the whole idea. A swap nobody hears about is a favour and a favour is remembered badly. A swap on the board is a system.
  - ForemanEllis | Y151-08-14 | Please put the swap in the rota file as well as the board. I do not need to approve it. I need to know who is on the floor at three.
    - marge_on_four | Y151-08-14 | Both, then.
  - kettlekeeper | Y151-08-15 | And if he covers you twice in a month, you cover him twice in a month whether he asks or not. That is not etiquette, that is arithmetic.
