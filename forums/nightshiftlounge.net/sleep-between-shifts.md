- NurseOdile | Y136-02-09 | I am the plant nurse and I am not going to tell anybody how to live. I am going to write down what the night men tell me works, and what they tell me does not.
  Works, according to them: the same bedtime every day, including days off; a dark room; a cold room; no argument with the family about breakfast at nine at night; and eating before the shift rather than during it.
  Does not work, according to them: "catching up" at the weekend; going to bed as soon as they get in; and coffee after four, which they all do anyway.
