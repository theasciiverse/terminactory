# Tea at the gate: whose job is it?
board: Canteen   date: Y149-12-19   author: the_last_round   status: open

- the_last_round | Y149-12-19 | There is a pot at the gate and there is no rota. Somebody fills it, somebody empties it, and nobody has ever been told to. Who is supposed to do it?
  - west_gate_pat | Y149-12-19 | The gatehouse does it and always has. It is not a job, it is a habit, and a habit does not need a rota.
    - the_last_round | Y149-12-19 | Then I will stop doing it.
      - west_gate_pat | Y149-12-19 | You will not. You have been doing it for a year.
  - kettlekeeper | Y149-12-20 | A pot that is nobody's job is a pot that gets filled by whoever is coldest. That is a system and it works.
  - canteen_agnes | Y149-12-20 | It works until the man who is coldest is also the man who is busiest. Then it is empty for a week and everybody complains about the gate.
    - west_gate_pat | Y149-12-20 | It has never been empty for a week.
      - canteen_agnes | Y149-12-20 | Not yet.
  - night_porter_iss | Y149-12-21 | I will do the four o'clock fill. I am on the corridor anyway and it is four minutes.
    - the_last_round | Y149-12-21 | Then it is your job and we will all pretend it is not.
