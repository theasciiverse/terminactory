# A new porter's first week
board: The Lounge   date: Y149-03-30   author: night_porter_iss   status: open

- night_porter_iss | Y149-03-30 | First week on nights as a porter. I have a lamp, a list and a corridor. What am I supposed to be doing that is not on the list?
  - dawn_porter | Y149-03-30 | Listen at every door for three seconds before you open it. You will learn which machines are well and which are pretending.
    - night_porter_iss | Y149-03-30 | That is not on the list.
      - dawn_porter | Y149-03-30 | It is the whole job. The list is the excuse.
  - third_shift_tom | Y149-03-31 | Find the kettle before you need it. A porter who knows where the kettle is becomes a useful man in a week.
  - quiet_corridor | Y149-03-31 | And do not run. A man running down a corridor at night means something is wrong. Walk fast if you must.
    - night_porter_iss | Y149-03-31 | What if something is wrong?
      - quiet_corridor | Y149-03-31 | Then run and let them worry.
  - MillaOnNights | Y149-04-01 | Keep the same round in the same order. If you change the order you will not notice what is missing from it.
    - night_porter_iss | Y149-04-01 | I have written the round in the front of the book.
