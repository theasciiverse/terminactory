# Who was Mallory?
board: Archive   date: Y154-06-18   author: archive_clerk   status: open

- archive_clerk | Y154-06-18 | There is a name in the Y124 gate book that appears eleven times and nowhere else. Mallory. No first name, no trade, no account on this board. Eleven loads signed for and then nothing. Does anybody know the name?
  - GerdV | Y154-06-18 | Mallory was on the gate for a few months in Y124 and then went to the coast. I remember the coat. I do not remember a first name and I am not sure I ever heard one.
    - archive_clerk | Y154-06-18 | Which gate?
      - GerdV | Y154-06-18 | The west. She signed for the Line 2 girders and she would not sign for anything she had not counted.
  - OldHollowmere | Y154-06-19 | I remember the counting. She made a driver open a crate of bolts and count them in the rain and he did it. That is the whole of my memory of her.
    - archive_clerk | Y154-06-19 | That is more than the book has. Thank you.
  - west_gate_pat | Y154-06-19 | Before my time on the gate, but her handwriting is in the Y124 book and it is the neatest in the box. Whoever she was, she was careful.
    - archive_clerk | Y154-06-19 | Careful is the only thing the record says about her and it says it eleven times.
  - brass_tally | Y154-06-20 | She signed for the girders on Line 2 and I never thanked her. If anybody is going to the coast I would like that passed on.
    - GerdV | Y154-06-20 | I do not know where on the coast and I do not think she would remember me.
      - brass_tally | Y154-06-20 | Pass it on anyway.
