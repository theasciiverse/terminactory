# The first floor, in their own words
board: Archive   date: Y150-07-19   author: archive_clerk   status: locked

- archive_clerk | Y150-07-19 | Assembled from the first four years of the board, before it had sections. The first floor is the one under the yard, dug out with a cheap drill and a great deal of opinion. Everyone quoted here has stopped posting. I have left the spelling as it was left.
  **Y120-05-02, [Deleted User #209]:** Someone should write down what the first floor is like before we all agree on a version of it.
  **Y120-05-03, eldermill:** It is cold and it is loud and the lift takes forty seconds. I would not swap it.
  **Y120-06-11, the_first_drill:** First hole went in at six this morning. Nobody clapped. I did not expect anybody to.
  **Y120-09-27, greasy_hands:** The belt from the drill to the furnace is nine tiles and it has been adjusted four times. It is right now. Do not touch it.
  **Y121-01-14, Voss_shift:** Coal in, coal out, coal on the floor, coal in my coat. I have stopped noticing the colour black. Somebody tell me it is still a colour.
  **Y121-03-02, [Deleted User #209]:** It is a colour. It is the only one down here.
  **Y121-08-19, cold_forge:** The forge is up. Whoever built the floor put the beam in the wrong place and we have built around it for a year. It is our beam now.
  **Y122-02-07, Marnie_Q:** There is a pot of soup at the bottom of the lift at half five and it is for whoever is on the floor. Do not take the pot.
  **Y122-11-30, otto_bench:** A bench by the wall, a vice on the bench, and a window at the top of the shaft. That is all I want and I have got it.
  **Y123-04-16, eldermill:** The mill is turning. It has taken two years and I have not been home before dark once.
  **Y124-01-09, the_first_drill:** They are opening Line 2. I am not going. Somebody has to stay with the first hole.
  - archive_clerk | Y150-07-19 | These are all the entries I have from those years that are about the floor itself. There are forty more about machines and I have not transcribed them. The originals are in the box with the gate book.
