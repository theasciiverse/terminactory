- eldermill | Y134-02-27 | I am putting this here because I have answered it forty times in forty threads and I am not going to answer it again.
  A rolling mill is not a press. A press squeezes a plate into shape with a die. A mill rolls it between two hard cylinders, and the plate comes out thinner and longer and no weaker. That is why the mill takes two plate for a sheet where the press takes three, and three steel for two girders where the press takes two steel for one.
  If your mill is jamming, it is not the mill. It is the feed. A mill wants a steady plate and it wants it flat. A plate that has been sitting in a crate with a girder on top is not flat and it will jam. That is the whole of it. Mind the mill. It will take a finger and not slow down.
