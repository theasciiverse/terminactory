# Wire drawer or press for copper wire?
board: Beginner Questions   date: Y145-07-14   author: tiny_lathe   status: open

- tiny_lathe | Y145-07-14 | My wire drawer takes a plate and gives three wire. My press gives two from the same plate. Which should be feeding a winder?
  - brass_tally | Y145-07-14 | The drawer. Three for one instead of two, and four seconds against five. It costs research and it draws EC, and it is worth both the day you build a second winder.
    - ferrous_ann | Y145-07-14 | And it frees the press. A press making wire at two for one is a press you will replace and then wonder what to do with.
  - tiny_lathe | Y145-07-15 | I have both. The drawer goes to the winders and the press goes to the line that does not care.
    - browncoat_bex | Y145-07-15 | Keep one press able to make wire. The day the drawer stops at three in the morning you will want the press, not an argument with a fitter.
      - tiny_lathe | Y145-07-15 | Noted. I have left the press with the recipe on it.
  - six_belts | Y145-07-16 | Fine wire is the other way round. Only a drawer makes it, and a winder takes six fine wire for two coils, which is better than three wire for one. Count before you build.
