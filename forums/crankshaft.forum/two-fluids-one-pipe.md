# Two fluids in one pipe if I empty it between?
board: Beginner Questions   date: Y136-05-02   author: grey_belt   status: open

- grey_belt | Y136-05-02 | Can I run molten copper and then molten iron down the same pipe if I let it run empty in between?
  - pipefitter_uri | Y136-05-02 | No. A joined run is one pool and it will not take a second fluid while the first is in it, and there is no emptying it. You are not cleaning a pipe, you are changing what the pipe is.
    - grey_belt | Y136-05-02 | So two runs.
      - pipefitter_uri | Y136-05-03 | Two runs, or a furnace against a molder with no pipe at all, which is what I would do with three furnaces and one molder.
        - grey_belt | Y136-05-03 | Three furnaces into one molder?
          - molder_max | Y136-05-03 | They will take turns and lose very little. A molder casts twenty-four a minute and a copper furnace makes twelve. Two is comfortable. Three is a queue.
            - grey_belt | Y136-05-04 | I will do two runs and keep the third furnace for later.
