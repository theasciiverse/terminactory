# Solar panels touching but not powering anything
board: Troubleshooting   date: Y130-07-09   author: TamsinR   status: solved

- TamsinR | Y130-07-09 | I have four cheap solar panels in a square, touching each other, and my molder is one tile away and still says PWR. What am I doing wrong? I am an apprentice and I have been at this for an hour.
  - ivo_p | Y130-07-09 | Nothing is wrong with the panels. Panels never link to each other directly, even when they touch. You need a pole between them. The pole joins them into one grid and the molder joins it too if it is in reach.
    - TamsinR | Y130-07-09 | A pole in the middle of the panels?
      - ivo_p | Y130-07-10 | A pole anywhere their reach touches. Put it beside the panels and run a second pole towards the molder if the molder is out of reach. Poles link to each other, so a line of them can carry a grid across the floor.
        - TamsinR | Y130-07-10 | It works. The molder is running. Thank you. Edit: solved.
          - kettle_ron | Y130-07-10 | Everybody has done this. Do not feel badly about it.
          - brass_tally | Y130-07-11 | A panel is a source, not a wire. A pole is the wire. That is the whole of it.
  - slow_steam | Y130-07-11 | And a word for later. On an underground floor there is no sunlight, so no panels. An elevator carries power down to the floor it serves, which is the only way to light a dug floor.
    - TamsinR | Y130-07-11 | I will remember that when I am allowed to dig.
