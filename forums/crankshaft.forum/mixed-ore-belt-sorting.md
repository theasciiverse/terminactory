# One belt with copper and iron on it: how do you sort it?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y155-03-09   author: ferrous_ann   status: open

- ferrous_ann | Y155-03-09 | We have two patches close together and one belt coming off both, so the belt carries copper ore and iron ore mixed. I would rather not build two belts to the furnaces. What is the tidiest way?
  - six_belts | Y155-03-09 | Filter splitter. One item out the front, everything else out the sides. Set it to the ore your furnace can take and let the rest go by.
    - ferrous_ann | Y155-03-09 | Then I need one filter per furnace.
      - six_belts | Y155-03-09 | One filter per branch, not per furnace. A branch can serve a bank if the bank all wants the same ore.
  - mateo_belts | Y155-03-10 | We did this with two filters and a tunnel to cross the aisle. The filters are on the branch, the mixed belt runs past them, and the tunnel takes the iron under the copper line.
    - ferrous_ann | Y155-03-10 | That is two tunnels.
      - mateo_belts | Y155-03-10 | It is two tunnels and no crossed belts, and you will not be rebuilding it in a year.
  - brass_tally | Y155-03-11 | A note on the count. A filter splitter passes one item out the front per pass and sends the rest out the sides, so the side belt carries more than the front one. Put the side belt where the volume is, not where the space is.
    - ferrous_ann | Y155-03-11 | That is the bit I would have got wrong. Thank you.
  - ivo_p | Y155-03-12 | And check the EC on the filters. Two filters are two machines and they want poles. A filter with no pole is a belt with a lid on it.
    - ferrous_ann | Y155-03-12 | Poles first. Noted.
  - sand_and_stone | Y155-03-13 | We tried this and gave up. The mixed belt was fine; the counting was not. Every time a patch ran dry we had a belt of the wrong ore and nobody noticed until the furnace said NO ORE.
    - ferrous_ann | Y155-03-13 | What did you do?
      - sand_and_stone | Y155-03-13 | Two belts and a wider aisle. It was less clever and it has not stopped since.
        - brass_tally | Y155-03-14 | Both answers are correct. One is correct for a plant with a man counting, and one is correct for a plant without one.
  - kiln_marit | Y155-03-20 | We are still on one mixed belt and the filter has been right for six months. I check the patches on Mondays.
    - ferrous_ann | Y155-03-20 | Mondays. I will remember that.
