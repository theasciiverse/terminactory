# Steam tractor: is the control panel line worth it?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y156-07-02   author: foreman_hale   status: open

- foreman_hale | Y156-07-02 | We have been asked for a steam tractor. The recipe wants a control panel, and a control panel wants two control boards, four lamps, two gauges and a steel sheet. The rest of the tractor is engine block, frame, four hardened gears and a generator. I can see the tractor. I cannot see why the panel is so dear. Is there a cheaper way round it?
  - tiny_lathe | Y156-07-02 | There is no way round it. The panel is two control boards, four lamps, two gauges, one steel sheet, and the large advanced assembler takes twenty seconds. It is dear because the boards are dear and the lamps are four.
    - foreman_hale | Y156-07-02 | Four lamps for one panel.
      - tiny_lathe | Y156-07-02 | A lamp is glass and two fine wire. It is the cheapest thing in the panel and it is the one that will starve you if you have one lamp line.
        - foreman_hale | Y156-07-02 | Fine wire again.
          - tiny_lathe | Y156-07-02 | Fine wire again. It is always fine wire.
  - rivet_pete | Y156-07-03 | Control boards. One relay, four fine wire, one iron sheet, and the advanced assembler takes ten seconds for one board. Two boards a panel. If you are on one assembler you will be making boards all week.
    - foreman_hale | Y156-07-03 | We have one advanced assembler and it is also making tube boards for the terminal line.
      - rivet_pete | Y156-07-03 | Then it is making neither properly. One assembler, one job, and a second assembler for the other. That is the answer and it is not cheap.
        - anvil_jo | Y156-07-04 | And the tube boards want six tubes each. Tubes come from the evacuator, two a run, with water. If you are baking them in a kiln instead you are paying waste for the privilege.
          - foreman_hale | Y156-07-04 | We have an evacuator.
            - anvil_jo | Y156-07-04 | Then keep the water on it. An evacuator with a dry pipe is a machine that waits and looks broken.
  - halverson_adjacent | Y156-07-04 | We built one panel and it took a month. The thing nobody warns you about is the lamps: four of them, glass and fine wire, and a lamp line is a fine wire line. If your drawer is already feeding masks and boards, the lamps are what will starve.
    - foreman_hale | Y156-07-04 | We have one drawer.
      - halverson_adjacent | Y156-07-04 | Then you have a lamp bottleneck and you will meet it in the third week.

  - ferrous_ann | Y156-07-05 | Gears. Four hardened gears, and a hardened gear is two hardened plate, and a hardened plate is a plate and a coke. Eight hardened plate, eight coke, no waste if you use the coke recipe. Use the coal recipe and you have sixteen coal and sixteen waste, and you will be paying the fee on a tractor.
    - foreman_hale | Y156-07-05 | We are on coke.
      - ferrous_ann | Y156-07-05 | Good. Two plate, one coke. It does not change at the top of the tree.
  - ratio_warden | Y156-07-06 | A note on the question as asked. "Is it worth it" is not answerable without knowing the alternative. The alternative to a control panel is not a cheaper control panel, it is no steam tractor. If the plant wants a tractor, the panel is the price. If the plant wants a cheaper machine, it wants a different machine.
    - foreman_hale | Y156-07-06 | That is fair. I will put the question to the plant in those words.
