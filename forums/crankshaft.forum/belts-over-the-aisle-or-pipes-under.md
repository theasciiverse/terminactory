# Belts over the aisle or pipes under it?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y130-01-14   author: pipefitter_uri   status: open

- pipefitter_uri | Y130-01-14 | This is an argument I have had four times this year and I would like to have it once, properly, with the people who build both.
  My position: if a thing has to cross the main aisle, put it under. A pipe under the aisle is out of the way, it costs nothing to look at, and nobody trips over it. A belt over the aisle is a lid on a corridor and it has to be high enough for a man with a crate. It never is.
