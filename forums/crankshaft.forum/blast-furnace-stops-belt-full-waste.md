# Blast furnace stops and the belt behind it is full of waste
board: Troubleshooting   date: Y141-05-19   author: noon_shift_nils   status: solved

- noon_shift_nils | Y141-05-19 | Hello. My blast furnace stops every ten minutes or so. The belt behind it is full of waste and I cannot get it to move. I have tried a second belt and it fills too. What am I doing wrong?
  - pipefitter_uri | Y141-05-19 | You are not doing anything wrong with the furnace. You are doing something wrong with the waste. A belt is not a drain. It carries waste to somewhere that takes it, and the only thing that takes it is a trade post set to sell for money.
    - noon_shift_nils | Y141-05-19 | I have a post at the end of the belt. It is set to split.
      - pipefitter_uri | Y141-05-19 | There it is. A post set to split refuses waste outright. Put it on money and the belt will clear.
        - noon_shift_nils | Y141-05-20 | That was it. It has run for two hours. Thank you. Edit: solved.
          - ferrous_ann | Y141-05-20 | For the record, a research post refuses it too. Only money takes waste, and it charges a small fee for each one.
  - brass_tally | Y141-05-20 | A second belt into a blocked post is two full belts. Clear the end first, then decide whether you need more belt.
  - browncoat_bex | Y141-05-21 | And if the post is on money and it still will not take, look at the belt going into it. A post takes from any side, so a belt that ends one tile short is a belt that ends on nothing.
