# The pipe is holding steam and will not take water
board: Troubleshooting   date: Y143-06-21   author: pipefitter_uri   status: open

- pipefitter_uri | Y143-06-21 | I am writing this here because I have answered it twice this month and the answer is short. A joined run of pipe is one pool and it holds one fluid. If the boiler has pushed steam into the run, water will not go in behind it. There is no draining it. Build the second run.
