# What belongs on a night shift handover sheet?
board: Off Topic   date: Y148-02-05   author: MillaOnNights   status: open

- MillaOnNights | Y148-02-05 | We are rewriting the handover sheet for Line 3 and the argument has gone on for a week. What do you actually write down at four in the morning?
  - ForemanEllis | Y148-02-05 | Every stoppage, with the time and the machine, and what was done. Not what you think caused it. What was done. Please keep it to one line each.
    - MillaOnNights | Y148-02-05 | That is what we have and nobody reads it.
      - ForemanEllis | Y148-02-05 | They read it when a machine stops twice in a week. That is the only time it is worth writing.
  - ivo_p | Y148-02-06 | The state of every grid. Which poles were moved, which machine was left on a separate supply, and anything you switched over to get through the night. The morning shift will not guess and should not have to.
    - slow_steam | Y148-02-06 | And the coal. Nobody writes the coal down and everybody asks about the coal.
  - six_belts | Y148-02-06 | Six lines. Anything that stopped. Anything that is about to stop.
    - MillaOnNights | Y148-02-06 | What is the sixth line?
      - six_belts | Y148-02-06 | Whatever the day shift will blame you for.
  - green_helmet | Y148-02-07 | The back of every machine that makes waste. If a crate is nearly full at four, say so, or the day shift will find out at nine with the furnace stopped.
    - MillaOnNights | Y148-02-07 | That one is going on the sheet.
  - canteen_agnes | Y148-02-07 | Write who was on. Not for the record. So the next one knows who to ask.
    - MillaOnNights | Y148-02-07 | That is the best line in this thread and it came from the canteen.
  - bench_light | Y150-11-19 | We kept the sheet for two years and then stopped. I found one from Y148 last month and it was the only record of a lift that was out for a week. We have started again.
    - MillaOnNights | Y150-11-19 | Good.
