# Gearbox at last. What do I do with fast belts?
board: Beginner Questions   date: Y139-05-21   author: grey_belt   status: solved

- grey_belt | Y139-05-21 | I have made my first gearbox after a week of steel. It opens fast belts and the basic assembler. Where do I put the fast belts? I am inclined to replace every belt I own.
  - ivo_p | Y139-05-21 | Do not replace every belt. Replace the stretch that is choking. A fast belt feeding a slow machine just backs up faster.
    - grey_belt | Y139-05-21 | How do I know which stretch is choking?
      - six_belts | Y139-05-21 | Look for the belt that is full from end to end while the rest of the line has gaps in it. That is the choke. Upgrade that. Leave the rest.
        - grey_belt | Y139-05-21 | That is easy enough.
          - six_belts | Y139-05-22 | It is easy once you know. It took me four years.
  - crated_up | Y139-05-22 | And the basic assembler is the bigger prize. It runs the crude recipes twice as fast and it makes electric motors and pistons. Motors open Ampleworth. That is the road to the advanced drill.
    - grey_belt | Y139-05-22 | Motors need coils, steel rods and bearings. I do not have a lathe.
      - tiny_lathe | Y139-05-22 | Then the lathe is your next research, not the belts. Steel rods and bearings come from it. An electric motor is two coils, one steel rod and two bearings.
        - grey_belt | Y139-05-22 | So the gearbox was the easy part.
          - tiny_lathe | Y139-05-23 | The gearbox is always the easy part. That is why people remember it.
  - apprentice_nell | Y144-08-04 | I know this is years old, but I have just done this and the upgrade planner saved me. It swaps a whole area at once and you pay only the difference. It does not know which stretch was choking, though. I marked too much and had to undo it.
    - ivo_p | Y144-08-04 | Undo is z. It has saved me more than any machine.
  - brass_tally | Y139-05-23 | A last thought. A fast belt is not a reward for making a gearbox. It is a part you buy when a belt is the limit. Buy it for the limit. Edit: solved, by the look of it.
