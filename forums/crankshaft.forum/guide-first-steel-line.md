# Guide: your first steel line, step by step
board: Guides & Sticky   date: Y137-03-08   author: ForemanEllis   status: open

- ForemanEllis | Y137-03-08 | This guide is for the operator who has iron plate running and wants steel. It assumes coking is researched and the blast furnace is unlocked. Please read all of it before you place a part.
  **What you need before you start**
  1. Iron ore, crushed or whole, going into a furnace. Crushed grit gives you more metal per lump; whole ore is simpler. Either will do for a first line.
  2. A molder and a press making iron plate steadily. Steel eats plate. Two plates for every steel.
  3. Coke. Three coal make one coke in a furnace once coking is researched. A coke oven does it with two coal and leaves one waste out the back, and needs no fire. If you have the oven, use it.
  4. A trade post behind the blast furnace, set to sell for money. It is the only post that will take waste. It charges a small fee. Do this before you switch the furnace on, not after.
  **The line**
  1. Blast furnace, recipe molten iron. It burns coke as its fuel, not coal. One iron ore in, one molten iron out, in half the time a furnace takes.
  2. Point it straight into a second blast furnace set to molten steel, or cast the iron and press it to plate first. The direct route saves a molder, a press and a belt. It is the better route and it is in the machine's own description.
  3. The steel furnace, coke recipe: two iron plate, one coke, one steel, one waste. Ten seconds.
  4. Molder on steel, press on the ingot if you want a plate, or send the steel on to a rolling mill for sheets and girders.
  **The three mistakes**
  - Running the steel furnace on coal to save coke. Two plate and two coal give one steel and three waste. You have saved a coke and bought two extra waste and their fee.
  - Forgetting the back. A blast furnace with nowhere to put waste stops, and it stops mid-recipe.
  - Casting iron into ingots when the steel furnace could take the metal directly. You are paying for a molder to undo a pour.
