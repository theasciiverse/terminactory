# Kiln is burning more coal than the bricks justify
board: Troubleshooting   date: Y147-01-30   author: kiln_marit   status: open

- kiln_marit | Y147-01-30 | Our kiln goes through a belt of coal in a shift and the brick count does not explain it. I have counted the stone and the bricks twice.
  - halvard_k | Y147-01-30 | Hello. Count the batches, not the bricks. A kiln burns coal for every batch it fires, and a batch is eight runs however many of them you needed. If the stone arrives slowly it fires fewer batches, not smaller ones, and the coal per brick goes up.
    - kiln_marit | Y147-01-30 | So a kiln that waits is a kiln that wastes coal.
      - halvard_k | Y147-01-31 | A kiln that waits just waits. A kiln that fires half-full is a kiln that was fed badly. The fuel is per firing and the firing is eight runs.
        - kiln_marit | Y147-01-31 | We were feeding it in dribs from one belt. We have put a crate in front and it has settled at three batches a minute.
          - halvard_k | Y147-01-31 | Then it is doing what it should. Twenty seconds a batch, twenty-four brick a minute.
  - tilda_m | Y147-02-01 | For the file: three batches a minute is one hundred and forty-four brick an hour on stone at two a brick. If your stone line cannot do two hundred and eighty-eight stone an hour, the kiln is not your problem.
