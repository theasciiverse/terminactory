# Coke oven or coking in the furnace?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y136-11-02   author: halvard_k   status: open

- halvard_k | Y136-11-02 | Hello. I have been making coke in a furnace, three coal for one, and it works. Someone told me a coke oven does it with two coal for one, and no fire. That sounds too good. What is the catch?
  - brass_tally | Y136-11-02 | The catch is one waste out the back for every coke. A coke oven needs a crate or a trade post behind it, and a trade post on money, or it stops exactly as a blast furnace would.
    - halvard_k | Y136-11-03 | So I trade one coal for one waste and a fee.
      - slag_accountant | Y136-11-03 | You trade one coal for one waste and a fee, and you free a furnace. That last part is the real saving. A furnace making coke is a furnace not making metal.
      - ferrous_ann | Y136-11-04 | And the oven does not burn fuel, so it does not care whether the fire is lit. It runs on coal alone, all day, quietly.
  - OldHollowmere | Y136-11-02 | Two coal for one coke is a good bargain. A waste out the back is the price of a good bargain. There is always a price.

- molder_max | Y136-11-09 | I ran the numbers on my own line. A furnace coking is five seconds a coke from three coal. An oven is two seconds a coke from two coal. Twice as fast and a third less coal, and all it asks is a back.
  - halvard_k | Y136-11-10 | Twice as fast?
    - molder_max | Y136-11-10 | Twice as fast. The oven is a small machine and it does one thing.
      - kettle_ron | Y136-11-10 | It draws no EC either. That matters if your grid is already tight. A coking furnace draws nothing but coal, and so does the oven, but the oven was never going to be the machine that broke your grid.
        - ivo_p | Y136-11-11 | Nothing draws EC for coking. It is heat, not motion. The oven is simply better at it.

- pipefitter_uri | Y136-12-01 | Practical note. Put the oven near the furnace block, not near the coal drill. Coke is lighter to move than coal and you will be moving less of it. A belt of coke from the oven to the blast furnace is a short belt if you plan it, and a very long one if you do not.
  - crated_up | Y136-12-01 | And put a crate between the oven and the furnace, because the oven will outrun the furnace and then sit there full.
    - pipefitter_uri | Y136-12-02 | A full oven is a stopped oven only if its front is blocked. Its back is the waste. Do not put the crate where the waste goes.
      - crated_up | Y136-12-02 | I have made that mistake. The crate filled with waste and I could not work out why the coke had stopped.

- TamsinR | Y137-01-08 | I have run both. Furnace coking is fine when your coke demand is small and your furnaces are idle. The moment you are making steel properly, the oven pays for itself in a week of coal. I would not go back.
  - halvard_k | Y137-01-08 | Then I will build one. Thank you all. I did not expect so many answers to a question about coal.
    - TamsinR | Y137-01-09 | It is always about coal.
