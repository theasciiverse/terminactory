# Crate before the blast furnace or behind it?
board: Beginner Questions   date: Y146-10-03   author: apprentice_nell   status: open

- apprentice_nell | Y146-10-03 | Sorry for another one. I am building my first blast furnace and I have two crates. Does the crate go before it, with the plate, or behind it, with the waste?
  - ferrous_ann | Y146-10-03 | Before it, if you have plate and coke arriving unevenly. Behind it you do not want a crate, you want a trade post set to money, because a crate behind a blast furnace fills and then the furnace stops.
    - apprentice_nell | Y146-10-03 | So the crate goes in front and the post goes behind.
      - ferrous_ann | Y146-10-03 | Yes. And put the post close. A long belt of waste to a post is a long belt that will one day be full of waste.
  - crated_up | Y146-10-04 | If you do put a crate behind it as a buffer, belt it to the post rather than arming it. An arm moves one item at a time and a post takes a stack. You will empty a full crate in an afternoon with an arm and in a minute with a belt.
    - apprentice_nell | Y146-10-04 | Belt to the post. Thank you both.
