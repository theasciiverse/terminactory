# Silicon scrap: sell it or remelt it?
board: Waste & Byproducts   date: Y156-06-02   author: halverson_adjacent   status: open

- halverson_adjacent | Y156-06-02 | We have two barrels of silicon scrap a week from the etcher and I have been selling them. Somebody told me the arc furnace will take them back. Which is better?
  - clean_melt | Y156-06-02 | Remelt. Three scrap give two silicon and there is no waste out of the back of that recipe. Selling gets you one and a half a unit; remelting gets you two silicon, which is twelve at the posted rate, from three scrap you would have sold for four and a half.
    - halverson_adjacent | Y156-06-02 | That is not close.
      - clean_melt | Y156-06-02 | It is not close and it is the only clean recipe in the plant. It is also why the arc furnace is on the silicon floor and not in the melting shop.
  - slag_accountant | Y156-06-03 | The fee decides it, as usual. A dirty melt makes two waste for every ingot and the fee is on the back of that. Remelt makes none. You are comparing a price with a cost.
    - halverson_adjacent | Y156-06-03 | We do not have an arc furnace.
      - clean_melt | Y156-06-03 | Then you are selling, and you should be, until you do. Do not build an arc furnace to fix a barrel a week.
  - tiny_lathe | Y156-06-04 | The rough wafer on a lathe also makes scrap. Two wafers and two scrap from two ingots. If you are on a lathe because you have no saw, count the scrap before you call the recipe cheap.
    - halverson_adjacent | Y156-06-04 | We have a saw.
      - tiny_lathe | Y156-06-04 | Then you have no scrap from wafers and this is somebody else's thread.
  - scrap_king_pell | Y156-06-05 | I will buy it either way. But if you can remelt it, remelt it. I would rather sell you the machine that does it than the barrel.
    - halverson_adjacent | Y156-06-05 | That is a strange thing for a scrap man to say.
      - scrap_king_pell | Y156-06-05 | I am a businessman and a resident of this plant. The two do not always agree.
