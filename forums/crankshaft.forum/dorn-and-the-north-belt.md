# Dorn and the north belt
board: Off Topic   date: Y153-06-14   author: hem_of_the_line   status: open

- hem_of_the_line | Y153-06-14 | Third time this week I have come off the late shift and Dorn has been standing at the turn on the north belt, one hand on the rail, watching it run. He does not hear you when you say goodnight. I have worked here a year and a half and I do not know if this is a driller thing or a Dorn thing.
  - TamsinR | Y153-06-14 | He has done it since I came onto nights. Always the same corner, where the belt turns up to the gantry. I have walked past him four hundred times and he has never once looked round.
  - Dorn | Y153-06-15 | The north belt has run since Line 3 started. It has stopped twice that I know of. Once for a gearbox, and once because nobody had put coal on the gantry and it ran empty for a shift. From the turn you can see the whole run and the join and nothing else. I stop there at the end of a shift because a man should look at his work before he goes home. I have never seen it.
  - kettle_ron | Y153-06-15 | That is the most I have ever read from you in twenty years and I do not know what to do with it. I am going to read it again.
  - GerdV | Y153-06-16 | I have known him since he was a lad with a shovel and he has never told me what he is looking at. I stopped asking in about Y140. He is good company at a bench, mind. He just does not spend words.
