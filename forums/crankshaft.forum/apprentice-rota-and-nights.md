# Apprentices and the night rota
board: Off Topic   date: Y157-07-19   author: foreman_hale   status: open

- foreman_hale | Y157-07-19 | How do the rest of you handle apprentices on nights? We have two and the rota says they cannot be alone on a floor, which is fine until somebody is off.
  - MillaOnNights | Y157-07-19 | Pair them with the oldest hand on the floor, not the steadiest. The oldest hand has seen everything and is pleased to be asked.
    - foreman_hale | Y157-07-19 | That is not the answer I expected.
      - MillaOnNights | Y157-07-19 | It is the answer that works.
  - foreman_ferro | Y157-07-20 | On our floor an apprentice on nights does the first round with the shift lead and the last round alone, and writes both down. Nothing has ever gone wrong on the last round that would not have gone wrong anyway.
    - foreman_hale | Y157-07-20 | Writing it down is the part I can enforce.
  - canteen_agnes | Y157-07-20 | Feed them at four. An apprentice who has not eaten makes mistakes you will spend the morning finding.
    - foreman_hale | Y157-07-21 | The canteen is closed at four.
      - canteen_agnes | Y157-07-21 | Not for an apprentice it is not. Send them to the hatch and tell them I said so.
  - green_helmet | Y157-07-22 | Nobody under eighteen alone on a floor with a boiler. That is the only rule I would not bend, and I have bent a great many.
    - foreman_hale | Y157-07-22 | That is already the rule and neither of mine is under eighteen.
  - TamsinR | Y157-07-25 | I was fifteen on a floor with a boiler and nobody thought twice. I would not do it now. That is not an argument against the rota, it is an argument for the rule.
    - foreman_hale | Y157-07-25 | Thank you. That is the first answer that has made me feel better about the rule rather than worse about the rota.
