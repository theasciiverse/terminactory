# Build order megathread: what to build first, and what to build next
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y128-09-17   author: six_belts   status: open

- six_belts | Y128-09-17 | Six belts, six faults, six fixes. This thread is the order I would build in if I started again. Yours will differ. Post yours and say why.
  1. A drill on copper, a belt, a trade post. Watch it for an hour. If it stops, you have learned something cheap.
  2. A furnace in front of the drill, a molder in front of the furnace, a press in front of the molder. Copper plate sells for eight times what ore does.
  3. A solar panel and a pole. The molder and the press both need EC and neither will tell you politely.
  4. A second drill on coal. Your first drill is eating your money.
  5. Coke. Three coal make one coke in a furnace, once you have the research. Coke is what a blast furnace wants.
  After that it depends what you are short of. Post below.

- halvard_k | Y132-02-19 | Hello. I have been told to build steel and I do not know where to start. What comes before the blast furnace?
  - brass_tally | Y132-02-19 | Coke, iron plate, and a place to put the waste. In that order.
    - Coke: three coal in a furnace, once coking is researched. A coke oven does it with two coal and leaves waste, and needs no fire.
    - Iron plate: iron ore into a furnace, the metal into a molder, the ingot into a press.
    - Waste: a trade post set to money behind the furnace, before you start it. Not after.

- ferrous_ann | Y136-07-05 | Steel build, in the order I would do it now.
  1. Two iron furnaces, each with its own molder and press. You will need the plate.
  2. A coke line. Coke oven if you have it, coking in a furnace if you do not.
  3. A blast furnace with a trade post behind it, set to money.
  4. One blast furnace on steel, coke recipe. Two iron plate and one coke, one steel and one waste.
  5. A second blast furnace on iron, pointed straight into the steel one. No pipe, no cast, no press. The molten iron goes in one side and the steel comes out the other.
  That last step is the one people miss. It saves you a molder, a press and a belt.
  - six_belts | Y136-07-05 | That is the build. Number five is the whole trick.
