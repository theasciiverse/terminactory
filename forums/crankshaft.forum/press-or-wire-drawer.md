# Press or wire drawer for copper wire?
board: Beginner Questions   date: Y138-02-27   author: grey_belt   status: solved

- grey_belt | Y138-02-27 | I have a press making two wire from a plate. The wire drawer is available and it says three wire from a plate. Is it worth the research and the space?
  - tiny_lathe | Y138-02-27 | Three from one against two from one is a third more wire from the same plate, and it does it faster. If wire is what you sell, yes. If wire is a step on the way to coils, yes twice, because coils take wire by the armful.
    - grey_belt | Y138-02-27 | And the press?
      - tiny_lathe | Y138-02-27 | The press still makes pipe, plate, gears and girders. You are not replacing it. You are taking one job off it.
  - the_quiet_shift | Y138-02-28 | Also the drawer makes fine wire from wire, which the press cannot do at all. You will need fine wire for lamps, masks and boards later. The research pays for itself twice.
