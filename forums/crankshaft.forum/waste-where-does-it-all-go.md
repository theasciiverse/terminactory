# Waste: where does it all go?
board: Waste & Byproducts   date: Y133-06-02   author: pipefitter_uri   status: open

- pipefitter_uri | Y133-06-02 | This is the thread for waste, because we have now had eleven separate threads about the same white pile and I would rather have one.
  What we know:
  - Waste leaves a machine out the back. Never the front. If the back has nowhere to put it, the machine stops and says so.
  - Nothing burns it. Boilers refuse it, furnaces refuse it, kilns refuse it. Do not build a line that assumes otherwise.
  - A trade post set to sell for money will take it, and charge a small fee for each one. A post set to research, or split, refuses it.
  - Research earns nothing from waste. It has no research value at all.
  - Removing a part that still holds waste costs the same fee, and the machine will ask before an expensive removal.
  Post what you have learned. Keep it civil. Some of us are being paid to deal with this.

- ferrous_ann | Y137-04-03 | Numbers, as promised, though they are only worth what you paid for them.
  Steel from coke: two iron plate and one coke make one steel and one waste. Steel from coal: two iron plate and two coal make one steel and three waste. The coal recipe saves you one coke and costs you two extra waste, plus the fee on both. If coke is cheap that day, coke wins. If coke is scarce, coal wins and you pay at the back.
