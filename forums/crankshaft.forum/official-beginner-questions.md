- brass_tally | Y124-02-11 | This thread is for the small questions that do not deserve their own. There are no stupid ones, only ones we have all asked. A few things first, so we do not answer the same thing forty times.
  1. A drill burns coal. A drill set to coal feeds itself from what it mines. Any other drill needs coal brought to it.
  2. A furnace burns coal too, and it stops when the fire is out. Put coal on the same belt as the ore if you must, and let a filter splitter take the coal off before it reaches the furnace.
  3. A molder and a press need EC. A solar panel and a pole will do for a start. A panel does not join another panel directly; put a pole between them.
  4. Every machine's product leaves by the front. Waste leaves by the back. If the back is blocked, the machine stops, whatever else you have done right.
  5. Ore sells for almost nothing. Melt it, cast it, press it, and sell that.
  Ask away. Someone here is always awake.

- molder_max | Y132-11-08 | Two furnaces into one molder. Is that allowed, or will they fight?
  - brass_tally | Y132-11-08 | They will not fight, but only one can pour at a time, and the other will sit with its metal waiting. One molder can keep up with one copper furnace comfortably, and with two only if you do not mind them taking turns.
    - molder_max | Y132-11-09 | So one molder per furnace is the simple answer.

- brass_tally | Y141-01-08 | A note for the new arrivals, since we are at eleven pages. If your line has stopped and you cannot see why, check these in order: is the machine's front clear, is its back clear, has it got coal, has it got EC, and is the belt behind it full. Nine times in ten it is one of those five, and the machine will tell you which one if you look at it.

- young_hal | Y149-06-18 | My iron furnace takes three ore and gives two molten iron, but my copper furnace takes one ore and gives one. Is the iron furnace broken?

  - sand_and_stone | Y149-06-19 | Crush it and melt the grit. Two grit melt to one iron, and one ore crushes to two grit, so a lump of ore gives more metal as grit than as whole rock. It is the crusher's whole reason for existing.
    - young_hal | Y149-06-19 | So I should crush everything?
      - sand_and_stone | Y149-06-20 | Crush iron when you can spare the EC. Copper gains less, but it still gains. Stone into sand is a different job and you will want it later for glass and concrete.
