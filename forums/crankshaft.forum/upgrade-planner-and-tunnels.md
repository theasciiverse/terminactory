# Upgrade planner and belt tunnels
board: Beginner Questions   date: Y153-09-17   author: mateo_belts   status: open

- mateo_belts | Y153-09-17 | Does the upgrade planner upgrade the belts inside a tunnel, or only the ones you can see?
  - ForemanEllis | Y153-09-17 | Only what is in the area you select, and a tunnel's inside is not in the area. You will upgrade both ends and leave the middle on the old belt, and then wonder why it is still slow.
    - mateo_belts | Y153-09-17 | So tunnels have to be done by hand.
      - second_floor_ivy | Y153-09-18 | Take the entrance up, upgrade, put it back. It is two minutes each and it is the only way I have found.
        - mateo_belts | Y153-09-18 | We have nine.
          - second_floor_ivy | Y153-09-18 | Then do nine. Or lay the fast belt through the tunnel on the day you build it, and never come back.
  - six_belts | Y153-09-19 | Or do not tunnel with basic belt on a line you know will grow. Six faults, six fixes, and one of them is a tunnel you have to dig up twice.
  - hem_of_the_line | Y153-09-20 | We found this out on a Friday afternoon with a full line behind it. The planner said it had done the job and it had done most of it.
    - mateo_belts | Y153-09-20 | How did you find it?
      - hem_of_the_line | Y153-09-20 | The line was the same speed afterwards. That is how you find it.
  - apprentice_tomas | Y153-10-02 | Is there anything else the planner does not reach?
    - ForemanEllis | Y153-10-02 | Drills under an overhang and anything on a floor you have not selected. Read the selection before you agree to the price. It tells you the price before it does the work, which is more than most things do.
