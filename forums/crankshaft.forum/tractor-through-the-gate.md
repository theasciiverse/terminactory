# Will a steam tractor fit through the north gate?
board: Off Topic   date: Y158-03-11   author: foreman_hale   status: open

- foreman_hale | Y158-03-11 | We are taking delivery of a tractor and the haulier says it will not go through the north gate. The gate is a gate. Has anybody moved one?
  - west_gate_pat | Y158-03-11 | The north gate is nine foot at the posts and the road turns inside it. A tractor will go through if it goes through straight and it will not go through on the turn. Use the west gate and sign it in.
    - foreman_hale | Y158-03-11 | The west gate is further from the yard.
      - west_gate_pat | Y158-03-11 | It is further and it is straight. I have watched two cranes and a boiler go through it and I have never watched anything go through the north gate on a turn.
  - silo_sam | Y158-03-12 | I will not carry one on my cart. It does not fit and it does not balance. The haulier is right and he is also charging you for the day.
    - foreman_hale | Y158-03-12 | He is charging us for the day.
      - silo_sam | Y158-03-12 | Then use the west gate and save the afternoon.
  - contract_ines | Y158-03-12 | Read the delivery clause. If the gate is the reason for the delay, the delay is yours and not his. It will be on the second page.
    - foreman_hale | Y158-03-13 | There is no second page.
      - contract_ines | Y158-03-13 | Then somebody has sold you a one-page contract for a tractor. Ask them why.
  - sign_writer | Y158-03-14 | I have painted a plate for the west gate that says TRACTORS AND BOILERS. It is too small and it is the best I can do at that height.
    - foreman_hale | Y158-03-15 | It came through the west gate on Thursday. Thank you all. The plate helped.
