- brass_tally | Y140-02-08 | I will give the long answer, since this thread deserves one.
  Digging buys you two things: richer ore and room. It costs you three: the digging itself, the lifts, and the walk. Of those, the walk is the one people forget. A floor below is not far away in tiles, but it is a different place, and a fault down there is a fault you find an hour later.
  My advice: dig when the top floor is full, not before. Move the drills first, because they gain the most. Leave the presses and assemblers upstairs where you can see them. Put a lift where you will want a second one in a year, not where it fits today.
