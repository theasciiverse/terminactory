# Steel from plate, or direct from molten iron?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y150-08-22   author: foreman_hale   status: open

- foreman_hale | Y150-08-22 | We are putting in steel for the first time on Line 4. The book gives two roads: iron plate and coke into a blast furnace, or molten iron straight into it with coke. Which do you build if you are starting from nothing?
  - ferrous_ann | Y150-08-22 | Direct, if the plant is new. Three molten iron and one coke give one steel and two waste, and you skip the molder and the press that the plate road needs. You save a machine and the EC for it.
    - foreman_hale | Y150-08-22 | And two waste instead of one.
      - ferrous_ann | Y150-08-22 | Two waste for one steel against one waste for one steel. You are trading the fee for a molder and a press. On a new floor that is a good trade. On a floor that already has a plate line it is a bad one.
  - slag_accountant | Y150-08-23 | Put the fee in the sum before you choose. Two waste a steel is one unit of money a steel at the posted rate, which is small until you are making six steel a minute.
    - foreman_hale | Y150-08-23 | Six a minute is the plate road's rate.
      - slag_accountant | Y150-08-23 | It is. The direct road is the same rate with a bigger back.
  - brass_tally | Y150-08-24 | One thing the book does not say plainly. The direct furnace wants molten iron from a blast furnace against it or a pipe from one. If you have only a furnace and a molder, you do not have the direct road, you have a dream.
    - foreman_hale | Y150-08-24 | We have a blast furnace on iron already.
      - brass_tally | Y150-08-25 | Then build it against the steel furnace and skip the pipe. A pipe between two furnaces is a place for a mistake to live.
  - decimal_doreen | Y150-08-26 | The arithmetic, for the file. Direct: three iron and one coke for one steel, ten seconds. Plate: one iron and one coke for one steel, ten seconds, plus a molder, a press, and the iron that the plate took twice.
    - foreman_hale | Y150-08-26 | That last line is not arithmetic.
      - decimal_doreen | Y150-08-26 | No. It is the part everybody leaves out.
  - pinch_point | Y151-01-14 | We built direct and regretted it for a year, then built the plate line and kept both. Direct handles the iron that the plate line cannot take.
    - foreman_hale | Y151-01-15 | Both.
      - pinch_point | Y151-01-15 | Both, and one foreman who knows which is which.
