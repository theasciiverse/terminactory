# Tunnel under the main aisle or long arm over it?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y146-04-18   author: mateo_belts   status: open

- mateo_belts | Y146-04-18 | I need to get a belt across the main aisle without stopping the carts. Tunnel under it or long arm over it?
  - six_belts | Y146-04-18 | Tunnel. An arm over an aisle is an arm at head height and a cart is taller than you think.
    - mateo_belts | Y146-04-18 | The aisle is four tiles wide.
      - six_belts | Y146-04-18 | A tunnel reaches four. Measure the aisle, not the gap.
  - green_helmet | Y146-04-19 | Tunnel, and put a rail on the near side. I have had this argument with three fitters and I have won it every time a cart has gone past.
    - pipefitter_uri | Y146-04-19 | A tunnel also keeps the pipe runs clear. An arm needs the air above it, and the air above an aisle is where every pipe wants to go.
      - mateo_belts | Y146-04-19 | Then it is a tunnel. How deep does it have to be?
        - pipefitter_uri | Y146-04-19 | One tile under is enough. It is not a mine.
  - crated_up | Y146-04-20 | Whatever you build, sign it. A tunnel entrance in an aisle is a thing men trip over for a year.
    - sign_writer | Y146-04-20 | I will paint the plate if you tell me the tile.
