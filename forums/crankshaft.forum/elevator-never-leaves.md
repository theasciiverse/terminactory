# Elevator sits at the bottom and never leaves
board: Troubleshooting   date: Y139-10-05   author: second_floor_ivy   status: solved

- second_floor_ivy | Y139-10-05 | I am writing this because I have answered it four times this month. The car is not broken. It leaves only when it is full. A trickle of items from a single press will never fill it, and the car will sit there all shift looking like a fault.
  What to do: feed it in batches. A crate on the loading floor, filled all morning, emptied into the lift in one go, will fill the car. Or widen the feed so the car fills from three belts at once.
  What not to do: take the lift apart, rebuild it, and tell the foreman it was jammed.
