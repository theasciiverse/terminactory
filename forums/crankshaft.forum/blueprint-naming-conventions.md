# Blueprint names: how do you keep a library usable?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y152-01-14   author: second_floor_ivy   status: open

- second_floor_ivy | Y152-01-14 | I have forty blueprints and half of them are called "copper line 2" and "copper line 2 good". How do the rest of you name them so you can find one in a hurry?
  - sign_writer | Y152-01-14 | Machine first, then rate, then the date you last used it. "molder-24-crate" tells you more than "good one". And a name you cannot read at a glance is a name you will not use.
    - second_floor_ivy | Y152-01-14 | Do you put the rate in the name?
      - sign_writer | Y152-01-14 | Always. A blueprint without a rate is a drawing. With one it is a machine.
  - mateo_belts | Y152-01-15 | I name mine after the person who showed me the layout. There is a "halvard kiln" and a "uri crossing" in my library and I know exactly what both do.
    - halvard_k | Y152-01-15 | Hello. I hope the kiln is the one with the crate.
      - mateo_belts | Y152-01-15 | It is the one with the crate.
  - six_belts | Y152-01-16 | Six belts, six names. Mine are numbered by the fault they fix.
    - second_floor_ivy | Y152-01-16 | That is not a naming scheme, that is a filing system with opinions.
  - TamsinR | Y152-01-17 | I keep a text file beside the library and write one line for each. The library search finds the name; the text file tells me why I kept it.
    - second_floor_ivy | Y152-01-17 | That is the answer I am going to use.
  - hem_of_the_line | Y155-06-02 | Three years later, the text file is the only part that survived. My library got wiped when I moved machines and the notes are what rebuilt it.
    - second_floor_ivy | Y155-06-02 | Noted, and thank you for coming back to say it.
