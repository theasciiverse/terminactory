# How much better is the ore on the lower floor?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y154-10-25   author: sand_and_stone   status: open

- sand_and_stone | Y154-10-25 | We are deciding whether to dig a second floor down. The ore is better, everybody says so. How much better, and does it pay for the lift?
  - six_belts | Y154-10-25 | Each floor down is richer. Two floors down is meaningfully better than one. Whether it pays depends on what your lift costs you in time and what your line is short of.
    - sand_and_stone | Y154-10-25 | Our line is short of iron.
      - six_belts | Y154-10-25 | Then dig. A richer patch is the cheapest drill you will ever buy.
  - Dorn | Y154-10-26 | Deeper is better. Dig.
    - sand_and_stone | Y154-10-26 | That is two words and a plan.
  - foreman_ferro | Y154-10-26 | Count the trips. A lift carries per car and a car wants a full load. One floor further down is one more lift, not one more drill, and a lift that is half full is a lift you paid for twice.
    - sand_and_stone | Y154-10-26 | So fill the car before you add the floor.
      - foreman_ferro | Y154-10-26 | Fill the car, then dig. Not the other way round.
  - ivo_p | Y154-10-27 | And the lift carries power down. If you are digging a floor for the ore and putting panels on it, you will be disappointed. Sunlight does not go underground.
    - sand_and_stone | Y154-10-27 | We know. The lift is the pole.
  - decimal_doreen | Y154-10-28 | A number, since the board likes them. If the deeper patch gives you a third more metal for the same ore, then a line that was short by a quarter is a line that is short by nothing, and a line that was short by half is still short. Dig for the shortage you have, not for the shortage you fear.
    - sand_and_stone | Y154-10-28 | We are short by about a third. We will dig.
