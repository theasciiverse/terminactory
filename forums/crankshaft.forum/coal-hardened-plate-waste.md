# Hardened plate with coal leaves too much waste
board: Waste & Byproducts   date: Y145-09-12   author: ferrous_ann   status: open

- ferrous_ann | Y145-09-12 | A warning for anyone making hardened iron plate. The coke recipe is one plate and one coke, one hardened plate, no waste. The coal recipe is one plate and two coal, one hardened plate and two waste. It is not a small difference. Two waste is four times the fee of none and twice the coal of the coke version.
  - slag_accountant | Y145-09-12 | And the coke version is not free either. Coke costs coal to make. But the coke recipe's waste is zero, and zero is the number you want out the back.
    - ferrous_ann | Y145-09-12 | Exactly. I am not saying coke is free. I am saying the coal version is not cheap.
  - halvard_k | Y145-09-13 | Hello. Is there ever a reason to use the coal recipe?
    - ferrous_ann | Y145-09-13 | When you have no coke and a belt of hardened plate is stopping a line. Then you take the waste and you pay for it, and you know you are paying.
      - halvard_k | Y145-09-13 | So it is an emergency recipe.
        - ferrous_ann | Y145-09-14 | It is an emergency recipe. Put it in the line file that way and the next shift will not use it because it is there.
  - pinch_point | Y145-09-14 | There is a third option nobody uses. Make hardened gear from hardened plate and sell the gear. The gear is worth more than the plate and the waste is already paid. It does not reduce the waste, it just means you are paying it on a more valuable item.
    - ferrous_ann | Y145-09-14 | That is not how waste works. The waste is per recipe, not per value.
