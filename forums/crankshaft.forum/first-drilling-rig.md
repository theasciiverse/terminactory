# First drilling rig: what do I need before I start?
board: Build Orders & Layouts   date: Y152-03-14   author: young_hal   status: open

- young_hal | Y152-03-14 | The plant has asked for a drilling rig and I have been given the job of getting the line ready. I have read the recipe and I am not sure I have understood it. What do I actually need in place before I start placing machines?
  - brass_tally | Y152-03-14 | Four steel girders, two gearboxes, four reinforced concrete, one electric motor and one pump unit. That is the recipe. What it does not tell you is that each of those is a line of its own.
    - young_hal | Y152-03-14 | I had noticed. Which line is the hardest?
      - brass_tally | Y152-03-14 | The pump unit. It needs a motor, two valves, a silo shell and a gauge. The silo shell is three steel sheets and a valve, welded. The valve is a copper pipe and a gear. Nothing about it is difficult. All of it has to arrive at once.
        - molder_max | Y152-03-15 | And the large basic assembler takes twenty seconds for a pump unit, so you will be waiting on parts, not on the machine.
          - young_hal | Y152-03-15 | Twenty seconds is nothing.
            - molder_max | Y152-03-15 | Come back when you want forty.
  - ferrous_ann | Y152-03-14 | The girders are the easy part if you have steel. Four girders is six steel in a rolling mill, or eight in a press. Use the mill.
    - young_hal | Y152-03-14 | And the reinforced concrete?
      - ferrous_ann | Y152-03-14 | Two concrete and an iron rod each, mixed with water. Four of them is eight concrete and four rods. That is a mixer running for a while, and a mixer needs water and EC and sand and stone, and the etchant recipe on the same mixer will leave waste out the back.
        - pipefitter_uri | Y152-03-15 | Put the waste post behind the mixer before you run etchant. And keep the etchant run separate from the concrete run, because the mixer can only do one recipe at a time.
          - young_hal | Y152-03-15 | So a mixer per recipe.
            - pipefitter_uri | Y152-03-15 | A mixer per recipe is the safe answer. One mixer changing recipes is the answer that costs you a shift.
  - second_floor_ivy | Y152-03-16 | Room. A drilling rig is not a small thing to assemble. The large advanced assembler needs a clear square, and the rig has to go somewhere afterwards. Plan the floor before the parts, or you will build a rig facing a wall.
    - young_hal | Y152-03-16 | I have a clear square. I have been saving it.
      - second_floor_ivy | Y152-03-16 | Good. That is the first thing most people forget.
  - sign_writer | Y152-03-17 | A small request from the man who labels things. When the rig is done, put a plate on it that says what it is and the date. In ten years somebody will be glad of it, and it will not be you.
    - young_hal | Y152-03-17 | I will. And I will put my name on it.
      - GerdV | Y152-03-18 | Put your name on it. The first rig in a plant is worth a name.
  - [Deleted User #1180] | Y152-04-02 | It evens out. You will spend a month on the lines and a day on the rig. That is how it should be.
    - brass_tally | Y152-04-02 | It is. The rig is the easy part and everybody wants to start with it.
  - young_hal | Y152-05-19 | It is built. Two months, four girders, two gearboxes, four reinforced concrete, one motor and one pump unit, and a great deal of counting. It is the biggest thing I have ever made and it is on the floor with a plate that has my name on it.
