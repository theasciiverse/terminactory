# Haulage in winter: what do you add to the rate?
board: Haulage   date: Y157-12-03   author: silo_sam   status: open

- silo_sam | Y157-12-03 | The road is mud from the gate to the bridge and the cart is doing half the trips. I want to add a winter rate and I want to know what is fair.
  - freight_fen | Y157-12-03 | Add the trips, not the weather. If you did six a day in summer and three now, the rate doubles and nobody can argue with the arithmetic.
    - silo_sam | Y157-12-03 | They will argue with it.
      - freight_fen | Y157-12-03 | They will argue with a number. They cannot argue with a count.
  - cart_and_crate | Y157-12-04 | We put the winter rate on the board in the autumn and took it off in the spring. Two rates, both posted, and no argument at the gate.
    - silo_sam | Y157-12-04 | Two rates on one board.
      - cart_and_crate | Y157-12-04 | Copperkate keeps the dates at the top. It has worked for six years.
  - copperkate | Y157-12-04 | I will put the winter rate up as a second line with the dates. Post it before the first frost and not after the first complaint.
    - silo_sam | Y157-12-04 | It is posted.
  - west_gate_pat | Y157-12-05 | And tell the gatehouse. A rate the gate does not know is a rate that gets argued at the gate at six in the morning, and I am not at my best at six.
