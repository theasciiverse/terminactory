# Closing a deal at the gate: what do you say?
board: Rates   date: Y160-01-22   author: copperkate   status: open

- copperkate | Y160-01-22 | A young trader asked me what to say at the end of a deal. I have been doing it for thirty-five years and I have never put it in words, so here is an attempt.
  - old_ferrous | Y160-01-22 | Say the number, say the date, say the place, and then stop talking. The man who keeps talking after the number is the man who loses the number.
    - copperkate | Y160-01-22 | That is better than anything I had.
  - barter_bert | Y160-01-23 | Shake the hand and mean it. A deal closed without a hand is a deal that will be reopened by a letter.
    - copperkate | Y160-01-23 | And with a hand?
      - barter_bert | Y160-01-23 | It will still be reopened. It will be reopened politely.
  - contract_ines | Y160-01-23 | Write it down before he leaves the gate. A deal that is remembered differently by Tuesday was never a deal, it was a conversation.
    - copperkate | Y160-01-23 | I have a book for it. I have had the book for thirty years.
  - noon_market_mira | Y160-01-24 | I closed my first deal at the gate last month. I said the number, the date and the place, and then I stopped talking, and it was the longest silence of my life.
    - copperkate | Y160-01-24 | And?
      - noon_market_mira | Y160-01-24 | He said yes. I have been told the silence gets easier.
        - copperkate | Y160-01-24 | It does not. You get better at it.
