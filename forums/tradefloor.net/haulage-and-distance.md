# Haulage and distance: what does a gate charge?
board: Haulage   date: Y145-03-18   author: cart_and_crate   status: open

- cart_and_crate | Y145-03-18 | I run two carts and a boy. I charge by the load, not by the distance, because the distance does not change the work. A man at the north gate and a man at the west gate are the same job if the load is the same. Am I wrong?
  - silo_sam | Y145-03-18 | You are wrong and right. The load is the work, but the gate is the wait. The west gate has a signing-in book and a queue. That is a quarter of an hour and a quarter of an hour is money.
    - cart_and_crate | Y145-03-18 | Then I should charge for the queue.
      - silo_sam | Y145-03-18 | Charge for the queue and call it a queue charge. I do.
  - west_gate_pat | Y145-03-19 | The gatehouse would like it noted that the signing-in book is not a delay, it is a record. Every man who comes through this gate is on a page. If the page is not written, the man was not here.
    - cart_and_crate | Y145-03-19 | I have never said it was not a record. I have said it takes a quarter of an hour.
      - west_gate_pat | Y145-03-19 | It takes four minutes if the man has his name ready.
        - copperkate | Y145-03-19 | Four minutes is not a quarter of an hour. Cart, your queue charge is too high.
          - cart_and_crate | Y145-03-19 | My queue charge is four minutes and a boy.
  - contract_ines | Y145-03-20 | Put the charge on the paper. "Load, queue, waiting" as three lines. A man who can see the three lines will pay all three. A man who sees one number will argue about it.
    - cart_and_crate | Y145-03-20 | I have written three lines and I have already had fewer arguments.
      - contract_ines | Y145-03-20 | Fewer arguments is the whole of my profession.
  - barter_bert | Y145-03-21 | And a load is not a load. A crate of brick and a crate of girders are the same size and not the same weight, and my back knows the difference.
    - cart_and_crate | Y145-03-21 | Your back is not on my cart.
      - barter_bert | Y145-03-21 | My back has been on every cart in this city.
  - old_ferrous | Y145-03-22 | We used to say: weight, then words. Charge for the weight, then explain it. You are all explaining first.
    - freight_fen | Y145-03-22 | That is on my gate. Weight, then words. I did not know it was old.
      - old_ferrous | Y145-03-22 | Nothing is new. The carts are new.
  - cart_and_crate | Y146-08-01 | A year on. I charge load, queue and weight now, and I have lost two customers and gained four. The four are the ones who read the paper.
    - copperkate | Y146-08-01 | The ones who read the paper are the ones worth having.
