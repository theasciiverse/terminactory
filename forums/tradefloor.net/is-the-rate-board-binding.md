# Is the rate board binding, or is it just a habit?
board: Rates   date: Y150-02-11   author: barter_bert   status: open

- barter_bert | Y150-02-11 | A man offered me four and a half for copper plate last week. Kate's board says four. I took it. Am I allowed to say that here?
  - copperkate | Y150-02-11 | You are allowed to say it and it is not a breach. The board is the posted rate. If somebody offers you more than the posted rate, take it and buy me a drink with the difference.
    - barter_bert | Y150-02-12 | I do not drink.
      - copperkate | Y150-02-12 | Then buy the gatehouse a drink. They will not refuse.
  - old_ferrous | Y150-02-12 | The board is a floor and a habit. A floor because nobody can offer less without an argument; a habit because a man who quotes four all his life stops being able to see four and a half when it is in front of him.
    - barter_bert | Y150-02-12 | That is the most useful thing anybody has said to me on this board.
  - contract_ines | Y150-02-13 | Take the four and a half and put it on the paper as four and a half. The temptation is to write four and keep the difference quiet, and that is how a trader loses a supplier.
    - barter_bert | Y150-02-13 | I would not.
      - contract_ines | Y150-02-13 | You would be surprised how many would.
