# What is a gearbox actually worth to a man who needs one?
board: Rates   date: Y148-10-02   author: greta_sells   status: open

- greta_sells | Y148-10-02 | Posted rate is one hundred and twenty. I have sold three this month at one hundred and thirty to a man with a stopped line, and one at one hundred and ten to a man who was not in a hurry. I want to know whether I am being fair or being a pirate.
  - copperkate | Y148-10-02 | You are being a market. One hundred and twenty is the posted rate, not a law. A man with a stopped line is buying a shift, not a gearbox, and a shift is worth more than ten units.
    - greta_sells | Y148-10-02 | That is what I told myself.
      - copperkate | Y148-10-02 | Then you told yourself the truth. Write down what you sold and to whom, and if anybody complains, show them the book.
  - contract_ines | Y148-10-03 | And put the delivery on the paper. A gearbox sold at one hundred and thirty with no delivery date is a gearbox at one hundred and thirty and an argument in a week. Read the second page.
    - greta_sells | Y148-10-03 | There is no second page on a gearbox.
      - contract_ines | Y148-10-03 | Then write one. Date, place, and what happens if it is late. Two lines.
        - greta_sells | Y148-10-03 | I will.
  - barter_bert | Y148-10-03 | I sold a gearbox at ninety once and I have never stopped hearing about it.
    - copperkate | Y148-10-03 | You sold it to me and I have never stopped being grateful.
      - barter_bert | Y148-10-03 | You have brought it up at every rate meeting for six years.
        - copperkate | Y148-10-03 | I have brought it up because it was the best deal on this board and I want it remembered.
  - slag_accountant | Y148-10-04 | The arithmetic, for the pirate question. A gearbox is three iron gears and one steel. Three gears are six iron plate. One steel is two iron plate and a coke. So a gearbox is eight iron plate, a coke, and ten seconds in an assembler. At posted rates that is sixteen for the plate, two and a half for the coke, and the assembler's time. One hundred and twenty is not a pirate's price. It is a good price for a slow machine.
    - greta_sells | Y148-10-04 | Then I am not a pirate.
      - slag_accountant | Y148-10-04 | You are a trader. It is a different word and it is not an insult.
  - old_ferrous | Y148-10-05 | When I started, a gearbox was a rumour and a man who had one was a wizard. One hundred and twenty would have been laughed at as too low. You are all selling wizards' work by the dozen and calling it a rate.
    - greta_sells | Y148-10-05 | Then the rate is a good one and I will stop worrying.
  - waste_lars | Y149-01-19 | Nobody has mentioned the waste in a gearbox, because there is none. That is why it is worth what it is. A product with no waste at the back is a product you can sell at a clean price.
    - slag_accountant | Y149-01-19 | The steel in it made waste. The gearbox did not.
      - waste_lars | Y149-01-19 | That is a fair correction and it spoils a good sentence.
