# Credit and handshakes: how much do you carry?
board: Contracts   date: Y141-11-20   author: old_ferrous   status: open

- old_ferrous | Y141-11-20 | I have carried more credit than I should and I have been paid every time. I know men who carried less and were burned. Is there a rule, or is it luck with a good memory?
  - copperkate | Y141-11-20 | There is a rule. Carry credit for a man you have dealt with twice. Once is a stranger, twice is a trader.
    - old_ferrous | Y141-11-20 | And a man you have dealt with ten times?
      - copperkate | Y141-11-20 | Then carry it and stop worrying. If he were going to cheat you he would have done it when it was cheaper for him.
  - contract_ines | Y141-11-21 | The rule is a number and a date. A hundred units and a fortnight is credit. A thousand and a season is a partnership, and a partnership needs paper.
    - old_ferrous | Y141-11-21 | I have never had paper and I have never needed it.
      - contract_ines | Y141-11-21 | Then you have been lucky with honest men, which is the best kind of luck and not a method.
  - barter_bert | Y141-11-22 | I lend to anybody and I have lost twice. I would still lend. A plant where nobody lends is a plant where everybody waits.
    - old_ferrous | Y141-11-22 | That is a generous sentence from a man who cannot do arithmetic in public.
      - barter_bert | Y141-11-22 | I can do arithmetic. I just cannot do it out loud.
  - waste_lars | Y141-12-02 | I carry credit in one direction only. I take the waste and I send the bill at the end of the month. Nobody has ever refused to pay a waste bill. It is the only debt in this plant that nobody argues about.
    - old_ferrous | Y141-12-02 | Because arguing about it means keeping the waste.
