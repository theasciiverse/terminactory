# Is it disloyal to sell to a rival plant?
board: Contracts   date: Y151-06-22   author: noon_market_mira   status: open

- noon_market_mira | Y151-06-22 | A plant on the other road wants our surplus plate and they are a competitor. My foreman says sell. My father says do not. I am asking the board because I do not want to ask either of them again.
  - copperkate | Y151-06-22 | Sell. A plant that will not sell surplus to a competitor is a plant that will buy at the worst moment from one.
    - noon_market_mira | Y151-06-22 | That is very brisk.
      - copperkate | Y151-06-22 | It is a brisk question.
  - old_ferrous | Y151-06-23 | I sold to a rival for thirty years and drank with him at the gate. He was a rival on the price and a neighbour on the road. Both are true and neither cancels the other.

      - old_ferrous | Y151-06-23 | Your father has never run a surplus.

  - margin_mo | Y151-06-24 | And price it at the posted rate. A discount to a rival is a discount that will be quoted back at you by your own customers.
