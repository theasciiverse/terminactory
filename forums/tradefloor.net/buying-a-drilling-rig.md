# Buying a drilling rig second-hand
board: Rates   date: Y155-07-14   author: silo_sam   status: open

- silo_sam | Y155-07-14 | A plant two roads over is selling a drilling rig for two thousand. The board says three thousand five hundred new. What should I look at before I say yes?
  - scrap_king_pell | Y155-07-14 | Look at the pump unit. A rig is four girders, two gearboxes, four reinforced concrete, a motor and a pump unit, and the pump unit is the part that wears. If the pump unit is tired, you are buying a rig-shaped pile.
    - silo_sam | Y155-07-14 | How do I tell?
      - scrap_king_pell | Y155-07-14 | Ask them to run it for an hour with water in it. A tired pump unit will lose pressure and the rig will dig slower and they will tell you it is the patch.
  - barter_bert | Y155-07-15 | Two thousand is not a bargain, it is a price with a question in it. Offer fifteen hundred and see whether they laugh or think about it.
    - silo_sam | Y155-07-15 | They laughed.
      - barter_bert | Y155-07-15 | Then they think it is worth more than two thousand and you should walk away from the whole thing.
  - contract_ines | Y155-07-15 | And get the paper. A rig sold without a paper is a rig that was somebody else's last month.
    - silo_sam | Y155-07-16 | There is a paper and it has a name on it that is not the man selling it.
      - contract_ines | Y155-07-16 | Then there is your answer, and it is not a price.
