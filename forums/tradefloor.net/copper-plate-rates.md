# Copper plate at four. Is that a fair rate or are we all underselling?
board: Rates   date: Y139-09-15   author: barter_bert   status: open

- barter_bert | Y139-09-15 | Copper plate is four a plate on the posted rate. A plate is one ingot and an ingot is one ore, melted and cast and pressed. Four seems low for three machines' work. Are we underselling?
  - copperkate | Y139-09-15 | You are not underselling, you are looking at the wrong end of the chain. Ore is half a unit. Plate is four. The gain is in the pressing, not in the ore. If you want more per ore, press it into pipe: two plate make one pipe and a pipe is twelve.
    - barter_bert | Y139-09-15 | So pipe is the better product.
      - copperkate | Y139-09-15 | Pipe is the better product at twelve, and wire is two from a plate at three each, which is six from a plate of four. Both beat plate. Plate is what you sell when your presses are busy with something better.
        - tiny_lathe | Y139-09-15 | And coil is four wire into one coil at eighteen, which is four plates and a bit of cordial for eighteen. It is the best thing in the copper chain and it is the reason people build presses they do not need.
          - copperkate | Y139-09-16 | It is the best thing in the copper chain and it is on the rate board. Nobody reads the rate board.
  - slag_accountant | Y139-09-16 | A cost note. Three machines' work is not three machines' cost. The furnace burns coal, the molder and press burn EC. Plate at four covers all of it with a margin, and the margin is thin because everybody can make plate. Thin margins are what a market does to a product everybody can make.
    - barter_bert | Y139-09-16 | So I should make something not everybody can make.
      - slag_accountant | Y139-09-16 | You should make gearboxes. One hundred and twenty, and it takes steel and a crude assembler and most people have neither.
        - barter_bert | Y139-09-16 | I have neither.
          - slag_accountant | Y139-09-16 | Then sell plate and stop complaining about the rate.
  - old_ferrous | Y139-09-17 | I sold plate at three for nine years and was glad of it. Four is a good rate. A rate is not a judgement on your work, it is what the next man will pay.
    - barter_bert | Y139-09-17 | You are right and I am grumbling.
      - copperkate | Y139-09-17 | Grumble in the tavern. The rate board is for numbers.
  - greta_sells | Y140-02-08 | A practical note from somebody who sells plate every week. Four is the rate, and you can get five from a man who is short and in a hurry. You will not get six. If you ask six he will go to the next gate and you will have carried the plate for nothing.
    - barter_bert | Y140-02-08 | Five from a man in a hurry. That is worth knowing.
      - copperkate | Y140-02-08 | It is worth knowing and it is not the posted rate. Both of those things are true.
