# Is twelve for a pipe too high, or are we all undercharging?
board: Rates   date: Y144-05-02   author: greta_sells   status: open

- greta_sells | Y144-05-02 | A pipe is two copper plate and a press run, and plate is four. So the material is eight and the pipe is twelve. That is four for the pressing, and I want to know whether four is right.
  - slag_accountant | Y144-05-02 | Four for the press run is a third of the finished price, which is high for a part that takes two and a half seconds. But a pipe is worth twelve because a pipe is what a pump needs, and a pump is what water needs, and water is what everything needs.
    - greta_sells | Y144-05-02 | That is a story, not a price.
      - slag_accountant | Y144-05-02 | It is both. The price list says twelve and the price list is the only story that matters here.
  - copperkate | Y144-05-03 | Twelve is the posted rate. If you can make pipes for less than twelve you are making money and you should make more pipes. If you cannot, you should not be making pipes.
    - greta_sells | Y144-05-03 | I can make them for eight and a bit.
      - copperkate | Y144-05-03 | Then make them and stop asking whether twelve is fair. Twelve is what the board pays.
  - old_ferrous | Y144-05-03 | I sold pipe at nine for a year in my youth and thought I was clever. I was buying coal with the difference and calling it profit.
    - greta_sells | Y144-05-04 | That is the answer I actually wanted and I do not like it.
