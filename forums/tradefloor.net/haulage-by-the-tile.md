# Haulage by the tile: what should I charge?
board: Haulage   date: Y147-06-03   author: cart_and_crate   status: open

- cart_and_crate | Y147-06-03 | I am the only cart on two roads and I have been charging the same as the man who used to do it. He is gone and I do not know whether his price was right or just old.
  - silo_sam | Y147-06-03 | Price the road, not the man. Count the trip, count what you can carry, and count what you cannot carry on the way back. An empty return is half your day.
    - cart_and_crate | Y147-06-03 | I usually come back empty.
      - silo_sam | Y147-06-03 | Then your price is already too low and you have been paying for the return yourself.
  - freight_fen | Y147-06-04 | Weight first. A cart full of girders and a cart full of brick are the same trip and not the same cart. Charge by the load and say what a load is.
    - cart_and_crate | Y147-06-04 | A load is a load.
      - freight_fen | Y147-06-04 | Then you will be asked to carry a boiler.
  - copperkate | Y147-06-05 | And put the rate on this board when you have decided it. A hauler with a posted rate gets asked twice; a hauler without one gets asked to guess.
    - cart_and_crate | Y147-06-05 | I will post it when I have a number.
      - copperkate | Y147-06-05 | Post the number even if you change it next month. A number you can defend is better than a silence.
