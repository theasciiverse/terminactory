# Gate fees and the weighing: who pays for the scale?
board: Haulage   date: Y153-01-27   author: freight_fen   status: open

- freight_fen | Y153-01-27 | Our gate has started weighing every load and charging by the hundredweight. The scale is old and the gatehouse says it is right. I say it weighs heavy in the rain.
  - west_gate_pat | Y153-01-27 | Your gate is not my gate, but I will say this. A scale that is wrong is worse than no scale, because a man will believe it. Get it checked and put the check on the wall.
    - freight_fen | Y153-01-27 | Who checks a scale?
      - west_gate_pat | Y153-01-27 | A man with a known weight and no interest in the answer. We use a boiler shell. It weighs what it weighs.
  - silo_sam | Y153-01-28 | Weigh the cart empty and full on the same day. If the difference is not the load, the scale is telling you something about the weather.
    - freight_fen | Y153-01-28 | It is three hundred heavier in the rain.
      - silo_sam | Y153-01-28 | Then charge by the load and not by the hundredweight until the scale is fixed.
  - copperkate | Y153-01-29 | And put the fee on the paper. A gate fee that appears at the gate is a fee that will be argued about at the gate.
    - freight_fen | Y153-01-29 | It is on the paper now.
