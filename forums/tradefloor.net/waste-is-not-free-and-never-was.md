# Waste is not free and never was
board: Waste   date: Y130-04-08   author: waste_lars   status: open

- waste_lars | Y130-04-08 | I have been taking waste off this plant for six years and I would like to write down what I have learned, because every year somebody new tells me waste should be free and every year I have to explain it again.
  Waste is the only item on the rate board with a negative price. Minus half a unit. A trade post set to money will take it and charge you. A post set to research or split will refuse it. There is no third option and there is no negotiating, because it is not a price. It is a fee.
