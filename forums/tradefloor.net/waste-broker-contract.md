# A waste contract for a whole plant: how do you price it?
board: Waste   date: Y154-05-06   author: waste_lars   status: open

- waste_lars | Y154-05-06 | A plant wants me to take all their waste for a year at a fixed monthly fee instead of by the item. I have never priced a year before and I am uneasy.
  - slag_accountant | Y154-05-06 | Price the worst month and halve the difference. A fixed fee on a variable volume is a bet, and the only way to win a bet you cannot see is to be paid for the bad outcome.
    - waste_lars | Y154-05-06 | That is not reassuring and it is useful.
  - copperkate | Y154-05-07 | Put a cap on the volume and a rate above it. A fixed fee with no cap is a contract to be ruined by a good year.
    - waste_lars | Y154-05-07 | They will not sign a cap.
      - copperkate | Y154-05-07 | Then they are asking you to carry their risk for free. Say so, politely, and see what they do.
  - contract_ines | Y154-05-07 | And read their second page. A waste contract usually has a clause about what counts as waste, and their definition will not be yours.
    - waste_lars | Y154-05-07 | What would theirs be?
      - contract_ines | Y154-05-07 | Everything that leaves the back of a machine, including a barrel they dropped.
  - waste_lars | Y155-01-19 | Signed with a cap and a rate above it. Eight months in and I have taken two hundred units more than I guessed and been paid for them. The cap saved the year.
