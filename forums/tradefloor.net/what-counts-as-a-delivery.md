# What counts as a delivery: on the cart or on the floor?
board: Haulage   date: Y150-09-30   author: freight_fen   status: open

- freight_fen | Y150-09-30 | A load arrived at the gate and the buyer says it was delivered at the gate and the seller says it was delivered to the door. The paper says "delivered". We are three men and one word.
  - contract_ines | Y150-09-30 | "Delivered" on its own is not a word, it is a hope. It needs a place. Gate, yard, door, floor, and who unloads it.
    - freight_fen | Y150-09-30 | The paper has none of those.
      - contract_ines | Y150-09-30 | Then the paper is the problem and the three of you are arguing about a blank.
  - west_gate_pat | Y150-10-01 | At my gate a delivery is when the load is off the cart and signed for. Until then it is on the road, whatever the paper says.
    - freight_fen | Y150-10-01 | The buyer says it was off the cart at four.
      - west_gate_pat | Y150-10-01 | Then the buyer and I have different books, and I would like to see his.
  - silo_sam | Y150-10-01 | I unloaded at four and I signed at four. If the paper says five, the paper is wrong.
    - freight_fen | Y150-10-02 | It says five and it is in the buyer's hand.
      - contract_ines | Y150-10-02 | Then somebody wrote a time they did not see. Split the difference, write the place on the paper for next time, and keep the men.
  - freight_fen | Y150-10-03 | We split it. The next paper says "delivered to the yard, unloaded by the carrier, signed by the gatehouse". It has not been argued about since.
