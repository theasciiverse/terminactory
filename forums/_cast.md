# T Web forums — cast

Shared across crankshaft.forum, ratiohall.org, tradefloor.net, nightshiftlounge.net and oldposters.org.
Years are in-world. Nobody posts before their join year. Post counts are as of Y160 unless noted.

Abbreviations: CS = crankshaft.forum, RH = ratiohall.org, TF = tradefloor.net, NS = nightshiftlounge.net, OP = oldposters.org.

## The anchors

**brass_tally** — joined Y120, CS. Operator, then foreman of Line 2 from Y134, retired Y152. 9,412 posts.
Signature: *Measure twice. Belt once.*
Careful, courteous, writes long answers with the steps numbered. Early years: hedges everything ("I may be wrong, but", "someone will correct me"). From about Y140 the hedges thin out. After retirement he still answers beginner questions, calmly, as if he had always known. Ends some posts with "It comes out right in the end."

**OldHollowmere** — joined Y120, CS. Miner, third generation; retired Y139. 3,208 posts.
Signature: *The hill remembers what we forget.*
Plain-spoken, short sentences, ends nearly every post with a proverb or a half-proverb. Distrusts anything he did not see built. Never uses the word "optimise". Later posts are shorter and gentler.

**six_belts** — joined Y121, CS. Line operator, never promoted, never wanted to be. 6,740 posts.
Signature: *Six belts, six faults, six fixes.*
Terse. Answers in three lines where others write thirty, and is usually right. Has a habit of numbering faults. Will not argue; simply stops replying when a thread turns.

**ForemanEllis** — joined Y123, CS. Foreman, Line 2, then plant scheduling. 4,051 posts.
Signature: *Write it down or it did not happen.*
Formal, writes like a memo, uses "please" and "kindly". Keeps a notebook of every stoppage. His posts become noticeably tidier after Y145; the notebook is mentioned less.

**pipefitter_uri** — joined Y124, CS. Pipefitter. 5,110 posts.
Signature: *Water finds the joint you did not check.*
Patient, practical, fond of diagrams made of letters. Started as the man who asked whether a pipe could carry two fluids; ended as the man who answers it. Has a long-running joke about a pipe run under the canteen.

**quiet_oswin** — joined Y126, CS and NS. Instrument technician. 1,882 posts.
No signature. Posts perhaps twenty times a year. Every one of them is correct. Never quotes anyone, never says hello.

**TamsinR** — joined Y127, CS. Apprentice at fifteen, operator Y131, shift lead Y144. 7,344 posts.
Signature changes four times: *Apprentice, Line 1* → *Operator, Line 1* → *Shift lead, nights* → *Nothing to add.*
Starts apologetic and eager, becomes dry and funny, then very quiet and very certain. Keeps a list of "things I got wrong" until Y143 and then stops updating it.

**GerdV** — joined Y124, CS. Welder. 3,401 posts.
Signature: *A good seam is a quiet seam.*
Warm, digressive, tells stories about people who have left. The only poster who consistently remembers who used to work where.

**halvard_k** — joined Y129, CS. Kiln operator. 2,655 posts.
Signature: *Fire is patient if you are.*
Methodical. Answers kiln questions with batch counts and coal, never with ratios. Begins posts with "Hello." always.

**MillaOnNights** — joined Y130, CS and NS. Night operator, Line 3. 4,921 posts.
Signature: *The night line does not argue.*
Blunt, kind, tired. Writes at 04:00. Over the years her posts get shorter and her spelling gets better.

**browncoat_bex** — joined Y131, CS. Stores clerk. 2,214 posts.
Signature: *If it is not on the shelf it is on order.*
Cheerful, answers questions about what a part costs and how long a delivery takes. Knows every price list by heart.

**Dorn** — joined Y132, CS. Driller. 3,912 posts.

**ivo_p** — joined Y130, CS and NS. Electrician. 4,308 posts.

## Notes on consistency

- brass_tally retires in Y152; after that he posts as a retired man, never as a working foreman.
- TamsinR is an apprentice Y127–Y130, operator Y131–Y143, shift lead Y144 onward. She was not on the board before Y127; the apprentice on Line 2 in Y124 was apprentice_kit.
- halvard_k trains kiln_marit in Y142 and mentions it once, in Y143.
- canteen_agnes retires Y150; her last posts are about handing the ladle over to soup_ladle.
- Dorn never writes more than eight words before Y150; the long post in Y153 is remarked on by three people.
- GerdV is the only one who keeps asking after absent users by name, which is why he is useful in Old Posters threads.
- ratio_warden is a moderator on Ratio Hall only; on Crankshaft he is an ordinary pedant.
- Nobody in-world ever comments on a change in anyone's manner.
