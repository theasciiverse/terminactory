# Waste per silicon: blast furnace against arc furnace
board: Cost   date: Y148-06-30   author: slag_accountant   status: open

- slag_accountant | Y148-06-30 | Two ways to make silicon and one of them is twice as dirty. Blast furnace: three sand and one coke for one silicon and two waste, every ten seconds. Arc furnace: three sand and one coke for two silicon and one waste, every ten seconds.
  - halverson_adjacent | Y148-06-30 | So per silicon the blast is two waste and the arc is half a waste. Four times the fee on the blast.
