# Solar versus steam: where is the break-even?
board: Power   date: Y143-09-30   author: kettle_ron   status: open

- kettle_ron | Y143-09-30 | A solar panel burns nothing and a boiler burns coal. Everybody says the boiler is for big grids and the panels are for small ones. Where is the line?
  - ratio_warden | Y143-09-30 | There is no published table of EC per panel or per engine, so there is no break-even to calculate. Anyone who posts a figure without saying where it came from will be corrected.
    - kettle_ron | Y143-09-30 | Then I will ask it differently. What did you do, and did it work?
      - ratio_warden | Y143-09-30 | That is a question this board can answer. Proceed.
  - slow_steam | Y143-09-30 | The boiler burns only as much as the grid asks for. That is the whole of it. A boiler on a quiet grid costs almost nothing and a boiler on a busy grid costs coal in proportion. So the question is not "which is cheaper" but "what is my coal worth at three in the morning".
    - kettle_ron | Y143-09-30 | And at three in the morning I would rather not be carrying coal.
      - slow_steam | Y143-09-30 | Then panels. Until you need the power, and then steam.
  - ivo_p | Y143-10-01 | Practical. A panel does not join another panel directly, so a large solar field needs poles and floor. A boiler and an engine take a small footprint and give a great deal of power. On a full floor, steam wins on floor before it wins on coal.
    - second_floor_ivy | Y143-10-01 | And panels cannot go underground. If you dig, you are on steam, or on an elevator carrying power down.
      - kettle_ron | Y143-10-01 | That is the answer I needed. We are digging in the spring.
  - throughput_tony | Y143-10-02 | A method, since there is no table. Count your coal for a week on steam. Count the floor your panels would need for the same grid. Then decide which you are shorter of. That is a real comparison and it does not need an EC figure.
    - ratio_warden | Y143-10-02 | Accepted. This is the correct way to answer a question with missing data.
      - decimal_doreen | Y143-10-02 | And write the counts down. A week of coal is a number you will use for years.
