# Waste per steel: a table
board: Cost   date: Y148-04-22   author: slag_accountant   status: open

- slag_accountant | Y148-04-22 | The table nobody wants and everybody needs. All figures per one steel, from the blast furnace recipes. Waste costs a fee per item and earns no research.
  | Recipe | In | Out | Waste |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | steel, coke | 2 iron plate, 1 coke | 1 steel | 1 |
  | steel, coal | 2 iron plate, 2 coal | 1 steel | 3 |
  | steel, fast | 2 iron plate, 2 coke | 1 steel | 2 |
  | steel, direct | 3 molten iron, 1 coke | 1 steel | 2 |
  The direct recipe skips plate, a molder and a press, which is why its waste looks high. You are paying two waste to save a whole casting and pressing line. On a new plant that is a bargain. On an old one it is a choice.

  - pinch_point | Y149-02-07 | The direct recipe deserves a longer note. Three molten iron and one coke give one steel and two waste. The plate route is two iron plate and one coke for one steel and one waste, but the plate route costs you a molder, a press, and the EC for both. If your plant is new, direct is cheaper. If your plant already has the plate line, direct is a waste of a good line.
