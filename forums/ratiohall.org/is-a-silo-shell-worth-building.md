# Is a silo shell worth building for a pump unit?
board: Cost   date: Y155-08-19   author: margin_mo   status: open

- margin_mo | Y155-08-19 | A pump unit takes a motor, two valves, a silo shell and a gauge. The silo shell is three steel sheet and a valve, and steel sheet is thirty-six. I want to know whether the shell is the expensive part or the motor.
  - slag_accountant | Y155-08-19 | Price it. Silo shell 260, motor 160, valves 100, gauge 35, and a pump unit is 900. So the parts are 555 and the assembling is 345.
    - margin_mo | Y155-08-19 | The assembling is the largest single line.
      - slag_accountant | Y155-08-19 | It always is at the top of the tree, and nobody counts it because it is not an item.
  - clean_melt | Y155-08-20 | The shell is also the machine that gates you. Three steel sheet is three steel and three runs of a rolling mill. If your mill is busy, the shell is your rate.
    - margin_mo | Y155-08-20 | Ours is busy.
      - clean_melt | Y155-08-20 | Then price the second mill against the pump units it unlocks, not against the sheet.
  - decimal_doreen | Y155-08-21 | A silo shell is a valve and three steel sheet. A valve is a copper pipe and an iron gear. If you are short of pipe, the shell is a copper decision and not a steel one.
    - margin_mo | Y155-08-21 | That is the sort of sentence I keep this board for.
  - foreman_ferro | Y155-08-22 | And a pump unit sits on a floor with a welder and a large assembler, both of which want EC. Count the machines before you count the parts.
