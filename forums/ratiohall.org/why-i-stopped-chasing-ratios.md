# Why I stopped chasing ratios
board: Method   date: Y158-01-14   author: pinch_point   status: open

- pinch_point | Y158-01-14 | I have been on this board for twenty-five years. I have posted tables, corrected tables, and once argued for nine pages about a quarter of a second. I want to write down what I actually learned, while I still remember it.
  A ratio tells you what a line can do. It does not tell you what a line will do. The gap between the two is not error, it is people, floor, and the fact that a machine that is exactly fed is a machine with no room for anything.
  What I do now: find the slowest machine, feed it a little more than it needs, and leave the rest alone. I have not built a bad line since I started doing that, and I have built fewer of them.
