# Grit versus whole ore: the actual numbers
board: Throughput   date: Y141-03-02   author: sand_and_stone   status: open

- sand_and_stone | Y141-03-02 | Everyone says grit is better. Here is by how much, from the recipes.
  **Copper, whole ore.** One ore to one molten copper in five seconds. One metal per ore.
  **Copper, grit.** One ore crushes to two grit in four seconds. Three grit melt to two molten copper in two and a half seconds. So three ore give six grit, six grit give four molten copper. Four metal from three ore: one and a third per ore.
  **Iron, whole ore.** Three ore give two molten iron in ten seconds: two thirds of a metal per ore.
  **Iron, grit.** One ore crushes to two grit. Two grit give one molten iron in five seconds. One metal per ore, against two thirds whole. That is half again.
  So copper gains a third and iron gains a half. The crusher's own description says a third more, and it is being modest about iron.
