- unit_ursula | Y147-03-04 | Three answers in eleven years and I am putting all three in the first post, because this thread is now the page I send people to.
  1. A crate converts a trickle into a batch and a batch into a trickle, depending on which side the arm is on.
  2. A crate buys quiet, not throughput. Its unit is minutes of quiet and you get it by dividing its capacity by the demand after it.
  3. A crate that never empties is storage, not a buffer, and it is hiding something from you.
