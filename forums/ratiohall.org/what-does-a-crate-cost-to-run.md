board: Cost   date: Y149-03-02   author: slag_accountant   status: open

- slag_accountant | Y149-03-02 | A crate costs money once and then nothing. That is the whole cost argument and yet I am asked for the running cost every month.
  - margin_mo | Y149-03-02 | People want a per-hour number and there is not one. A crate is capital.
    - slag_accountant | Y149-03-02 | Then say capital. The word frightens them into thinking properly.
  - foreman_ferro | Y149-03-03 | There is a running cost and it is the space. A crate in the wrong place is a belt tile you did not build and a man walking round it twice a shift.

  - decimal_doreen | Y149-03-03 | There is one real running cost. A crate that is nearly full slows nothing; a crate that is full stops the line behind it. The cost of a full crate is the line, for as long as it takes somebody to notice.
