  - throughput_tony | Y147-02-13 | I will offer the only arithmetic available. A second belt requires a splitter, and a splitter shares evenly. Two belts at half rate each are not the same as one belt at full rate, because the splitter does not know which branch is hungry. If one branch backs up, the splitter sends everything down the other, which is a feature and also a surprise.
    - decimal_doreen | Y147-02-13 | That is correct and it is the reason a second belt is not simply "twice a belt".
      - throughput_tony | Y147-02-13 | Then we agree and I have said something useful by accident.
