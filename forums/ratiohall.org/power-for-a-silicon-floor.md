# Power for a silicon floor: what has to be on the grid?
board: Power   date: Y156-04-27   author: ivo_p   status: open

- ivo_p | Y156-04-27 | We are laying out a silicon floor and I want the electrical side agreed before the machines arrive. What draws, what bursts, and what can share a grid?
  - clean_melt | Y156-04-27 | The arc furnace is the big one and it is on all the time. The etcher is on all the time. The crystal grower is on all the time and is the slowest machine in the plant. The saw bursts when a crystal arrives.
    - ivo_p | Y156-04-27 | How long is the burst?
      - clean_melt | Y156-04-27 | Ten seconds a wafer run. It is not the burst that hurts, it is that the saw and the grower are never busy at the same time and the grid is sized for both.
  - slow_steam | Y156-04-28 | Then size for the sum and not for the average. Two machines that take turns still have a minute in which they do not, and that is the minute you will be called about.
    - ivo_p | Y156-04-28 | I would rather have one boiler too many than one explanation too few.
  - kettle_ron | Y156-04-28 | Steam on a silicon floor is a boiler and a pipe and a man who watches it. Solar is a roof you may not have and a lift that carries it down. I know which I would choose.

      - kettle_ron | Y156-04-29 | Then you have the cheaper answer and I will stop talking.
