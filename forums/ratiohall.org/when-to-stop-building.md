# When do you stop building and start running?
board: Method   date: Y158-06-02   author: foreman_ferro   status: open

- foreman_ferro | Y158-06-02 | Our floor has been under construction in some form since Y151. Every year there is one more machine and every year the rate goes up a little. I am starting to think the building is the habit and the running is the job.
  - brass_tally | Y158-06-02 | A line that is being rebuilt is a line that is not being counted. Stop for a quarter, count, and you will find the machine you actually need instead of the one you want.
    - foreman_ferro | Y158-06-02 | A quarter is a long time.
      - brass_tally | Y158-06-02 | It is shorter than a year of building the wrong thing.
  - throughput_tony | Y158-06-03 | There is a test. If your last three changes each gained less than a tenth of the line's rate, you are at the flat part and the next change will gain less again.
    - foreman_ferro | Y158-06-03 | The last three gained about a twentieth each.
      - throughput_tony | Y158-06-03 | Then you are past the flat part and into the noise.
  - half_belt_hana | Y158-06-03 | We stopped in Y155 and it was the best year we had. Nothing improved and nothing broke and we found out what the line actually was.
    - foreman_ferro | Y158-06-04 | What did you do with the year?
      - half_belt_hana | Y158-06-04 | We counted, we cleaned, and we taught two apprentices. The apprentices are why we are ahead now.
  - margin_mo | Y158-06-04 | And the money. A machine that is not built is money you still have, and a rate you do not need is a cost you have taken on.
    - foreman_ferro | Y158-06-04 | I am going to say that to a man who has wanted a fourth kiln since Y154.
      - margin_mo | Y158-06-04 | Say it with the count and he will listen. Say it without one and he will build the kiln.
  - pinch_point | Y158-07-11 | I spent fifteen years building and I have spent the last three running. The running is harder and nobody claps.
    - foreman_ferro | Y158-07-11 | Noted, and I would like that on the board in letters a foot high too.
