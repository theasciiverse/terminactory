# Stop using the word "optimal"
board: Method   date: Y150-05-05   author: ratio_warden   status: locked

- ratio_warden | Y150-05-05 | This thread is locked. I am writing it so that the next person who wants to argue about the word can be sent here and I do not have to have the argument again.
  "Optimal" means best of all possible arrangements. Nobody on this board has measured all possible arrangements. What you mean is one of these:
  - "faster than what I had";
  - "cheaper per steel";
  - "uses less floor";
  - "runs without me watching it";
  - "the best I have found".
  Write which one you mean and the thread gets better immediately. Write "optimal" and I will ask you what you measured, and you will not have measured it.
