# Is grit worth the extra machine?
board: Throughput   date: Y146-02-11   author: throughput_tony   status: open

- throughput_tony | Y146-02-11 | A crusher is one machine and a belt to feed it, and it changes what your furnace gets. Copper: three grit melt to two copper in half the time of one ore to one. Let us settle whether the crusher earns its place.
  - table_herr | Y146-02-11 | The table first.
    whole ore: 1 ore -> 1 copper, five seconds. Twelve a minute.
    grit: 1 ore -> 2 grit, four seconds. 3 grit -> 2 copper, two and a half seconds.
    So a crusher feeding a furnace gives the furnace more than it can melt, and the ore to copper is one and a third to one.
    - throughput_tony | Y146-02-11 | That last figure is the one people miss. You are not making copper faster from nothing. You are making a third more copper from the same rock.
      - table_herr | Y146-02-11 | And paying for a crusher and the EC to run it.
  - pinch_point | Y146-02-12 | The crusher is only worth it if the rock is your limit. If your limit is furnace time, grit makes it worse, because the furnace melts grit faster but you still have one furnace.
    - throughput_tony | Y146-02-12 | That is right and it is the opposite of what half this board will tell you.
  - sand_and_stone | Y146-02-12 | One crusher handles fifteen ore a minute. One furnace eats twelve copper a minute as whole ore or forty-eight as grit. So one crusher cannot feed one furnace on grit. You need two and a half crushers for one furnace to be rock-limited.
    - throughput_tony | Y146-02-13 | Two and a half crushers and their EC. Say that number out loud before you tell an apprentice to crush everything.
      - decimal_doreen | Y146-02-13 | Two and a half crushers, one furnace, and the furnace then melts forty-eight a minute, which is four times what it did. The crushers are the cheap part of that sentence and the furnace is not.
  - half_belt_hana | Y147-01-20 | We crush iron and not copper. Iron is three ore to two metal whole and one ore to one as grit, and iron is what our line is short of.
    - throughput_tony | Y147-01-20 | Iron grit is the better case and I should have said so in the first post. Three ore to two becomes two ore to two.
  - sand_and_stone | Y147-01-21 | And stone to sand is a different job with the same machine. If you are buying a crusher for glass, it pays for itself in a week and the ore argument is a bonus.
