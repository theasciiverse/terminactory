# How do you count a shift properly?
board: Method   date: Y144-09-14   author: half_belt_hana   status: open

- half_belt_hana | Y144-09-14 | I have been asked to count our line for a week and I want to do it once properly. What do you write down, and how often?
  - decimal_doreen | Y144-09-14 | Two numbers and one note. What went in at the start, what came out at the end, and what stopped you. Everything else is decoration.
    - half_belt_hana | Y144-09-14 | How often do you look?
      - decimal_doreen | Y144-09-14 | Twice a shift. Once at the start and once at the end. A man who watches a counter all shift sees nothing but the counter.
  - tilda_m | Y144-09-15 | And write the machine, not the symptom. "Press stopped" is not a count. "Press stopped, no ingot, molder waiting on furnace" is a count.
    - half_belt_hana | Y144-09-15 | That is three lines where I would have written one.
      - tilda_m | Y144-09-15 | And it will save you three days.
  - brass_tally | Y144-09-15 | Count a full week before you change anything. The first three days of a count are always the worst week the line has ever had, and it is not the line, it is the counting.
    - half_belt_hana | Y144-09-16 | That is exactly what has happened. Tuesday was a disaster and nothing was different.
  - ForemanEllis | Y144-09-16 | Please keep the sheet and put the week's number in the line file. We are asked for a rate every quarter and we guess at it, which I have never liked.
    - half_belt_hana | Y144-09-16 | I will send you a copy.
  - half_belt_hana | Y145-02-08 | The week, for anybody reading later. Four thousand one hundred and ninety ingots, two stoppages a shift, and one machine that was the fault in seven of the nine stoppages. It was the molder. It was always the molder.
    - decimal_doreen | Y145-02-08 | Then you have your answer and it took a week.
      - half_belt_hana | Y145-02-09 | It took a week and I have been arguing about it for four years.
  - half_belt_hana | Y152-11-03 | Seven years on, I found the sheet again while clearing a drawer. Four thousand one hundred and ninety. I remembered it as four thousand three hundred and I have been quoting four thousand three hundred at meetings since Y146.
    - tilda_m | Y152-11-03 | The sheet is right and your memory is a meeting.
      - half_belt_hana | Y152-11-03 | I have written the sheet number in the front of the new book. I am not to be trusted with a number I have not looked at.
        - decimal_doreen | Y152-11-04 | Nobody is. That is why we write them down.
