# Copper pipe

**Copper pipe** is pressed from [[copper-plate]] in a press. It is a part, used in building [[valve]]s.

Pipes are the parts line for valves, which are assembled with an [[iron-gear]].

## Tips
- Press pipe in a press next to the copper plate line; it is a quick, simple part.
- Valves are the main reason to make pipe, so match the pipe rate to valve demand.
