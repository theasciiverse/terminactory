# Crusher

**Crusher** grinds ore into grit, and stone into sand. Grit melts further than whole ore does, so a crushed lump yields more metal.

Ore or stone goes in from any side, and the grit or sand leaves by the front. It makes copper grit, iron grit and sand, each one a step toward a better melt or a glass. It runs on EC. The crusher is unlocked by the crusher research, after copper wire has been made.

## Tips
- Crush iron ore before melting it when you can: the grit gives a fair bit more metal per lump.
- Sand from stone is the start of glass, concrete and silicon, so keep a crusher near the stone drills.
- The crusher can push its grit straight into a furnace in front of it, with no belt between.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Grit or sand leaves by the front.
