# Iron rod

**Iron rod** is turned from [[iron-plate]] in a lathe. Rods go into [[screw]]s, [[spring]]s and [[reinforced-concrete]].

The lathe is the only machine that makes rods, so a lathe line is usually built for rods first.

## Tips
- Rods are needed for both springs and screws, so one lathe can supply two lines.
- Reinforced concrete takes a rod in its mix, so put a rod line near the mixer.
