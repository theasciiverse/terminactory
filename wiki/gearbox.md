# Gearbox

**Gearbox** is built in an assembler (the crude one is enough) from [[iron-gear]]s and [[steel]]. Gearboxes go into engine blocks and drilling rigs.

Gearboxes are a middle step in the large goods, so the line from gear and steel to gearbox is a common early build.

## Tips
- Keep the iron gear and steel supply steady so the assembler stays busy.
- Engine blocks and drilling rigs both take gearboxes, so one line can serve two goods.
- Higher assemblers make gearboxes too, only faster.
