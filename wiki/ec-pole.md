# EC pole

**EC pole** is a small post that carries EC from one power source to the machines near it. It is the glue of every electric grid.

A pole reaches out to a little way beyond its own edge, and every electric part in that reach joins its grid. Poles also link power sources that would not reach each other on their own, such as a pair of solar panels that sit apart. Poles cost very little, so it pays to place them generously. They need no fuel and no feeding. Each pole is a simple way to keep your factory powered without moving the panels.

## Tips
- Build a line of poles along a row of machines, so each one is inside a grid.
- Check that every machine is inside some pole's reach, or it sits unpowered.
- Poles join every grid their reach touches, so a line of them can link sources that sit far apart.

## Placement
- A single tile, with no front. It reaches in every direction.
