# Iron grit

**Iron grit** is made by crushing [[iron-ore]] in a crusher. It melts into molten iron in a furnace or a blast furnace, and it gives more metal per ore than the ore itself.

Blast furnaces take iron grit as an input, which makes it a useful feed for iron lines.

## Tips
- Crush iron ore before the furnaces so they get grit instead of raw ore.
- Blast furnaces melt grit quickly, which suits an iron line.
