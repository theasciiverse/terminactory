# Iron gear

**Iron gear** is pressed from [[iron-plate]] in a press. Gears go into gearboxes and valves.

Gears are a simple press job, made from plain iron plate.

## Tips
- A press that makes gears is a good early machine; the parts are simple.
- Gears are needed for both [[gearbox]]es and [[valve]]s, so keep the line fed.
