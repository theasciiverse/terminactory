# Silicon ingot

**Silicon ingot** is a cast bar of silicon, made by a molder from molten silicon.

A [[molder]] casts molten silicon into a silicon ingot. The molten silicon comes from an [[arc-furnace|arc furnace]] or a [[blast-furnace|blast furnace]], and the arc furnace is the cleaner choice. Ingots go into the [[wafer]] line and the [[silicon-crystal|silicon crystal]] grower.

## Tips
- The arc furnace leaves less waste, so it is the nicer way to start the silicon branch.
- Silicon scrap can be remelted in the arc furnace, so nothing needs to be thrown away.
