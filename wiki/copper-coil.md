# Copper coil

**Copper coil** is wound from [[copper-wire]] in a winder, or pressed from wire in a press. Coils go into relays, transformers and electric motors.

Winders are the usual way to make coils, from plain wire. A coil can also be made from [[fine-wire]] in a winder, which uses less wire but needs a wire drawer first.

## Tips
- Coils feed several common parts, so a steady coil line pays off early.
- Fine wire makes coils with less wire, so use it when plain wire runs short.
- Keep coil makers close to the wire drawers that feed them.
