# Crystal grower

**Crystal grower** pulls a silicon crystal out of molten ingots. It is slow, the slowest machine in the game, and it runs on EC.

Ingots go in from any side, and the crystal leaves by the front. Every crystal then goes to the wafer saw, which cuts it into wafers. The grower is unlocked by its research, once silicon ingots have been made, and it belongs to Halverson Smelting.

## Tips
- Expect to wait: one grower is slow, so it is worth building more than one.
- Keep a supply of silicon ingots close, since it takes several per crystal.
- Put a wafer saw straight in front of it, so the crystals go on to be cut without a belt.

## Placement
- Tall rectangle, with a front that turns to face any way. The crystal leaves by the front.
