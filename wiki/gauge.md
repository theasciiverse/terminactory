# Gauge

**Gauge** is a small instrument made from glass, a spring and a copper sheet.

The [[crude-assembler|crude assembler]] joins glass, a [[spring]] and a [[copper-sheet|copper sheet]] into a gauge. Gauges are needed by the [[pump-unit]], the [[generator]] and the [[control-panel]], so they tend to be a stop along the way to something larger.

## Tips
- Glass is one of the parts a gauge needs. If the glass line stops, the gauges stop with it.
- Keep gauges moving on a belt, since several large recipes take them.
