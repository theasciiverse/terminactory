# Winder

**Winder** winds wire into coils, and iron rods into springs. Coils and springs are both parts that many later recipes need.

Wire or rod goes in from any side, and the finished coil or spring leaves by the front. A coil can be wound from copper wire, or from fine wire, which goes further but takes a drawer to make. It runs on EC. The winder is unlocked by its own research, after copper coil has been made.

## Tips
- Fine wire makes more coils from the same copper, so it is worth the drawer if coils are scarce.
- Springs come from iron rods, so keep a lathe feeding the winder.
- Coils open the steam company, so one early winder is a good investment.

## Placement
- Rectangular, longer one way, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
