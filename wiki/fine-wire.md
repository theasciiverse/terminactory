# Fine wire

**Fine wire** is drawn from [[copper-wire]] in a wire drawer. It is a thin wire used in many electronic parts, such as [[lamp]]s, masks and control boards.

Lamps, masks, control boards and many chips all take fine wire, so a drawer for it serves a lot of lines.

## Tips
- Keep a wire drawer for fine wire separate from the one for plain wire.
- Fine wire makes coils with less wire, so it can stretch a coil line.
