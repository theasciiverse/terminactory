# Large advanced assembler

**Large advanced assembler** builds the biggest goods in the game: generators, control panels, drilling rigs and steam tractors. It also builds the large basic goods faster than the large basic assembler does.

Its inputs come in from any side, and products leave by the front. A drilling rig and a steam tractor each need many parts, so give each its own belt. It runs on a lot of EC. It is unlocked by its research, once control boards have been made.

## Tips
- Build the drilling rig's parts first. It needs steel girders, gearboxes, reinforced concrete, a motor and a pump unit.
- A steam tractor is the longest job here, so expect it to take a while.
- It is the path to the big machines, so plan the supply lines before you place it.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
