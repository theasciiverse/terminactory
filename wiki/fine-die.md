# Fine die

**Fine die** is a fine chip cut by a fine etcher from a doped wafer, a fine mask and etchant.

The [[fine-etcher|fine etcher]] cuts a fine die from a [[doped-wafer|doped wafer]], a [[fine-mask|fine mask]] and [[etchant]]. Each run also gives a spare die, and there is no scrap. Fine dies go into the [[microprocessor-x4x2|x4x2 microprocessor]].

## Tips
- The fine etcher is hungry for power, so give it a good supply before you add more machines.
- The spare die is a bonus. Make sure the die line can take it, so the output does not jam.
