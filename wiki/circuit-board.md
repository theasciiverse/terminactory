# Circuit board

**Circuit board** is a board made from ceramic, a copper sheet and fine wire.

The [[advanced-assembler|advanced assembler]] builds a circuit board from [[ceramic]], a [[copper-sheet|copper sheet]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. Circuit boards go into the [[keyboard]], the [[transistor-board|transistor board]] and the [[mainboard]].

## Tips
- Circuit boards are needed by several different recipes, so their line is worth keeping steady.
- Ceramic comes from the kiln, so check the brick and sand lines when circuit boards run short.
