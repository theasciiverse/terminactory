# Kiln

**Kiln** fires a whole batch of runs at once, burning coal. It turns stone into brick, sand into glass, and it bakes the tubes, dopant, doped wafers and ceramic.

Inputs come in from any side, and the batch leaves by the front. Because it fires a full batch at a time, it waits until it holds the inputs for the whole batch before it starts. Waste comes out the back when baking tubes. The kiln is unlocked by its research, once concrete has been made.

## Tips
- Keep a steady supply of inputs, since it waits for the full batch before it fires.
- Keep coal on a belt into the kiln, because every batch burns it.
- Tube baking leaves waste out the back, so give that side a way out.

## Placement
- Rectangular, longer one way, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front and waste out the back.
