# Microprocessor (x4x2)

**Microprocessor (x4x2)** is a fine chip made from a fine die, ceramic and fine wire.

The [[precision-assembler|precision assembler]] builds a fine microprocessor from a [[fine-die|fine die]], [[ceramic]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. It is the finer version of the chip, and it goes into the [[mainboard]] as the main chip in the best recipe.

## Tips
- The fine microprocessor is a step up from the coarse chip. It is worth the wait.
- Keep the fine die line tidy, since every fine microprocessor uses one.
