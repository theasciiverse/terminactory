# Etcher

**Etcher** prints a mask's pattern onto a doped wafer with etchant, and cuts out dies. Each wafer gives dies and scrap, and the scrap is worth keeping.

It runs on EC, and it draws a lot of it while working. The scrap is a product rather than waste: it sells, and it can be remelted. Dies go into the precision assembler to make microprocessors. It is unlocked by its research, once a control board has been made, and it belongs to the Lattice and Finch company.

## Tips
- Feed it a doped wafer, a mask and etchant together, or it waits.
- Keep the scrap moving, since it sells and remelts in the arc furnace.
- Its power draw is heavy, so put it on a grid with room to spare.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Dies and scrap leave by the front.
