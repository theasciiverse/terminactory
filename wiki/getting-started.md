# Getting started

**Getting started** is the short road from an empty map to your first working line of parts.

Run `tfac` in a terminal to continue your last save, or `tfac --demo` to start beside a small factory that already works. There are no ore patches: a [[cheap-drill|cheap drill]] works anywhere on the ground, and you pick its ore with the set-up key, such as [[copper-ore|copper ore]] or [[coal]]. Drills burn coal, so give them some. Carry the ore away on a [[basic-belt|basic belt]]. Ore sells for very little, so a [[furnace]] and a molder, and then a [[press]], soon turn it into something better. Molders and belts need EC, so put down a solar panel and a pole.

## Tips
- Build a small line first: drill, belt, and a [[trade-post|trade post]] at the end, set to sell. Watch it run before you add more.
- Press `?` at any time for the key list, and `t` to open the research tree.
- Save often by hand with Ctrl+S. The game also saves itself, but a keypress costs nothing.
