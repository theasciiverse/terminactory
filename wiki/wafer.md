# Wafer

**Wafer** is a thin slice of silicon, cut from a crystal or sliced from an ingot.

A wafer comes from a [[wafer-saw|wafer saw]], which cuts a [[silicon-crystal|silicon crystal]] with a [[fine-wire|fine wire]] and leaves no scrap, or from a [[lathe]], which slices a [[silicon-ingot|silicon ingot]] more slowly and leaves [[silicon-scrap|scrap]] behind. Wafers go into the kiln, where they are fired with [[dopant]] into [[doped-wafer|doped wafers]].

## Tips
- The wafer saw is the clean route, and it is quicker than the lathe, with nothing left over.
- Wafers go to the kiln next, so keeping them close saves a walk.
