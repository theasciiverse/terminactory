# Naphtha

**Naphtha** is the light fluid a [[still]] takes out of [[crude-oil|crude oil]]. It burns easily and it is the feed for the next step: another run of the still turns it into [[ethylene]].

Naphtha flows in pipes and holds its own pipe run, so keep it apart from crude oil and waste water. It can also be sold, at a much better price than the crude oil it came from. It is not a fuel for any machine.

## Tips
- Put a second still on the naphtha line to make ethylene, rather than belting it anywhere.
- Keep its pipe run short: the still has one fluid out and one fluid in.
- Sell the surplus if the ethylene line backs up.
