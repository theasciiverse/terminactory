# Large kiln

**Large kiln** is a much bigger kiln. It fires a far larger batch at once, and it burns a great deal more coal than a kiln does.

It makes the same things as the kiln: brick, glass, baked tubes, dopant, doped wafers and ceramic. Inputs and outputs work the same way, with the batch waiting until it holds the inputs for a full firing. It is a big machine, so it suits a line that already runs a full kiln. It is unlocked by its research, once concrete has been made.

## Tips
- Give it a coal belt sized for its big batches, since it burns coal quickly.
- It fires in large batches, so keep its inputs flowing or it waits for the whole batch.
- Baked tubes leave waste out the back, so give that side room.

## Placement
- Large rectangle, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front and waste out the back.
