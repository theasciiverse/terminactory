# Steam engine

**Steam engine** turns steam into EC for its grid. It is the heavy-duty source of power that takes over from the solar panels.

It takes steam from a boiler or a pipe, and it gives out power as far as its grid needs it. The engine burns only the steam it actually uses, so a quiet grid barely touches the boiler. Power from the engine is shared with every panel and pole on the same grid. It is unlocked by the steam research, together with the boiler.

## Tips
- Put the engine right against the boiler's front, so the steam goes straight in.
- Engines and solar panels share one grid, so the engine tops up whatever the panels miss.
- Add a second engine when the grid runs short on power, and check whether the grid is short on EC when machines slow down.

## Placement
- Square, with no set front. It takes steam from whatever touches it.
