# Copper plate

**Copper plate** is pressed from [[copper-ingot]] in a press. It is the base for most copper parts.

Plate becomes [[copper-wire]] and [[copper-pipe]], and it is rolled or pressed into [[copper-sheet]]. Wire drawers also turn plate straight into wire.

## Tips
- Put the press close to the molders so ingots do not travel far.
- Copper sheet can be pressed from plate without a rolling mill, though it takes more plate.
- Use a splitter to share plate between the wire and pipe lines.
