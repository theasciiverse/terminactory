# Cheap drill

**Cheap drill** is the first machine most players place: a drill that digs ore out of the ground and pushes it out of its front.

It works anywhere on the ground, is open from the start, and is the slowest of the drills. It cuts iron more slowly than a basic drill, which is still plenty to start. Like the other coal drills, it burns coal to keep going, and a drill set to coal fuels itself from what it mines. The ore it digs goes onto a belt or straight into the next machine. It leads to the basic drill and the furnaces that melt what it mines.

## Tips
- Give it a belt or a furnace in front straight away, or the ore piles up and it stops.
- Its setting cycles through the ores it can mine. A coal drill keeps its own fire going, and a coal drill pointed at another drill passes it coal.
- Drills need open ground, so they cannot go on an upper floor. Underground floors are fine, and the ore there is richer.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Ore leaves by the front only.
