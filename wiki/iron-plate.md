# Iron plate

**Iron plate** is pressed from [[iron-ingot]] in a press. It is the starting point for most iron goods and for steel.

Plate becomes [[iron-gear]]s, [[iron-rod]]s and [[iron-sheet]]s. It is also an input to [[steel]] in a blast furnace and to [[hardened-iron-plate]].

## Tips
- Gears and rods both start from plate, so put them on the same line when you can.
- Blast furnaces use plate as an input, so keep plate flowing to them as well.
- Iron sheet is quicker from a rolling mill than from a press.
