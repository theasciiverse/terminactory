# Fuel oil

**Fuel oil** is the heavy fluid left at the bottom of the barrel. It comes from the refinery, which arrives later; nothing makes it yet.

A [[still]] can use it with coal to make [[carbon-black|carbon black]], and that recipe waits until the refinery is built. Fuel oil is not a fuel for any machine in this workshop: the boilers and kilns still burn coal and coke only.

## Tips
- The carbon black recipe is in the book, but it cannot run until fuel oil exists.
- Do not plan a line around fuel oil yet; the refinery comes first.
