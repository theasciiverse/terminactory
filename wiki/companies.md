# Companies

**Companies** are the shops that sell you parts and unlock them. Each one has a key item, and making it opens the company.

**Hollowmere Mining Co.** sells cheap drills.
Key: none, open from the start.

**Brannock & Daughters** sells furnaces, molders, presses, belts, splitters, arms, crates, panels and poles, and the logistics and tool research.
Key: none, open from the start.

**Ashgrove Ironstone** sells the basic drill and the crusher.
Key: [[copper-wire|copper wire]].

**Cinderhall Foundry** sells the blast furnace and the coke oven.
Key: [[coke]].

**Wellbourne Waterworks** sells pumps, pipes, the mixer and the advanced pump.
Key: [[copper-pipe|copper pipe]].

**Kettering & Vane** sells boilers, steam engines, basic solar panels, the wire drawer and the winder.
Key: [[copper-coil|copper coil]].

**Pillar & Sons** sells support beams, the rolling mill and the welder.
Key: [[steel-girder|steel girder]].

**Underhill Excavation** sells excavation, the kiln and the large kiln.
Key: [[concrete]].

**Gearwright Machine Works** sells the crude assembler and the lathe.
Key: [[iron-gear|iron gear]].

**Swiftline Conveyors** sells fast belts, the basic assembler and the upgrade planner.
Key: [[gearbox]].

**Ampleworth Motor Works** sells the advanced drill and the large basic assembler and drill.
Key: [[electric-motor|electric motor]].

**Lattice & Finch** sells the advanced assembler, the precision assembler, the etcher and the large advanced machines.
Key: [[control-board|control board]].

**Sablon Glassworks** sells the evacuator.
Key: [[tube]].

**Halverson Smelting** sells the arc furnace, the crystal grower, the wafer saw, the still and the liquid trader.
Key: [[silicon-ingot|silicon ingot]] or [[crude-oil|crude oil]].

**Meridian Instruments** sells the fine etcher and the large precision assembler.
Key: [[microprocessor-x4x1|microprocessor (x4x1)]].

## Tips
- Each company's letter is worth reading. They are short and a little funny.
- Pick the key that matches what your factory already makes.
