# Die

**Die** is a cut chip made by an etcher from a doped wafer, a mask and etchant.

An [[etcher|etcher]] cuts a die from a [[doped-wafer|doped wafer]], a [[mask]] and [[etchant]]. It also leaves [[silicon-scrap|scrap]] behind, and the scrap is worth keeping. Dies go into the [[microprocessor-x4x1|microprocessor]] and [[ram-chip|RAM chip]] recipes.

## Tips
- The etcher makes dies and scrap together, so make room for both on the output side.
- The fine etcher makes a fine die and a spare die with no scrap, which is the cleaner choice once you have it.
