# Basic solar panel

**Basic solar panel** is the better solar panel. It gives more EC than the cheap one, and it reaches further, so fewer poles are needed to link it up.

It needs no fuel, and like the cheap panel it does not join another panel directly. Poles still go between panels to link them. It costs more than the cheap panel, because you pay for reach rather than raw power. It is unlocked by its research, which comes early in the power branch, beneath the copper coil.

## Tips
- Choose it when the panels are spread out, since its reach saves poles.
- It joins other panels only through poles, so plan the poles before you place the panels.
- Panels suit the early grid well, and steam can take over later on.

## Placement
- Square, with no set front. It sends its power out in every direction within its reach.
