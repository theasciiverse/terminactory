# Large precision assembler

**Large precision assembler** builds the computer, and it makes the large advanced goods faster. It does nothing below that level.

It is a big assembler, and its inputs come in from any side, with the finished computer leaving by the front. A computer needs a mainboard, a transformer, a terminal, a disk drive and a frame, so it sits at the end of a very long line. It draws a very large amount of EC. It is unlocked by its research, once a microprocessor (x4x1) has been made, and it belongs to Meridian Instruments.

## Tips
- Build the parts of the computer first, since it needs several different inputs at once.
- It draws more power than any other assembler, so make sure its grid is ready.
- Because it does the large advanced goods faster too, it can speed up the drilling rig line.

## Placement
- Large square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
