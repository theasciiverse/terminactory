# Crude oil

**Crude oil** is the thick, black fluid that an [[oil-extractor|oil extractor]] draws out of the ground. It is not used as it comes: a [[still]] splits it into lighter fluids.

Crude oil travels through pipes like any other fluid, and a pipe run holds it alone, so give the oil its own run from the extractor to the still. Making the first unit of crude oil opens Halverson Smelting, which sells the machines that work with it. It has no other use yet; the refinery arrives later and will take it further.

## Tips
- Put the extractor underground and the still down there too, so the pipe run is short.
- Keep oil away from water runs: one pipe network holds one fluid at a time.
- The still takes crude oil from any side but the front.
