# Steel

**Steel** is cast by a molder from molten steel, which a blast furnace makes from [[iron-plate]] and coke or coal, or straight from molten iron and coke. Steel goes into girders, gearboxes, rods and sheets.

The blast furnace leaves [[waste]] out the back. Molten steel can also be piped out of the furnace, so it does not need a press or a second melt.

## Tips
- A blast furnace set up for direct steel can take molten iron from an iron blast furnace beside it, skipping the casting step.
- Coke gives steel with less waste than coal.
- Keep a waste belt running out the back, or the blast furnace will stop.
