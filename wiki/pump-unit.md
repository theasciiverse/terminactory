# Pump unit

**Pump unit** is a large machine part made from an electric motor, valves, a silo shell and a gauge.

The [[large-basic-assembler|large basic assembler]] builds a pump unit from an [[electric-motor|electric motor]], [[valve|valves]], a [[silo-shell|silo shell]] and a [[gauge]]. It is one of the first big goods, and it is needed by the [[drilling-rig]].

## Tips
- Pump units ask for a lot of parts at once, so a small buffer of each input helps the assembler keep going.
- Keep the valve and gauge lines close to the assembler, since the pump unit takes both.
