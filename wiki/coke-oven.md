# Coke oven

**Coke oven** cooks its own coal into coke. It needs no fire to feed and no EC, but it leaves waste out the back.

Coal goes in from any side, and coke leaves by the front. It makes the same coke the furnace does, though it uses less coal for each one. The catch is the waste out the back, which has to go somewhere. It is unlocked by the coke oven research, after coke has been made.

## Tips
- Keep a crate or a trade post behind it, so the waste never stops the oven.
- Use it to make coke without tying up a furnace's fire.
- The oven runs on coal alone, so a coal belt is all it needs to keep working.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Coke leaves by the front and waste out the back.
