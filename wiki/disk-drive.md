# Disk drive

**Disk drive** is a storage unit made from an electric motor, a steel sheet and a transistor board.

The [[basic-assembler|basic assembler]] builds a disk drive from an [[electric-motor|electric motor]], a [[steel-sheet|steel sheet]] and a [[transistor-board|transistor board]]. Disk drives go into the [[computer]], which is the only recipe that uses them.

## Tips
- A disk drive needs a motor and a transistor board, so plan both lines before you build the drive.
- The disk drive is the only part that uses the motor and transistor board together.
