# Evacuator

**Evacuator** pulls the air out of glass with a water jet from a pipe. It makes tubes with no waste and no fire, in pairs.

It takes lamps, copper sheets and fine wire, plus water from a pipe. Tubes leave by the front. Because it uses water rather than coal, it sits well in a line that already runs a pipe. It is unlocked by its research, once a tube has been made, and it belongs to the Sablon glassworks.

## Tips
- Use it instead of the baked kiln recipe, which leaves waste behind.
- Keep a pipe with water running to it, or the evacuator stops.
- Its paired output makes it an efficient way to get tubes for tube boards.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Tubes leave by the front, and water comes in from a pipe on any side but the front.
