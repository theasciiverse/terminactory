# Copper ore

**Copper ore** is a mined rock that holds copper metal. Drills dig it out of the ground, and it is the first step in most copper goods.

Furnaces melt it straight into molten copper, which molders cast into ingots. Crushers can also grind it into [[copper-grit]], which melts into more metal than the ore it came from.

## Tips
- Crush spare ore into grit when the furnaces are busy; it gives more metal when melted.
- Keep ore on its own belt so a stalled furnace does not block the drills.
