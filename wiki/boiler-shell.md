# Boiler shell

**Boiler shell** is a steel casing made from a steel sheet and bricks.

The [[welder]] joins a [[steel-sheet|steel sheet]] and [[brick|bricks]] into a boiler shell. Boiler shells go into the [[engine-block]], so they sit at the end of a short line that starts with steel.

## Tips
- The welder only draws power while it works, which makes it a gentle addition to a quiet power grid.
- Keep the brick and steel sheet lines close to the welder so the shells do not have to travel far.
