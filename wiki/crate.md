# Crate

**Crate** is a simple box that holds items until something takes them out. Belts and arms feed it from any side, and it pushes its items out of its front.

It is a handy buffer: when a machine or a belt runs faster than the next stage, a crate soaks up the surplus. Items inside a crate are lost if you remove it, so empty it first. It is also where waste can sit while you work out how to get rid of it. A warehouse is the same idea at a much larger size.

## Tips
- Put a crate between a fast drill and a slow machine so the drill does not stall.
- Remove a crate only once it is empty, unless you are happy to lose what is inside.
- A filtering arm can pull items out of a crate and onto the next belt.

## Placement
- A single tile with a front that turns to face any way. Items go in from any side and leave by the front.
