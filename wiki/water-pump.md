# Water pump

**Water pump** draws water out of the ground and pushes it into any pipe it touches. It runs on EC and needs no fuel.

It works anywhere on the ground or underground, though not on an upper floor. Its water goes into a pipe beside it, on any side. The water it adds is steady, so a single pump can feed a good number of machines. It is unlocked by the pumps research, after copper pipe has been made. The advanced pump is the better version, with much more water.

## Tips
- Place it at the edge of a pipe run, touching a pipe, so the water flows straight in.
- Pumps need open ground, so check the spot before you build.
- One pump with a pipe run can water a whole row of machines.

## Placement
- Long, and turning it swaps its width and height. Water goes into any pipe on any of its sides.
