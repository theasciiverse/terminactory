# Advanced assembler

**Advanced assembler** builds control boards, and then the tube boards, circuit boards, transistors, transistor boards and masks. It also runs everything below it much faster.

Inputs come in from any side, and the boards leave by the front. Control boards are the first real chip-stage part, and the proper recipe is here, so the slow hand-wired version is no longer needed. It runs on EC. It is unlocked by its research, once control boards have been made.

## Tips
- Make control boards here rather than by hand, since the hand-wired recipe is far slower.
- Tube boards need a steady supply of tubes from the kiln or the evacuator.
- The advanced assembler hides the hand-wired recipe, so you will not see it once you have this machine.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
