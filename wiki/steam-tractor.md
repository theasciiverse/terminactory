# Steam tractor

**Steam tractor** is a crown item, one of the biggest goods the factory makes.

The [[large-advanced-assembler|large advanced assembler]] builds a steam tractor from an [[engine-block|engine block]], a [[frame]], [[hardened-gear|hardened gears]], a [[control-panel|control panel]] and a [[generator]]. Crown items are only for selling and research, so a steam tractor is a trophy as much as a part.

## Tips
- Crown items are worth a great deal to sell and to research, so keep a tidy record of what goes into one.
- A steam tractor is a long chain of parts. Build the chain one part at a time, and the final assembly will feel much calmer.
