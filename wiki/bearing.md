# Bearing

**Bearing** is turned from [[steel-rod]] in a lathe. Bearings go into [[electric-motor]]s.

The lathe makes bearings from steel rod, which makes it a second step in the steel line.

## Tips
- Electric motors take bearings, so the bearing line should keep up with the motor line.
- Bearings are a quick lathe job, so one lathe can make them as needed.
- Keep steel rod coming to the lathe, or bearings stop.
