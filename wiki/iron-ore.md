# Iron ore

**Iron ore** is a mined rock with iron in it. Drills dig it out, though a cheap drill mines it more slowly than a basic drill does.

Furnaces melt it into molten iron, and blast furnaces melt it much faster. Crushers can grind it into [[iron-grit]], which gives more metal per ore.

## Tips
- Blast furnaces suit iron ore best; they melt it quickly.
- Grit is the better use for ore, since it melts into more metal.
