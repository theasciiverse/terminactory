# Advanced pump

**Advanced pump** draws water out of the ground and pushes it into pipes, like the water pump, but with much more water. It runs on EC.

It works on open ground and fills any pipe it touches, on any side. Its extra water lets one pump supply a long run of machines that the basic pump would starve. It is unlocked by its research, after copper pipe has been made.

## Tips
- Use it to feed a big mixer or boiler line from a single source.
- It needs open ground beneath it, so check the spot before you build.
- Let it touch the pipe you want filled, since it adds water to whatever pipe is beside it.

## Placement
- Square. Water goes into any pipe on any of its sides.
