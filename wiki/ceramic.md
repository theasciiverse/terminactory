# Ceramic

**Ceramic** is a hard material fired in a kiln from brick and sand.

A [[kiln]] fires [[brick|bricks]] and [[sand]] into ceramic. Ceramic is used by the [[circuit-board|circuit board]] and by all the [[microprocessor-x4x1|microprocessor]] and [[ram-chip|RAM chip]] recipes, so it is one of the more widely used parts of the chip branch.

## Tips
- Ceramic needs both brick and sand, so check both lines before you wonder why the kiln is quiet.
- Keep the ceramic output on its own belt, since several recipes draw from it.
