# Carbon black

**Carbon black** is a fine black powder made in a [[still]] from coal and [[fuel-oil|fuel oil]]. It is a product, not waste: it sells, and it will be used once the later oil machines arrive.

For now it waits for a use. The [[still]] that makes it also leaves [[waste]] out the back, and fuel oil itself arrives with the refinery, so carbon black is the last recipe of the oil line to come online.

## Tips
- Keep a crate or a trader ready for it; it has no recipe use yet.
- The recipe needs fuel oil, so it stays idle until the refinery is built.
