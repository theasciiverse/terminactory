# Lamp matrix

**Lamp matrix** is a grid of lamps, made with a copper sheet and fine wire.

The [[crude-assembler|crude assembler]] builds a lamp matrix from a lot of [[lamp|lamps]], a [[copper-sheet|copper sheet]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. Lamp matrices go into the [[terminal]].

## Tips
- Lamps are the main part of a lamp matrix, so feed that line steadily and let the wire follow.
- Lamp matrices take a lot of lamps, so the lamp line is the one to watch.
