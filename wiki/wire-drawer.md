# Wire drawer

**Wire drawer** pulls plate into wire, and wire into fine wire. It is the machine that makes the thin wire the electronics later depend on.

Plate or wire goes in from any side, and the drawn wire leaves by the front. It makes copper wire from plate, and fine wire from copper wire. It runs on EC. The wire drawer is unlocked by its own research, after copper coil has been made.

## Tips
- Feed it plate for plain wire, and wire for the fine kind.
- The fine wire is what the assemblers and the lamp recipes need, so it is worth a drawer of its own.
- Keep its plate supply steady, or it sits waiting.

## Placement
- Rectangular, longer one way, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
