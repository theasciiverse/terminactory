# Trade post

**Trade post** is where your factory sells what it makes, and where waste is paid to be taken away. Belts feed it items, and it turns them into money or research.

Each trade post has a sell mode, changed with the set-up key: sell for money, split between money and research, or sell for research. Middle-clicking copies the mode to the next trade post you place. Research sales fade as the same item sells again and again, so variety pays. Waste is taken only by a post set to money, and it costs a small fee. It is the one place your products stop being parts and start being cash.

## Tips
- Give each product its own belt, so you can see what sells and what backs up.
- Choose the sell mode for the stage you are at: money when you need cash, research when you need the next unlock.
- A trade post can refuse waste for a while, and posts set to research or split refuse it outright, so keep a second way out for it.

## Placement
- A large square, with no set front. It takes items from any side.
