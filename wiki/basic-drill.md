# Basic drill

**Basic drill** is the faster drill that follows the cheap one. It digs ore out of the ground and pushes it out of its front, and it runs on coal.

Once you have the research, it mines faster than the cheap drill, and it cuts iron much faster. It needs open ground, and it burns coal to keep going. It is unlocked by research, after copper wire has been made.

## Tips
- Keep a coal supply near it: it burns fuel while it works, and it stops without it.
- A basic drill on coal feeds itself, which is handy for a coal patch by the furnaces.
- Make sure its front has a belt or a machine that takes ore, or the drill fills up and waits.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Ore leaves by the front only.
