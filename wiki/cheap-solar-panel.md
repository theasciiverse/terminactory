# Cheap solar panel

**Cheap solar panel** is the first source of EC: a small panel that makes steady power without any fuel.

It gives a modest, constant supply to every electric part within its reach. A panel does not link to another panel directly, so poles are needed to join them together. Its reach is short, so a grid of panels usually wants a few poles to bridge the gaps. Because it burns nothing, it is the calmest way to light up a first factory. The basic solar panel is the better-reaching version of it.

## Tips
- Put a pole between panels when they are spread out, since panels do not link on their own.
- Panels need sunlight, so they cannot go on an underground floor. An elevator carries power down to the floor below.
- When a machine runs slowly, check whether its grid is short of EC, since a short grid slows everything on it.

## Placement
- Square, with no front. It sends its power out in every direction within its reach.
