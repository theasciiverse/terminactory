# Iron sheet

**Iron sheet** is rolled from [[iron-plate]] in a rolling mill, or pressed from plate in a press. It goes into relays, transformers, control boards, keyboards, tube boards and transistors.

The rolling mill is quicker for iron sheet than the press. The press works early, before a mill is available.

## Tips
- Use a rolling mill for iron sheet when you can; it is quicker.
- Iron sheet is used by a lot of electronics parts, so it is worth a steady line.
- The press version wastes more plate, so it is a stopgap.
