# Doped wafer

**Doped wafer** is a wafer that has been treated with dopant in a kiln.

A [[kiln]] makes a doped wafer from a [[wafer]] and [[dopant|dopant]]. Doped wafers are the starting point for the [[transistor]], the [[die]] and the [[fine-die|fine die]], so they sit in the middle of the silicon branch.

## Tips
- Doped wafers feed several different lines. Keep an eye on the wafer supply if one of them starts to stall.
- A kiln can fire a lot at once, so a steady input belt helps it stay busy.
