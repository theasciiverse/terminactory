# Large advanced drill

**Large advanced drill** is the biggest drill, running on EC. It mines a fast belt's worth of ore with no coal to feed.

Like the large basic drill, it is a large square with its output spread along its front edge. Because it runs on power, its only need is a grid that can carry it. It is unlocked by its research, once control boards have been made.

## Tips
- Power is its only need, so a steam engine or a row of solar panels is what keeps it running.
- Put a fast belt along its front edge to carry all of its output.
- It is best on the richest floors, where the ore comes in fastest.

## Placement
- Large square, with a front that turns to face any way. Ore leaves along the whole front edge.
