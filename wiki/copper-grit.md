# Copper grit

**Copper grit** is made by crushing [[copper-ore]] in a crusher. Furnaces melt grit into molten copper, and it melts faster than the ore does.

Grit gives more metal per ore than the ore itself, so a crusher is a good addition to any copper line.

## Tips
- Crushing takes time, so put a crusher on the ore belt before the furnaces.
- Grit melts quickly, which helps when a furnace is the bottleneck.
- Grit and ore can share the same molten copper line.
