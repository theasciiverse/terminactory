# Oil extractor

**Oil extractor** draws [[crude-oil|crude oil]] out of the ground and pushes it into a pipe at its front. It runs on EC and needs no fuel.

It works on any open ground, though not on an upper floor. It gives one unit of crude oil at a steady pace, and stops when the pipe in front of it is full. Deeper floors are richer, just as they are for drills, so an extractor underground gives its oil faster. It is unlocked by the oil extractor research, after copper pipe has been made; a [[water-pump|water pump]] is the cheaper way to learn how pipes work.

## Tips
- Point its arrow at the pipe run, since the oil only leaves by the front.
- One extractor fills a pipe slowly; a [[still]] takes the crude oil from the other end.
- Put it underground if you can, where the ground gives more.

## Placement
- Tall and narrow, and turning it lies it down. Crude oil leaves by the front only.
