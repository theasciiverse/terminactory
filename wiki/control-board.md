# Control board

**Control board** is a circuit made from a relay, fine wire and an iron sheet.

The [[advanced-assembler|advanced assembler]] builds a control board from a [[relay]], [[fine-wire|fine wire]] and an [[iron-sheet|iron sheet]]. A crude assembler can make one by hand as well, but it is slow and the advanced machines hide that recipe. Control boards go into the [[tube-board]], the [[generator]] and the [[control-panel]].

## Tips
- If your control boards are slow, check the relays and the fine wire before you add a machine.
- The precision assembler also builds control boards, and it does so faster than the advanced assembler.
