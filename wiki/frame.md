# Frame

**Frame** is a structural part made from steel girders and screws.

The [[welder]] makes a frame from [[steel-girder|steel girders]] and [[screw|screws]]. Frames are used by the [[engine-block]], the [[generator]], the [[steam-tractor]] and the [[computer]], so they are a common stop on the way to the largest goods in the game.

## Tips
- Screws are the bulk of a frame. If the frame line is slow, look at the screws before the girders.
- The steam tractor and the computer both need frames, so a steady frame line pays off across several branches at once.
