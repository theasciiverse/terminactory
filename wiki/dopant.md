# Dopant

**Dopant** is made in a kiln from stone, with coal as the kiln's fuel.

A [[kiln]] fires [[stone]] into dopant, burning coal to do it. Dopant goes into the [[doped-wafer|doped wafer]], where it is mixed with a [[wafer]] to prepare it for etching.

## Tips
- Dopant is made in batches, so give the kiln a steady stream of stone and coal.
- The kiln makes brick and dopant from the same stone, so the stone line is worth a look when either is slow.
