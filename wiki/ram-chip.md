# RAM chip

**RAM chip** is a memory chip made from a die, ceramic and fine wire.

The [[precision-assembler|precision assembler]] builds a RAM chip from a [[die]], [[ceramic]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. RAM chips are used in the [[mainboard]], where several are needed at once.

## Tips
- RAM chips are a steady part of the mainboard, so keep their line flowing.
- A precision assembler can make RAM and microprocessors side by side. A shared die line feeds both nicely.
