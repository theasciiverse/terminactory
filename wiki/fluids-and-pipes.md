# Fluids and pipes

**Fluids and pipes** cover water, steam and molten metal, which travel through pipes instead of on belts.

A [[water-pump]] draws water from the ground, and [[pipe|pipes]] carry it wherever you need. A [[boiler]] takes water from any side but its front and turns it into steam, which a [[steam-engine|steam engine]] uses for power. A [[mixer]] takes water from a pipe to make concrete, and an [[evacuator]] uses a water jet to pull air out of glass. Molten metal can run through pipes too, from furnaces to the machines that use it, or straight from a furnace into a molder that touches its front.

## Tips
- Put a pump down anywhere. Water goes wherever your pipes go, so run the pipe first.
- A pipe run holds one fluid at a time, so keep water and molten metal on separate runs.
- Check where each pipe ends before you add a machine to it.
