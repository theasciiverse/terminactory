# Computer

**Computer** is the crown of the chip branch, a large machine built from a mainboard, a disk drive, a terminal, a transformer and a frame.

The [[large-precision-assembler|large precision assembler]] builds a computer from a [[mainboard]], a [[disk-drive]], a [[terminal]], a [[transformer]] and a [[frame]]. It is the crowning item of the chip branch, worth a lot to sell and to research, so it is a fine place to aim.

Every part of the chip branch ends up here, and the view from the end is lovely.

## Tips
- Take your time. A computer is a long chain, so build it one part at a time and let each line settle before you start the next.
- The large precision assembler is the only machine that builds a computer, so give it room and a steady supply of parts.
- Keep a record of what each part needs. It is a pleasure to see the whole thing come together.
