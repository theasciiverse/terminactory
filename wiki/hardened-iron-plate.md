# Hardened iron plate

**Hardened iron plate** is made in a blast furnace from [[iron-plate]] and [[coke]], or from plate and coal. It goes into pistons and hardened gears.

The coke version leaves no waste. The coal version leaves [[waste]] out the back.

## Tips
- Use coke for hardened plate when you can, since it leaves no waste.
- The coal version needs a waste belt, so plan the back of the furnace.
- Hardened plate is the start of the hardened gear line.
