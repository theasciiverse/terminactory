# Welder

**Welder** joins steel into boiler shells, silo shells and frames. It draws EC only while it is working, so it is quiet when idle.

Steel parts go in from any side, and the welded part leaves by the front. A frame needs steel girders and screws, while a silo shell needs steel sheets and a valve. Boiler shells and frames are among the big goods that the large assemblers use. It is unlocked by its research, once steel girders have been made.

## Tips
- It draws power only while working, so a grid sized for its quiet moments slows down when it starts. Size the grid for its busy ones.
- Keep the steel girder line close to the welder, since frames and shells both need girders or sheets.
- Boiler shells go into engine blocks, so the welder is part of the road to the steam tractor.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
