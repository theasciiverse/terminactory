# Glass

**Glass** is fired from [[sand]] in a kiln. Glass goes into [[lamp]]s, [[gauge]]s, masks and fine masks.

The kiln burns coal while it fires glass, and it works in batches like it does for brick.

## Tips
- Feed the kiln with sand on a dedicated belt.
- Lamps need glass and fine wire together, so the lines should meet near the lamp maker.
- Masks and gauges also use glass, so the kiln is a shared resource.
