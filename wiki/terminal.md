# Terminal

**Terminal** is a finished console unit, made from a lamp matrix, a keyboard and a tube board.

The [[basic-assembler|basic assembler]] joins a [[lamp-matrix|lamp matrix]], a [[keyboard]] and a [[tube-board|tube board]] into a terminal. The terminal is one of the parts the [[computer]] needs.

## Tips
- A terminal is a long chain of parts. Build each part on its own line and bring them together at the end.
- Keyboards and lamp matrices both feed the terminal, so both lines are worth watching.
