# Basic assembler

**Basic assembler** is a faster assembler that builds electric motors, pistons, terminals and disk drives, and runs the crude parts faster than the crude assembler does.

It takes its inputs from any side, and parts leave by the front. Electric motors are the big reason to build one: they open the advanced drill and the large machines, and they go into the disk drive. It also builds pistons, terminals and disk drives. It runs on EC. It is unlocked by its research, which needs a gearbox made first.

## Tips
- Move the crude parts here as soon as you can. They come out much quicker.
- Electric motors need copper coil, steel rod and bearings, so plan those lines before you place it.
- Motors open the advanced drill and the large basic assembler, so this machine is a turning point.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
