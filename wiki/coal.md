# Coal

**Coal** is a black fuel mined from the ground. It keeps furnaces, kilns and boilers running, and it is also an ingredient in several recipes.

Furnaces and kilns burn coal while they work, and boilers burn it to make steam. Coal is baked into [[coke]], and it is used in etchant and in some slower steel and silicon recipes.

## Tips
- Keep a coal belt running to every fire; a machine with no fuel simply stops.
- Coke burns for longer than coal, so it makes the better boiler fuel per piece.
- [[waste]] can also be burned in a boiler, which helps when coal runs short.
