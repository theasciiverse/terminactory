# Stone

**Stone** is a mined rock. Crushers grind it into [[sand]], and kilns bake it into [[brick]].

Stone is also used in [[concrete]] and in silicon's dopant. It is a common, plain input, which makes it a good first thing to mine.

## Tips
- Stone feeds several lines at once, so give it a belt of its own.
- Crush stone into sand before a glass or silicon line needs it.
