# Blast furnace

**Blast furnace** melts iron fast, makes molten steel and hardened plate, and melts sand into molten silicon. It burns coke, and most of its recipes leave waste out the back.

Its molten output leaves by the front as fluid, into a pipe or straight into a molder. Steel is the big reason to build one: cast the molten steel in a molder, and the steel goes on to girders, sheets and rods. The steel recipes that use coke are the cleanest, and the coal versions leave more waste behind. Waste has to go somewhere, so plan a crate or a trade post behind it before you start.

## Tips
- Use coke rather than coal where you can. The coke recipes leave less waste.
- Keep the back of the blast furnace clear, or the waste stops it.
- Point an iron blast furnace straight into a steel one to pass molten iron along with no pipe between them.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front and waste out the back.
