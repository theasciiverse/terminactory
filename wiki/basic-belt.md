# Basic belt

**Basic belt** is the plain conveyor that carries items from one machine to the next. It is the first piece of logistics you will lay down.

Belts take items in from the back and the sides and send them out of the front. Anything in front of a belt that takes items, such as another belt or a machine, receives them. Belts are cheap. Each belt moves its items at a steady pace, and a faster belt is available later on.

## Tips
- Lay belts so each machine's output leaves by the front and runs into the next machine's input side.
- A belt that ends on nothing just stops, so the items wait at the end.
- Sorting and splitting are handled by the splitters and filters, not by the belt itself.

## Placement
- A single tile, turned to face the way the items should travel.
