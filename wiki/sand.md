# Sand

**Sand** is crushed from [[stone]]. It goes into [[glass]], [[concrete]], [[ceramic]] and etchant, and it is an input to molten silicon.

Sand is made in a crusher, so a stone line and a crusher line usually come together.

## Tips
- Sand is needed in many places, so give it a dedicated belt.
- Glass in a kiln takes a lot of sand, so feed the kiln first.
- Molten silicon takes sand, and its waste leaves out the back.
