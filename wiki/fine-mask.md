# Fine mask

**Fine mask** is a finer pattern sheet made from a mask, glass and fine wire.

The [[precision-assembler|precision assembler]] builds a fine mask from a [[mask]], [[glass]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. Fine masks go into the [[fine-die|fine die]] recipe, which is the cleaner way to cut chips.

## Tips
- A fine mask needs a mask first, so the mask line has to be running before the fine mask line can work.
- Glass and fine wire are both needed here. Check both before you assume the masks are stuck.
