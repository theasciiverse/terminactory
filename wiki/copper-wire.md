# Copper wire

**Copper wire** is drawn from [[copper-plate]] in a wire drawer, or pressed from plate in a press. It is the start of coils and fine wire.

A winder or press turns wire into [[copper-coil]]s, and a wire drawer turns it into [[fine-wire]].

## Tips
- Use a wire drawer for wire when you need a lot of it; it makes more per plate than a press.
- Keep wire on its own belt so coil makers are not waiting on other parts.
- Fine wire takes wire as its input, so a drawer can chain straight into a second drawer.
