# Advanced drill

**Advanced drill** is a large drill that runs on EC instead of burning coal. It mines ore much faster than a basic drill, and pushes it out of its front.

It needs ground beneath it, like the other drills. Because it runs on power, there is no coal to supply, and it is a good fit for a grid that already has steam or solar behind it. It is unlocked by its research, once electric motors have been made.

## Tips
- Power is its only need, so place it where a pole already reaches.
- It mines much faster than a basic drill, so one can fill a belt that a basic drill cannot.
- Deeper floors give richer ore, so it is worth placing one underground once you can dig.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Ore leaves by the front only.
