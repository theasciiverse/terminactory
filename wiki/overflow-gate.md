# Overflow gate

**Overflow gate** is a splitter that keeps everything going straight ahead until the front is full. Only then does it send items out its two sides. It takes items in from its neighbours, passes them out the front first, and turns to the left or right only for what the front cannot take.

Use it to make a line that fills one machine first and spills the rest onward: put the belt to the machine in front, and the belt for the overflow to a side. When the machine backs up, the gate shares what is left between its sides. It needs a little EC to work, and it turns to face any way the way a filter splitter does. It comes free with the filter splitter, once enough copper pipe has been made, or by research.

## Tips
- Put the belt you always want fed in front, and the spare belts on the sides.
- With nothing taking the overflow, the gate simply backs up, like any belt.
- Pair it with a trade post on the side to sell only what your machines could not use.

## Placement
- A single tile with a front that turns to face any way. Items go ahead first, then to the left and right, only onto belts that do not point back into it.
