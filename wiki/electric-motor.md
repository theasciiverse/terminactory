# Electric motor

**Electric motor** is a motor made from copper coils, a steel rod and bearings.

The [[basic-assembler|basic assembler]] builds an electric motor from [[copper-coil|copper coil]], a [[steel-rod|steel rod]] and [[bearing|bearings]]. Motors are needed by the [[disk-drive]], the [[pump-unit]], the [[generator]] and the [[drilling-rig]].

## Tips
- The steel rod and the bearings both come from steel, so the steel line feeds the motor line.
- Motors are needed by several different machines, so keep their output on a belt that reaches all of them.
