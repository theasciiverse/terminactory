# Mask

**Mask** is a pattern sheet made from glass, a copper sheet and fine wire.

The [[advanced-assembler|advanced assembler]] builds a mask from [[glass]], a [[copper-sheet|copper sheet]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. Masks go into the [[die]] recipe and the [[fine-mask|fine mask]] recipe.

## Tips
- Masks are needed for every die from the etcher, so a steady mask line is worth the space.
- Glass and fine wire are the two inputs to watch. If either lags, the masks slow down with them.
