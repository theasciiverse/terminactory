# Research

**Research** is the points, RP, you earn to unlock new parts. It is the quick way in, and the other way is to make the key items.

Each unlock costs RP and sits in one of the [[companies]]. Costs rise the further you go, from the copper and iron age to the processor machines at the top. RP comes from selling items. Each item pays less as you sell more of it, so you cannot farm one thing forever. [[waste]] earns nothing. A [[trade-post|trade post]] can sell for money, for research, or for a mix of both.

A company opens when you make its key item, and only then can you research its parts. The free unlock is separate: make enough of a part's milestone item and the part unlocks without spending RP, though its company must be open first.

## Tips
- Check the company's key before you plan a research. Without the key made, its parts cannot be researched at all.
- Sell extra stock for research when your money is already healthy.
- Press `t` to browse the research tree before you plan a new line.
