# Basic splitter

**Basic splitter** shares one stream of items between the belts beside it. It takes items in from its neighbours and passes them out to every neighbouring belt that leads away from it.

It hands items out in turn, so each belt that leads away gets a fair share. It does not turn to face a direction the way belts and machines do, and it needs a little EC to work. It is a good way to feed a pair of furnaces from one drill, or to split coal between a boiler and a furnace. Its cousin, the filter splitter, sends one chosen item out the front and everything else out the sides.

## Tips
- Only belts that lead away from the splitter get items. A belt pointing back into it does not.
- Use it to share one drill's ore between two machines that need the same thing.
- If one side backs up, the splitter sends the items down the other sides instead.

## Placement
- A single tile, with no front. It sends items out to every side that has a belt leading away.
