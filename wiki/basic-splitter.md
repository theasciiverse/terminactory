# Basic splitter

**Basic splitter** shares one stream of items between the belts beside it. It takes items in from its neighbours and passes them out to every neighbouring belt that does not point back into it.

It hands items out in turn, so each belt that leads away gets a fair share. It does not turn to face a direction the way belts and machines do, and it needs a little EC to work. It is a good way to feed a pair of furnaces from one drill, or to split coal between a boiler and a furnace. Its cousins are the filter splitter, which sends one chosen item out the front and everything else out the sides, and the overflow gate, which sends everything ahead until the front is full.

## Tips
- A belt that points back into the splitter feeds it and gets nothing back. Any other neighbouring belt can be fed.
- Use it to share one drill's ore between two machines that need the same thing.
- If one side backs up, the splitter sends the items down the other sides instead.

## Placement
- A single tile, with no front. It sends items out to every side that has a belt that does not point back into it.
