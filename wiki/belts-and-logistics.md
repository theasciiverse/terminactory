# Belts and logistics

**Belts and logistics** are the parts that move things from one machine to the next.

A [[basic-belt|basic belt]] carries items in a line, and a [[fast-belt|fast belt]] carries much more. A [[filter-splitter|filter splitter]] sends one chosen item out its front and the rest out its sides. A [[filtering-arm|filtering arm]] picks items off a belt, and a [[long-arm|long arm]] reaches further. A [[belt-tunnel|belt tunnel]] dives under other parts so a belt can cross a busy spot.

## Tips
- Keep belts in straight runs where you can, so a line is easy to follow. Belts need a little EC, and a belt without it shows red.
- Use a filter splitter to pull one item off a mixed belt before it jams the rest.
- Fast belts are worth it on a line that is already full.
