# Warehouse

**Warehouse** is a large crate. It holds far more items than a crate does, so it can soak up a long stretch of surplus.

Items go in from any side and leave by the front when something takes them. Like a crate, it is a buffer, so a warehouse helps when one stage of a line runs slower than the one before it. Items inside are lost if you remove it, so empty it first. It comes free after a large pile of coal has been made, or by research.

## Tips
- Put a warehouse where a fast line feeds a slow one, to smooth out the difference.
- Empty it before you move it, since the contents are lost when it is removed.
- A warehouse full of waste is still a full warehouse, so keep waste moving.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Items go in from any side and leave by the front.
