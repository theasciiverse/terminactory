# Iron ingot

**Iron ingot** is cast by a molder from molten iron. Ingots are pressed into [[iron-plate]].

The molten iron comes through a pipe, or straight from a furnace or blast furnace. Iron melts more slowly in a furnace than in a blast furnace.

## Tips
- A blast furnace feeds molten iron faster than a furnace does, so it suits iron ingot lines.
- Iron ingots are the only route into iron plate, so keep the molders fed.
- Iron grit melts into molten iron too, and is often the better input.
