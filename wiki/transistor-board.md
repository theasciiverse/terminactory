# Transistor board

**Transistor board** is a board of transistors, built with a circuit board and fine wire.

The [[advanced-assembler|advanced assembler]] builds a transistor board from [[transistor|transistors]], a [[circuit-board|circuit board]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. Transistor boards go into the [[disk-drive]], which also needs an electric motor and a steel sheet.

## Tips
- Transistor boards need a lot of transistors. Check that the transistor line is steady before you add more assemblers.
- The transistor board goes straight into the disk drive, so a short path between them keeps both moving.
