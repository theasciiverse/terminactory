# Tube board

**Tube board** is a board of tubes, built with a control board and an iron sheet.

The [[advanced-assembler|advanced assembler]] builds a tube board from [[tube|tubes]], a [[control-board|control board]] and an [[iron-sheet|iron sheet]]. A tube board is one of the parts that goes into the [[terminal]].

## Tips
- Tube boards take longer to build than most advanced parts, so expect the line to be slow.
- The tube line and the control board line usually run side by side. Keep them close to make the join easy.
