# Boiler

**Boiler** turns water and coal into steam. The steam leaves by the front, to feed a steam engine or a pipe.

It takes water from pipes on any side but the front, and it burns coal as fuel. It refuses [[waste]]. The boiler burns only as much as the grid needs, so it saves its coal when the power is spare. It is unlocked by the steam research, after copper coil.

## Tips
- Keep the front clear for the steam, since water can come in from any other side.
- The fire only burns while steam is wanted, so an idle grid costs nothing to run.
- Connect the front to a steam engine directly, or to a pipe that runs to one.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Steam leaves by the front.
