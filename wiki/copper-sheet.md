# Copper sheet

**Copper sheet** is rolled from [[copper-plate]] in a rolling mill, or pressed from plate in a press. It goes into gauges, lamp matrices, masks, circuit boards and tubes.

The rolling mill is the proper way to make sheet. The press makes it too, without any research, but it uses more plate.

## Tips
- Press sheet early, before the rolling mill is ready.
- A rolling mill wastes less plate than a press.
- Copper sheet is needed by many electronics parts, so keep a supply up.
