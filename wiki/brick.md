# Brick

**Brick** is baked from [[stone]] in a kiln. Bricks go into [[boiler-shell]]s and [[ceramic]].

The kiln fires batches of eight at once and burns coal to do it, so it works best with a steady coal belt.

## Tips
- Keep coal running to the kiln, or it stops.
- Boiler shells need a lot of brick, so plan a brick line before the boilers.
