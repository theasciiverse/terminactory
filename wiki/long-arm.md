# Long arm

**Long arm** is a mechanical arm with a longer reach than the filtering arm. It grabs items from a little way behind it and drops them a little way ahead, over whatever sits between.

It can jump over a belt, a machine, or a wall of parts that would block a shorter arm. It works the same way as the filtering arm otherwise, and it takes more power to run. It is unlocked by the long arm research.

## Tips
- Use it to hop a belt over a machine without rebuilding the line.
- Keep the source and the drop point in a straight line, since the arm reaches only along that line.
- The long arm takes more power than the short one, so check the grid before you add a row of them.

## Placement
- A single tile, with a front that turns to face any way. It takes from far behind and drops far ahead.
