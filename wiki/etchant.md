# Etchant

**Etchant** is mixed from coal, sand and water.

The [[mixer]] takes water from a pipe and mixes coal and sand into etchant. It makes a lot of etchant with a little [[waste]] out the back. Etchant is used in the [[die]] and the [[fine-die|fine die]] recipes.

## Tips
- The water arrives by pipe, so the pipe matters as much as the coal and sand.
- Most of the mixer's output is etchant, so keep the waste line clear to avoid a blocked back end.
