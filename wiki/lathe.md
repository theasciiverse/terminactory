# Lathe

**Lathe** turns plate into rods and rods into screws, and steel into steel rods, which it turns into bearings. It also slices silicon ingots into wafers, slowly, with the scrap left over to remelt.

Its inputs come in from any side, and the finished parts leave by the front. Iron rods and screws are among its first jobs, and the screws feed the frames and pistons further on. The slow wafer cut is the lathe's longest job, and the scrap it leaves sells and remelts. It runs on EC, and it is unlocked by its research once an iron gear has been made.

## Tips
- Rods feed the winder for springs, so keep the two close together.
- Bearings and steel rods need steel, so keep the steel line close by.
- Wafer slicing is slow. Use a wafer saw when you need wafers quickly.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
