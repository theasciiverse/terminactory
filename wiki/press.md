# Press

**Press** squeezes ingots into plates, and turns plates and wire into wire, pipe, coils and gears. It also presses steel into girders. It runs on EC and is one of the first machines you will build.

Items go in from any side, and the press pushes its finished parts out of the front. Most of its recipes need nothing but plate or ingot, so it is the workhorse of the copper and iron stage. The steel girder and hardened gear need the blast furnace research first. Copper sheets and iron sheets can also be pressed here, though the rolling mill makes them from less plate.

## Tips
- A press sitting straight in front of a molder can take ingots without a belt between them.
- Pick the recipe you need with the set-up key. Middle-clicking copies a recipe to the next press you place.
- Wire needs a steady plate supply, so keep a belt of plate running into the press.

## Placement
- Rectangular, longer one way, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
