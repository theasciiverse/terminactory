# Mainboard

**Mainboard** is the main circuit board of a computer, made from a circuit board, a fine microprocessor and RAM chips.

The [[precision-assembler|precision assembler]] builds a mainboard from a [[circuit-board|circuit board]], a [[microprocessor-x4x2|fine microprocessor]] and [[ram-chip|RAM chips]]. A coarser recipe uses the [[microprocessor-x4x1|coarse microprocessor]] instead, and takes longer. Mainboards go into the [[computer]].

## Tips
- The fine recipe is the quicker one, so use it when the fine microprocessors are running well.
- Mainboards use several RAM chips each, so keep that line busy or the mainboards will wait.
