# Control panel

**Control panel** is a large machine part made from control boards, lamps, gauges and a steel sheet.

The [[large-advanced-assembler|large advanced assembler]] builds a control panel from [[control-board|control boards]], [[lamp|lamps]], [[gauge|gauges]] and a [[steel-sheet|steel sheet]]. Control panels go into the [[steam-tractor]], so they are one of its big parts.

## Tips
- Control panels need a lot of lamps and gauges. Lay out those lines so they meet at the assembler.
- A control panel takes time to build. A short wait at the assembler is normal and not a sign that anything is broken.
