# Valve

**Valve** is built by a crude assembler from [[copper-pipe]] and an [[iron-gear]]. Valves go into pump units and silo shells.

The crude assembler is the machine that makes valves early in the game, so it is often the first assembler.

## Tips
- Valves are a simple first assembler job, so they are a good early build.
- Pump units take valves, so the valve line should keep up with pump production.
- Silo shells also take valves, so one valve line can serve two goods.
