# Pipe

**Pipe** carries fluid from one place to another. A joined run of pipes is one pool, and it holds one fluid at a time.

Pumps and machines that sit beside a pipe join it automatically. Fluid does not flow in a direction or build pressure, so a pipe just shares what it holds with everything connected to it. A pipe network can carry water, molten metal, or steam, but never two fluids at once. Keep the runs short where you can, because a long pipe is easy to lose track of.

## Tips
- Keep one fluid to each run of pipe, or a second fluid will not get in.
- Put a pump at the start of a run, then machines along the sides that need the water.
- A furnace can pour molten metal straight into a molder in front of it, with no pipe between them.

## Placement
- A single tile, with no front. It joins any pipe, pump or machine beside it.
