# Silicon crystal

**Silicon crystal** is a grown crystal of silicon, made slowly from silicon ingots.

The [[crystal-grower|crystal grower]] grows a silicon crystal from [[silicon-ingot|silicon ingots]]. It is the slowest machine in the game, so the crystal is a patient part of the silicon branch. Crystals go into the [[wafer-saw|wafer saw]], which cuts them into [[wafer|wafers]].

## Tips
- The crystal grower is slow, so give it a steady ingot supply and let it work in the background.
- Crystals go straight to the wafer saw, so a short belt between them is enough.
