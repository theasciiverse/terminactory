# Ethylene

**Ethylene** is the lightest fluid the [[still]] makes, from [[naphtha]]. It is the most valuable fluid that flows, and for now it is made to be sold: the machines that turn it into plastic arrive later.

Ethylene rides in pipes like any other fluid. Its price is high, so a short line from a still to a [[liquid-trader|liquid trader]] pays well, and the research it brings is worth the trouble too.

## Tips
- Run it straight from the still into a trader, with no long pipe in between.
- Watch the still's waste: the back needs a belt or a crate, or the line stops.
- Do not burn it; no machine takes it as fuel.
