# Reinforced concrete

**Reinforced concrete** is mixed from [[concrete]] and an [[iron-rod]] in a mixer, with water from a pipe. It goes into drilling rigs.

The mixer takes both the concrete and the rod from belts, along with the water.

## Tips
- Run a water pipe to the mixer, as the concrete and reinforced mixes both need it.
- Drilling rigs take a lot of reinforced concrete, so plan the line before the rig.
- Keep the iron rod supply close to the mixer.
