# Large basic drill

**Large basic drill** is a very big drill that mines a whole belt's worth of ore. It burns a great deal of coal to do it, and it pushes its ore out of its front.

It needs ground beneath it, and it takes up a large square. Its output is spread along its front edge, so a single large drill can fill a belt on its own. Because it burns so much coal, plan its coal supply first. It is unlocked by its research, once electric motors have been made.

## Tips
- Put a belt along its front edge, since it pushes ore out across the whole face.
- Its coal use is heavy, so give it a coal belt before you start it.
- It suits a rich deep floor, where the ore comes faster still.

## Placement
- Large square, with a front that turns to face any way. Ore leaves along the whole front edge.
