# Arc furnace

**Arc furnace** melts silicon on EC alone, with no fuel. It makes more silicon from the same sand and coke than the blast furnace does, and it leaves less waste behind.

It also remelts silicon scrap cleanly, with no waste at all. Molten silicon leaves by the front into a pipe, and waste leaves out the back. It is the cleaner way to make silicon once you can power it. It is unlocked by its research, once silicon ingots have been made, and it belongs to Halverson Smelting.

## Tips
- Use the remelt recipe to turn etcher and lathe scrap back into silicon.
- Its waste leaves out the back, so keep a crate or a trade post there.
- It runs on power alone, so no coal line is needed for the fire.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Molten silicon leaves by the front and waste out the back.
