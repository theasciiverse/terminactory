# Still

**Still** splits [[crude-oil|crude oil]] and [[naphtha]] into lighter fluids. It takes its fluid from pipes and coal from a belt, and needs no EC and no fire of its own.

It gives one fluid out the front and [[waste]] out the back, like the other dirty machines. Its recipes are naphtha from crude oil, ethylene from naphtha, and [[carbon-black|carbon black]] from coal and [[fuel-oil|fuel oil]]. Fuel oil has no maker yet, so the carbon black recipe waits for the refinery. It is unlocked by the still research, after crude oil has been pumped.

## Tips
- Point the front at a pipe or a machine that takes the fluid, and leave the back for waste.
- Feed coal in on a belt from behind or the side; the front is for the fluid.
- The carbon black recipe cannot run until fuel oil arrives.

## Placement
- Wide and deep, with a front that turns to face any way. Fluid leaves by the front, waste by the back.
