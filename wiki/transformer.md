# Transformer

**Transformer** is a coil-and-sheet part made by the crude assembler.

The [[crude-assembler|crude assembler]] makes a transformer from [[copper-coil|copper coils]] and [[iron-sheet|iron sheets]]. Transformers go into the [[computer]], where they are one of the parts that makes it all work.

## Tips
- Transformers are made only from coils and sheets, so their line is a simple one to start with.
- Keep the coil and sheet lines apart so one does not stall the other.
