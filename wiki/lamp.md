# Lamp

**Lamp** is a light made from glass and fine wire.

The [[crude-assembler|crude assembler]] makes a lamp from [[glass]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. Lamps go into the [[lamp-matrix]], the [[control-panel]] and the [[tube]] kiln recipe, so the same little light turns up in several places.

## Tips
- Lamps need both glass and fine wire, so check both lines when lamps run short.
- Keep the lamp line and the fine wire line close together, so the wire is not carried far.
