# Precision assembler

**Precision assembler** builds microprocessors (both the x4x1 and the x4x2), RAM chips, fine masks and mainboards. It makes control boards faster than the advanced assembler, and it runs the advanced goods faster.

It takes its inputs from any side, and its parts leave by the front. The microprocessors and RAM chips are the heart of a mainboard, and this is the machine that makes them. It does not make the crude or basic goods at all, so pair it with the lower assemblers. It runs on a lot of EC. It is unlocked by its research, once control boards have been made.

## Tips
- Feed it dies, ceramic and fine wire for the microprocessors, and keep the fine wire line busy.
- It needs a lot of power, so give it its own grid before you build it.
- It does nothing for the basic goods, so keep the basic assembler running alongside it.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
