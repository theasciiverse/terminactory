# Floors and elevators

**Floors and elevators** let your factory grow up and down, not just across.

Research opens a floor above the ground, and separate research opens a floor dug below it. Ore is richer underground, so the floor below is a good home for drills. Upper floors cannot hold drills or pumps. An [[elevator]] moves items between floors. It only travels when it is full, so it runs best when its loads arrive in steady batches. The same elevator serves each of its doors on every floor it links.

## Tips
- An elevator needs free room on every floor it serves, so plan its spot before you fill the floors around it.
- Drills below ground mine faster, and faster still the deeper you go.
- Keep an eye on the car. If it waits for a load, a slow feed upstream is usually to blame.
