# Elevator

**Elevator** carries items between floors. A car runs between the doors you place on each floor, loading at one, travelling, and unloading at another.

Once a second floor is open, you can place elevators to reach it. An elevator is built on every floor it serves, and it gets a free unloading door on each new floor when that floor opens. The car travels only when it is full, so a slow trickle of items will wait a while before it moves. Bigger elevators cost more, because they cover more tiles. They link the grids on the floors they touch.

## Tips
- Fill the car before you expect much to come up: it leaves only when full.
- Put a load door on the floor you are sending from and an unload door where the items should arrive.
- Each floor you open gives the elevator a new door for free, so you rarely have to rebuild one.

## Placement
- Square, with a door on every floor it serves.
