# Molder

**Molder** casts molten metal and silicon into ingots. Molten metal comes in through a pipe, or straight from a furnace that faces it, and ingots leave by the front.

It makes copper ingots, iron ingots, steel and silicon ingots, each from the matching molten fluid. It runs on EC. Ingots are the raw form that presses turn into plate, so most factories have a molder near the end of every smelting line. The molder has no research of its own: it is open from the start.

## Tips
- Put it right in front of a furnace, so the molten metal goes straight in with no pipe between.
- Give each metal its own molder line, so the ingots stay sorted.
- Copper and iron ingots need a press next, so put the press in front of the molder's output.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Ingots leave by the front, and molten metal can come in from a pipe on any side but the front.
