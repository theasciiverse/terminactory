# Mixer

**Mixer** takes water from a pipe and mixes concrete, then reinforced concrete, and etchant from coal and sand. It runs on EC and is one of the Wellbourne machines.

Solid inputs come in from any side, and water comes in from a pipe on any side but the front. The finished mix leaves by the front. Concrete needs sand and stone, and reinforced concrete adds an iron rod to concrete. The etchant recipe leaves some waste out the back. The mixer is unlocked by its research, after copper pipe has been made.

## Tips
- Run a pipe from a water pump to the mixer, and keep the sand and stone on belts.
- The etchant recipe is the one that feeds the etcher, so keep its waste moving.
- Concrete opens the Underhill company, so the mixer comes early.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Water comes in from a pipe on any side but the front, and products leave by the front, with waste out the back.
