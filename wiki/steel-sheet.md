# Steel sheet

**Steel sheet** is rolled from [[steel]] in a rolling mill. Steel sheets go into [[boiler-shell]]s, [[silo-shell]]s, disk drives and control panels.

The rolling mill is the only machine that makes steel sheet, so the mill is needed before any of these goods.

## Tips
- Steel sheet goes into several large goods, so keep the mill busy.
- Feed the mill from the blast furnace steel line.
