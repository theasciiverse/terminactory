# Transistor

**Transistor** is a switching part made from a doped wafer, fine wire and an iron sheet.

The [[advanced-assembler|advanced assembler]] makes transistors from a [[doped-wafer|doped wafer]], [[fine-wire|fine wire]] and an [[iron-sheet|iron sheet]]. One batch yields several transistors at once. They go into the [[transistor-board|transistor board]].

## Tips
- A batch of transistors is a good way to feed the transistor board in bulk.
- Keep the doped wafer line close to the advanced assembler. It is a short walk, and the wafers do not sit around.
