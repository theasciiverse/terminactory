# Rolling mill

**Rolling mill** rolls plate flat into sheets, and steel into steel sheets and girders. It is the cheaper route to sheets, and it makes girders from steel in quantity.

Plate or steel goes in from any side, and the sheets and girders leave by the front. It makes copper sheets and iron sheets, along with steel sheets and steel girders. Sheets feed the gauges, relays and circuit boards further on. It runs on EC, and it is unlocked by its research once a steel girder has been made.

## Tips
- Use it for sheets rather than the press, since it takes less plate per sheet.
- Steel girders from here are what the welder needs for frames.
- Keep the steel supply steady, since the girder and steel sheet recipes both draw on it.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
