# Tools and blueprints

**Tools and blueprints** are the helpers for working on big areas at once.

Press `c` to start the area tool, then mark a rectangle. From there `c` again copies the area to your hand so you can paste it anywhere. `x` removes whatever is marked. Once you have blueprints researched, you can save a copy in the library with `l`, and every save can use it. A blueprint copies the layout and settings, not what the machines hold. The upgrade planner, which needs its own research, swaps every belt, drill, panel and assembler in an area for its next version, and you pay only the difference. Press `z` to undo the last removal or upgrade.

## Tips
- Save a blueprint before you tear a line down, so the rebuild is a single paste.
- Check the preview line before you confirm an upgrade. It shows what it will cost.
- Undo with `z` is the quickest way back from a mistake.
