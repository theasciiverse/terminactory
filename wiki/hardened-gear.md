# Hardened gear

**Hardened gear** is pressed from [[hardened-iron-plate]] in a press, once the blast furnace research is done. It goes into steam tractors.

Hardened gears are a large-goods part.

## Tips
- Keep hardened plate supplied to the press.
- Steam tractors take several hardened gears, so the line needs a steady flow.
- The press needs the blast furnace research before it can make hardened gears.
