# Silicon scrap

**Silicon scrap** is the leftover silicon from etching and wafer slicing.

Silicon scrap is left over when a [[etcher|etcher]] cuts dies and when a [[lathe]] slices rough wafers. It is not waste: it sells, and an [[arc-furnace|arc furnace]] can remelt it into molten silicon with no waste.

## Tips
- Scrap is worth keeping. Remelting it in the arc furnace is cleaner than melting new sand.
- If scrap piles up at the etcher, check that its output belt has room to move.
