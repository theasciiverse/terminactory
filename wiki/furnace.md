# Furnace

**Furnace** melts ore into molten metal and bakes coal into coke. It burns coal as its fuel, and it has no need for power.

Ore or grit goes in from any side. Molten copper and molten iron leave by the front as fluid, into a pipe or straight into a molder. Coking needs research first, and coke is what the blast furnace feeds on. The furnace is the first machine that turns raw ore into something a factory can use.

## Tips
- Feed it a steady coal supply, or the fire goes out and the melt stops.
- Pipe its molten output to a molder, so the metal does not sit in the furnace.
- Iron melts slowly, and a furnace makes one recipe at a time, so give iron its own furnace rather than sharing one with copper.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Molten metal leaves by the front.
