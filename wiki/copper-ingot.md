# Copper ingot

**Copper ingot** is cast by a molder from molten copper. Ingots are pressed into [[copper-plate]].

The molten copper comes through a pipe, or straight from a furnace, so the molder can sit right beside the furnace.

## Tips
- Put the molder next to the furnace to skip a pipe run.
- Ingots are the only route into copper plate, so a press always needs a supply of them.
- Copper ore and grit both melt into the same molten copper, so either can feed the molders.
