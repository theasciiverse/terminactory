# Crude assembler

**Crude assembler** is the first machine that builds finished parts. It makes small goods such as valves, gauges, lamps, relays, gearboxes, transformers and keyboards, one at a time.

It runs without any EC. Its inputs come in from any side, and the parts leave by the front. It is slow, so it suits the early game, when every part is a step forward. It also has a slow hand-wired recipe for the control board, which later assemblers hide because they can make the proper one. Crude assembler research is unlocked after the gear is made.

## Tips
- Give it a spread of inputs, since most recipes need several different parts at once.
- Gearboxes from this machine are what open the fast belts and the basic assembler.
- It is slow for big jobs, so move on to the basic assembler once you can.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
