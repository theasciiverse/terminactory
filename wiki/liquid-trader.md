# Liquid trader

**Liquid trader** sells the fluid in the pipe network it touches. It is a [[trade-post|trade post]] for anything that flows.

It takes fluid out of the pipes beside it and sells it at that fluid's price, so a line can earn without a belt or a crate. It refuses water and steam, which cannot be sold, and an empty pipe gives it nothing to do. Like the trade post it has three modes: money, research, or a split of both, changed with the same key. The research it earns fades as more units are sold, just as an item's does. It is unlocked by the liquid trader research, after crude oil has been pumped.

## Tips
- Put it at the end of a line whose fluid has nowhere else to go.
- Sell the surplus of a line that has backed up, rather than letting the pipe fill.
- Research mode pays well at first; the fade is per unit, so a steady trickle lasts longer than a flood.

## Placement
- Square, with no front to turn: it sells from the pipes on any side.
