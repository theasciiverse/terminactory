# Large basic assembler

**Large basic assembler** is a big assembler for the bigger goods: pump units and engine blocks. It is much larger than the other assemblers, and it is slow, since each part takes a long time.

Inputs come in from any side, and the finished goods leave by the front. A pump unit needs a motor, valves, a silo shell and a gauge, so it sits at the end of a long line. The engine block needs a frame, pistons, a gearbox and a boiler shell. It runs on EC. It is unlocked by its research, once electric motors have been made.

## Tips
- Bring every input from its own belt, since a pump unit needs several different parts.
- It is slow, so one large basic assembler feeding a single machine is plenty early on.
- The big goods lead to the large advanced assembler, so keep the path clear.

## Placement
- Square, with a front that turns to face any way. Products leave by the front.
