# Coke

**Coke** is coal baked into a harder fuel, in a furnace after the coking research, or in a coke oven. It burns for longer than coal.

Coke is the preferred fuel for blast furnaces, and it is an ingredient in [[steel]], [[hardened-iron-plate]] and molten silicon. The coke oven cooks its own coal and leaves [[waste]] out the back.

## Tips
- Coke ovens need no fire of their own, which keeps the line simple.
- Coke in a blast furnace leaves less waste than coal does.
- Coke is a good boiler fuel, since each piece burns longer than coal.
