# Filter splitter

**Filter splitter** is a splitter that sends one chosen item out its front and everything else out its sides. It is the way to pull a single kind of item off a mixed belt.

Pick the item with the set-up key, and the splitter lets that one through to the front. Everything else leaves by the other sides, where the belts lead away. With no filter set, it behaves like a plain splitter and shares everything out. It comes free once enough copper pipe has been made, or by research.

## Tips
- Put the belt you want in front, and the rest of the line on the sides.
- With no filter set, it acts like the basic splitter, so set the filter before you rely on it.
- Pair it with a second filter splitter to sort a mixed line into several streams.

## Placement
- A single tile with a front that turns to face any way. The filtered item leaves ahead, the rest to the other sides, and only onto belts that lead away.
