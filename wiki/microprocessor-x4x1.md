# Microprocessor (x4x1)

**Microprocessor (x4x1)** is a chip made from a die, ceramic and fine wire.

The [[precision-assembler|precision assembler]] builds a microprocessor from a [[die]], [[ceramic]] and [[fine-wire|fine wire]]. It is the first of the microprocessors, and it goes into the [[mainboard]] through the slower coarse recipe. The finer [[microprocessor-x4x2|x4x2]] is a separate chip, built from a fine die.

## Tips
- Keep the die line steady, since the precision assembler is shared with the RAM chip recipe.
- The coarse mainboard recipe uses this chip. It is slower than the fine recipe, so it is a good place to start.
