# Generator

**Generator** is a large machine part made from a frame, electric motors, a gauge and a control board.

The [[large-advanced-assembler|large advanced assembler]] builds a generator from a [[frame]], [[electric-motor|electric motors]], a [[gauge]] and a [[control-board|control board]]. Generators are used by the [[steam-tractor]], and they are a good example of a part that asks for several parts from across the factory.

## Tips
- Generators draw from many lines at once. Keep the frame, motor, gauge and board lines reachable from one spot if you can.
- Only the large advanced assembler builds generators, so that machine is the one to plan around.
