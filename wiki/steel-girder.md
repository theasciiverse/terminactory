# Steel girder

**Steel girder** is pressed from [[steel]] in a press, or rolled from steel in a rolling mill. Girders go into frames and drilling rigs.

Pressing girders needs the blast furnace research. The rolling mill gives more girders from the same steel than the press does.

## Tips
- Use the rolling mill for girders when you have it; it wastes less steel.
- Girders make frames, so a frame line needs a steady supply.
