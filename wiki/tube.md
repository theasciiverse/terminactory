# Tube

**Tube** is a lamp with its air taken out, made from a lamp, a copper sheet and fine wire.

A tube is made by a [[kiln]] in the slow baked recipe, which leaves [[waste]] out the back, or by an [[evacuator]], which uses water from a pipe and leaves nothing behind. The evacuator is the clean way. Tubes go into the [[tube-board]], which is the next step up in the terminal branch.

## Tips
- The evacuator makes tubes with no waste and no fire, so it is the better choice once it is available.
- The baked kiln recipe leaves waste out the back. Give that waste somewhere to go before the kiln backs up.
