# Fine etcher

**Fine etcher** etches a fine die and a spare die from each wafer, with a fine mask. It leaves no scrap, but it is very hungry for power.

It takes a doped wafer, a fine mask and etchant, and the dies leave by the front. Fine dies go into the finer microprocessor, the x4x2. It is unlocked by its research, once a microprocessor has been made, and it belongs to Meridian Instruments.

## Tips
- Its power draw is heavy, so give it a grid with room to spare.
- It leaves a spare die with every fine die, so both outputs are worth collecting.
- Fine masks come from the precision assembler, so plan that line before you place this.

## Placement
- Tall rectangle, with a front that turns to face any way. Both dies leave by the front.
