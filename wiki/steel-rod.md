# Steel rod

**Steel rod** is turned from [[steel]] in a lathe. Steel rods go into [[electric-motor]]s, [[piston]]s and [[bearing]]s.

The lathe makes steel rods the same way it makes iron rods, but from steel instead of plate.

## Tips
- Steel rods are the start of the bearing line, so feed the lathe with steel.
- Pistons and motors both take steel rods, so the rod line serves two chains.
- Keep the lathe supplied with steel from the blast furnace.
