# Wafer saw

**Wafer saw** cuts a silicon crystal into wafers along a fine wire. It cuts several wafers from each crystal and leaves no scrap.

It runs on EC and takes a crystal and a fine wire for each cut. The wafers leave by the front. It is much quicker than the lathe at wafer slicing, and it wastes nothing, which makes it the better way to get wafers. It is unlocked by its research, once silicon ingots have been made, and it belongs to Halverson Smelting.

## Tips
- Feed it crystals from a crystal grower, and keep fine wire arriving too.
- Because it leaves no scrap, the wafers it makes all go on to the doping and etching steps.
- It is a long machine, so leave room for the grower and the wire to reach it.

## Placement
- Long rectangle, with a front that turns to face any way. Wafers leave by the front.
