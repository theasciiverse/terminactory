# Power

**Power** is the electric charge, EC, that keeps most of your machines running.

EC comes from [[cheap-solar-panel|cheap solar panels]], [[basic-solar-panel|basic solar panels]] and, later, from [[boiler|boilers]] and [[steam-engine|steam engines]]. [[ec-pole|EC poles]] carry it from a source to the machines around it. A boiler needs water from a pipe and coal, and it can burn [[waste]] as well, so a spare heap of waste can keep the lights on. Some machines draw EC only while they work, and a few, such as the coke oven and the crude assembler, need none at all.

## Tips
- Put a pole near every new machine. Panels that are not joined by poles stay on their own grid.
- Solar panels need sunlight, so they stay on the ground and upper floors. An elevator carries power to the floor below.
- Check the EC readout before you add a big machine. A short supply slows every part on that grid evenly.
