# Concrete

**Concrete** is mixed from [[sand]], [[stone]] and water in a mixer. It is the base of reinforced concrete.

The mixer takes water from a pipe and the sand and stone from belts. Concrete is also the material that reinforced concrete is made from, which goes into drilling rigs.

## Tips
- Run a water pipe to the mixer before anything else; no water, no concrete.
- Keep sand and stone on separate belts so one does not block the other.
